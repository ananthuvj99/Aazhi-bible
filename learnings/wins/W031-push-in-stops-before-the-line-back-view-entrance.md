Symptom: 1C-a v5 approved after v4.1 felt static: the camera pushed in on Rosamma cooking, Antony walked in past the camera, and her teasing line lip-synced.
Cause: the camera moved only before the line and was still for it; the second person was a back view walking along one side of the frame, never between her face and the lens; she turned toward him, which turned her face to about 15 degrees off the lens.
Rule (recipe): to give a one-line dialogue shot life without breaking lip-sync: (1) one start image; (2) a slow straight push-in (about 2 m in 4.5 s) that stops 1 s before the line; (3) the listener enters from behind the camera as a back view only, walking along one side of the frame, never crossing the speaker's face; (4) the speaker turns toward the listener so her face comes to about 15 degrees off the lens and her head is still for the line; (5) a "the room stays the same" block that says the door edges slide out as the camera moves in.
