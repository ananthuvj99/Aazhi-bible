Symptom: after nine aerial takes with frozen palms (1A v17–v25, L157), the locked-off 1A-wind v4 came out right: the house still, every coconut palm moving in the wind.
Cause: with no camera move the model spends all its motion on the scene; a clean, realistic start image fixed the light and layout, so only the leaves had to change.
Rule (recipe): for foliage that must move, make it its own shot: (1) a relit, realistic start image of the set (@houseexteriorc1platesunny, Nano Banana relight); (2) camera locked off, 8 s; (3) "the leaves are moving the whole time" as the first line; (4) a time beat for every 1–2 s with gusts; (5) three layered movements per palm (crown, fronds, leaflets) with sizes; (6) a motion-only reference video (@palmgreenwind, text-free); (7) CONSTRAINTS naming the frozen-leaf failure. Join it to a camera-move shot by using its first frame as the moving shot's end frame.
