**W23 — A seated talk in one 15 s generation: a short two-shot, then hard cuts to single close-ups, came out right** (3tree-a v2.4)

Recipe:
1. CUT 1 (about 1.5 s): the two of them together, locked off, so the audience sees where they sit.
2. Then one locked-off single close-up per line, hard cuts at exact times, only the speaker in frame (lip-sync stays on the right face). Up to six cuts in 15 s worked.
3. Every close-up has an EYELINE line: the listener "just off the right/left edge of the frame", the camera "right beside the listener's head", the eyes "straight into his/her eyes, about 15 degrees off the lens", never away, never into the lens (L117).
4. Delivery as feelings, not style words, plus the "real, conversational speech … not acted" voices line and "each reply starts right after the other finishes, no dead air" (L115).
5. One continuous ambience bed listed by layer (sea, wind, life around, distant class, close sounds), "unbroken across every cut", and no music.
