Symptom: 1B-3 v11 approved after v8.2–v10 failed: the room, window view and clock stayed the same, Antony sat up slowly, kept his hands on his knees, and the lip-sync worked.
Cause: each failure was fixed one at a time: one start image only (L176), a locked camera, a slow sit-up with hands clear of the mouth (L175), the platform duration set to 15 s, and his face turned straight to the camera for the line.
Rule (recipe): for a wake-and-speak shot in one room: (1) attach one start image of the room and nothing else of the same view; (2) lock the camera and add a "the room stays the same" block naming the window, view and clock; (3) slow, simple body action with a time for each beat; (4) hands rest below the chest from 1.5 s before the line; (5) the speaker faces the camera squarely for the line, eyes about 5 degrees beside the lens, never side-on, even when the line is called to someone off screen (the sound carries the direction); (6) duration and aspect set on the platform to match the prompt (15 s, 21:9).
