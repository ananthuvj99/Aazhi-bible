**W14 — 2-bath v3.1 approved: the mug pour worked once the mug had its own sheet** (2-bath v3.1)

What came out well: Antony, waist up in the red thorthu, pours the red mug over his head with his eyes closed, shakes his head as the water falls, a natural shiver; the actions and the sound effects all landed. Only the light face rub (4.6–6.2 s) did not appear — Ananthu takes that moment from an earlier take.

Recipe to repeat:
1. Costume change from the usual look → its own character look sheet (`@antonybath`), not text alone (L73).
2. The hand prop the action depends on → its own prop sheet (`@bathmug`), attached, named with the hand, full/empty, in the reference line, every ACTION beat and POSITIVE LOCKS, and "the only mug in the frame" (L79, L51).
3. Waist up only, so no bend to the bucket is ever needed; the shot opens with the mug already full in his hand.
4. Physical reaction written as visible, timed movements (eyes closed, head shake as the water falls, shoulders tense, sharp breath), with "natural, not exaggerated" in PERFORMANCE.
5. Sun from the land side (L11); timed sound effects with no music.

Not landed: a small hand action late in a busy shot (the face rub) can be skipped — give it its own beat of at least 2 s, or cover it in the edit.
