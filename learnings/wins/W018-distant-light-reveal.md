**W18 — A distant story light reads when it is tiny, on one side, and timed** (s23-sh01 v1 approved, SB028)

What came out well: first take. A single warm point of light appeared on the far-left horizon at about 1.5 s and Antony's turn and lean sold it.

Recipe:
1. Same camera and plate as the approved shot before (`@urudeckc2platenight`), start image = its last frame (L52).
2. The light is sized ("a tiny warm orange point, no bigger than a star"), placed ("on the horizon at the LEFT of the stem post"), timed ("at about 1.5 s") and locked ("nothing else of it is seen") in four places: SCENE, the place block, ACTION and POSITIVE LOCKS.
3. The light "lights nothing near": no warm spill on the deck or on Antony.
4. The W17 alive-body beats for the watcher (breathing, sway, weight shifts; the turn flows into the lean).

Note on sound: the platform laid a low, intriguing music cue under the moment Antony sees the light, even though the prompt carried the standard no-music audio block, and Ranjith liked it. The no-music rule is unchanged; whether a scene may ask for music is Ranjith's (or Ananthu's) call per scene.
