**W8 — Give the listener something to do, and cut back for their reaction**
1C-c v4.1 almost worked but Antony stood still. v5 was approved: start frame → hard cut to the speaker's face (alone in frame, natural delivery, no tag — W6) → hard cut back to the listener for a silent reaction (scratching his head with both hands, a laugh through the nose, lips closed, a half-turn toward where he goes next).
Use it for every two-person dialogue: the listener has a physical action in the shots around the line, never in the speaker's frame, mouth closed; the reaction shot sets up the next move.
