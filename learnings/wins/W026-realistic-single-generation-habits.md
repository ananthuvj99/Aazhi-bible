**W26: Five habits from a realistic single-generation video, added to our team format**

Ranjith shared a post where one Seedance 2.5 generation looked like real footage, with its prompt and workflow. What we took from it:

1. **A CONSTRAINTS block of known failures, stated plainly** ("the camera, crew or tripod is NEVER visible in the mirror", "one hand per job, no hand swaps", "both cuts are instant hard cuts: no dissolve, no fade, no zoom"). Ours lists our own failures from the lessons: one lantern (L155), no wheel on the open deck, no hand near the camera (L150), Raghavan's towel at his neck (L149), no moon in aft views (L142).
2. **Small capture imperfections, second by second**: when the person breathes, a slight drift of the camera, an exposure change, a focus breath, pauses. They make a take feel filmed, not generated.
3. **The lens in millimetres and what is sharp** in each beat ("85mm, shallow focus... only the reflection is sharp"; a punch-in is "a hard cut, not a zoom, tighter lens").
4. **No smoothing in the base image**: an over-smoothed start image gets worse when animated. Image QA checks real pores and texture on every face.
5. **Global look line**: "24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation, visible skin texture and pores, no smoothing".

Also noted, not yet a rule: **headless full-body panels** on character sheets (a tiny distant face on the sheet can make the video face smooth and washed out; Ranjith asked for headless versions of the crew sheets); and **one change at a time by video-to-video** once a take's motion is right (character, then a prop, then the location), if the platform takes a video input.
