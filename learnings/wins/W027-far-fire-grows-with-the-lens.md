**W27: A far fire that glows, reflects and grows in one take**

Ranjith: "The fires glow, the reflection of the fire also came really well. So that's the good work I want the prompt to do continuously."

The recipe (s23-sh02 v5):
1. **A "THE BURNING VALLAM (most important)" block**, written by the second: start exactly as the dot in the start image (about a fortieth of the frame); it grows only because the lens tightens and because the fire spreads; at a named second it "catches the nets and flares" along the whole hull; a thin plume starts, lit orange at its base, leaning right with the wind.
2. **Size in frame as a fraction, and a cap**: "about a twentieth of the frame wide... never bigger than a twentieth" (L156), plus "no huge smoke cloud, no blaze filling the sea" in CONSTRAINTS.
3. **The reflection named on its own**: "a narrow broken orange reflection streak on the swells running from the boat down toward the uru", breaking and re-forming on each swell (PHYSICS).
4. **Light that stays out there**: fire about 1800K, "the only warm light out on the sea, lighting nothing near... lighting the base of its own smoke and its reflection streak but nothing on the uru".
5. **An end keyframe at the true size** (@s23sh02nightend), scaled by hand when the image model drew it too big.
6. **Camera that makes it grow**: one smooth push of 1 m over the shoulder while the lens goes 35mm to 85mm; the focus passes from his head to the horizon.

Use this block in every later shot that shows the fire. Carry the size forward from each approved last frame and change it only by the lens and by real growth.
