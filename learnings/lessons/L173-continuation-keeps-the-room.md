**L173: the second half of a split scene lost the room and where things stood** (s24-sh09 v3 B)

Symptom: part B, made to continue part A, came out in a different-looking cabin with the lamp, jug and men placed differently.
Cause: part B's prompt dropped the room block to save length; "continue" alone does not hold the set.
Fix: when a scene is split, copy the room block into every part and write the layout from the approved frame of part A (who sits left and right, where the window, lamp and props are); save a frame from part A as a location sheet and attach it to part B. Never shorten the room block in a continuation.
