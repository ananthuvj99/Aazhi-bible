**L72 — Higgsfield's content filter rejected a harmless kitchen scene as NSFW**
1D-exit v2 (a son talking to his mother while she cooks) was refused as NSFW. v2.1 — same shot, only reworded — passed and was approved.
Words removed in v2.1 (the exact trigger is unknown; avoid them all): "barefoot", "bare feet / bare footsteps", "sleeveless", "grin/grinning", clothing that "swings", "brush-off", "waves (someone) off", "off-face", "held" (for a sound).
Rule: describe costume without skin words (a "white cotton vest", no "sleeveless"; leave feet out unless they matter); use "smile" rather than "grin"; describe clothing as worn, not moving. If a prompt is refused, reword these first, then test the start image.
