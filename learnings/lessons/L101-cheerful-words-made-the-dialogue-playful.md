**L101 — "Cheerful", "a smile in the voice" and "drawn out and lifting" made a mother-and-son exchange sound playful** (2B wide v3)

Symptom: Rosamma's worried question and Antony's reassurance came out light and sing-song, a bit playful; Ananthu: "the dialogue seems unnatural."

Cause: delivery words that ask for colour (cheerful, smile in the voice, a word drawn out and lifting at the end, stretched and warm) push the platform voice into performance; with two calls across a distance it turned into a singsong.

Fix: for everyday family talk write plain delivery — ordinary speaking voice, raised only as much as the distance needs, even pace, natural falling ends; say what it is not (no laugh, no teasing, no sing-song). Keep only the stress and one pause; no stretched or lifted words.
