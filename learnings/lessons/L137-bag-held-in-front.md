**L137: Antony held his bag in front of him with both hands instead of carrying it on his shoulder** (review of the generated cut, 2026-11-11)

Symptom: While walking, Antony holds the canvas bag in front of his body with both hands. It looks staged; he should carry it on his shoulder.

Cause: The prompt mentioned the bag and his hands without saying how it is carried while he walks, so the model put the bag in his hands.

Rule:
1. Walking: "the strap over his LEFT shoulder, the bag hanging at his left hip and swinging a little with his steps, his left hand resting on the strap, his right arm swinging free".
2. Lock: "the bag is never held in front of him, never in both hands, never across his body".
3. Attach @antonybag in every shot where he walks with it (card rule), with the shoulder written in POSITIVE LOCKS.
