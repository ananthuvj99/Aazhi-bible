**L86 — When a line names something, the audience must be able to see it** (s23-sh04 v1, approved but flagged)

Symptom: Antony shouts "ആശാനേ, അവിടെ നോക്ക്! ഒരാൾ വെള്ളത്തിൽ!" (someone's in the water), but nobody can be seen in the water in his shot: behind him there is only the small, far burning vallam. The take works, but the line feels a little dull and unsupported.

Cause: the hand was shown only in the cutaway before it (s23-sh03), and it had already gone under. The shot with the line placed the fire far off and small, with no person written near it.

Rule:
1. When a line or a reaction points at something (a person, a boat, a light), that thing is visible in the same shot or in the shot right after it, and readable at its size.
2. In this sequence (SB031 onward), every shot that looks toward the fire shows Selvan in the water beside the burning vallam: his head and one arm above the swell, lit orange by the fire, struggling. Give him a size and place ("a small dark head and one raised arm just LEFT of the burning hull") and a timing.
3. If the thing is too far to read, cut closer (long lens) or bring it nearer in the story; never leave the line pointing at nothing.
