**L74 — A line-up sheet attached with the featured men's own sheets cloned Babu, and an unasked young man (Antony's look) joined the dance** (s16b-sh02 v1) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh02-v1-babu-twice-extra-young-man.webp`

Symptom: in the circle dance two men wore Babu's red-and-white checked head towel and face, side by side. A young, short-haired man in a collared shirt, who reads as Antony, stood among the dancers, although Antony is on the upper deck with Aashaan in this part of the storyboard and no Antony sheet was attached.

Cause: (1) `@crew` (the eight-man line-up) was attached together with `@sasi`, `@suresh`, `@raghavan` and `@babu`, and the line-up shows those same four men again (men 2, 5, 6, 8). "Men 1, 4 and 7 only" in text did not stop the model drawing Babu from both images. (2) Six dancers + the plate player gave the model a crowd to fill; with no sheet for every face it filled a gap with a generic young lead in a shirt.

Rule:
1. Never attach a group/line-up sheet in the same generation as individual sheets of men who also appear on it. Background men get their own sheets, or they are written in words with a clearly different costume.
2. Cast only men who have a sheet; say "exactly N men on the whole deck, each appears once, each a different face", and give each one a single unique marker (only Babu has a head towel; only Raghavan has a towel round the neck…).
3. Fill the rest of the deck with named empty things (planks, coiled rope, the rail), so no one is added. Do not name the absent lead in the prompt (L27).
