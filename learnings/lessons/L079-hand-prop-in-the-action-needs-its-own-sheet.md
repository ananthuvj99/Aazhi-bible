**L79 — A hand prop the whole action depends on dropped out: no mug in his hand** (2-bath v2.1)

Symptom: Antony had no mug in his hand, so the pour-over-the-head bath could not happen.

Cause: the mug lives only in the background plate (`@backyardwell`, standing on the slab), not on the character sheet (`@antonybath`), and v2.1 ran without a start image that showed it in his hand. Text alone ("a small red plastic mug in his right hand") was not enough — the same failure as L64 (the bag), this time for a hand prop the whole action needs.

Rule:
1. When the action is done with a hand prop (a mug, a ladle, a comb, a rope), the prop has its own prop sheet and ID and is attached in that shot, with its size, colour, material, which hand holds it and that it is full/empty.
2. Name it in the reference line, in every ACTION beat that uses it and in POSITIVE LOCKS (L64).
3. If the plate also shows the prop elsewhere (e.g. on the slab), say the one in his hand is the only one in frame (L51: no second copy).

Checklist line: every hand prop the action depends on has its own registered prop ID attached, with the hand, its state (full/empty) and size, in reference, every beat and locks; it is the only copy in frame.
