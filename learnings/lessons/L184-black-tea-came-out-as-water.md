Symptom: In the take, Antony's glass held plain, clear water instead of kattan chaya, though the start image shows dark red tea.
Cause: The tea was named by its kind ("kattan chaya: black tea, no milk, amber reddish-brown") and the prompt said "no milk" twice; nothing said what the liquid must look like on screen or that it must not be clear. The model kept the glass and dropped the colour, worst while the glass moves and is sipped.
Rule: A drink is written by its visible colour and opacity with a familiar comparison ("deep, dark reddish-brown like dark honey, the background only dimly through it"), tied to the start image ("exactly the colour in the start image"), and locked as "never clear, never colourless like water" in the prop block, PHYSICS (keeps its colour when lifted and sipped) and POSITIVE LOCKS.
Checklist line: Drinks keep their colour (L184): every drink in frame is written by visible colour and opacity, tied to the start image, and locked "never clear like water" in the prop block, PHYSICS and the locks.
