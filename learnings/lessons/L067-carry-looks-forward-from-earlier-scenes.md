**L67 — What a character gains in one scene must reach every later scene's prompt** (s11 → s13)

Symptom: Antony gained his beige canvas shoulder bag in the scenes Ranjith made (s11 tuition). When the next scene (SB019, harbour) was prompted, the creator had to point out again that he arrives with the bag and supply its image.

Cause: the bag lived only in the s11 shot files and an evidence frame. Nothing told the next scene's prompt to look back at what Antony had become, so the carry-over depended on someone remembering.

Rule: before every breakdown and prompt, for each character in the shot:
1. Read the carry-forward lines in `continuity/showreel.md` and the end frames of the previous scenes they appear in (`scenes/showreel/approved/`, and the reference frames in `scenes/showreel/evidence/` that the shot files point to).
2. Carry every prop, costume change, injury or state they gained into the new prompt: in their reference line, in each ACTION beat they are in, and in POSITIVE LOCKS (L64).
3. Attach the registered ID for it. If the only picture of it is a frame from another place, do not attach that frame (it drags that place in, L60/L61): write it in full and propose a clean prop image to register.
4. When a scene gives a character something new, add a carry-forward line for it to `continuity/showreel.md` in the same push.

Checklist line: item 79 in `rules/pre-delivery-checklist.md`.
