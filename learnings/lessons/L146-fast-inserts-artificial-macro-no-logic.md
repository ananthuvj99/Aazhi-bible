**L146 — "Fast close inserts" came out artificial: macro body parts on an empty quay, a porter staring into the lens, a handcart rolling by itself, actions with no cause** (s16b-sh34 v1, failed)

Symptom: six 1.3 s cuts of isolated feet, a sack filling the frame, a headless back against the sky, a face glaring straight at the camera, a cart moving with nobody pushing; the quay empty behind; no physics, no logic, an advertisement look.

Cause: the prompt asked for "close", "low angle at ground level", "close on a face" without saying what the whole person and the people around were doing; "two pairs of legs pushing it" was dropped; 1.3 s per cut is too short to show a complete action; "eyes squinting in the sun" with no eyeline turned the face to the lens.

Rule:
1. Inserts in a working scene are medium shots that show whole people doing a complete action with a cause and an effect (lift, carry, drop, push), and the busy place behind them; no macro body parts on an empty background.
2. Write how the work is really done (who lifts, who carries, how the weight sits, what the body does) in a block of its own, and that objects never move by themselves.
3. At least 2 s per cut; three or four cuts in 8 s at most.
4. Every face has an eyeline on the work or on another person; "never to the lens".
5. Ask for a documentary feel: moments caught, not posed.
