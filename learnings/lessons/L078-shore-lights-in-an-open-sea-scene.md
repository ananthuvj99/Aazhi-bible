**L78 — A palm line with shore lights appeared in a mid-ocean scene, because the prompt asked for it** (s16b-sh02 v3) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh02-v3-sasi-twice-land-on-horizon.webp`

Symptom: the song on deck happens far out at sea, but a dark coast with palms and small lights ran along the horizon.

Cause: the prompt's background line said "a thin black palm line on the horizon with a few tiny white shore lights", copied from the look of an earlier frame. Everything written gets drawn (L27). No registered night-at-sea plate was attached for the background.

Rule:
1. Check the storyboard for where the scene is (harbour, near the coast, open sea) before writing the background, and never carry a background over from another scene's frame.
2. Open-sea scenes say "open sea in every direction, one clean, empty horizon line between dark sea and sky" and attach a registered at-sea plate (e.g. `@urudeckc2platenight`, background only) so the sea is copied, not invented.
