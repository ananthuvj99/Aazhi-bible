Symptom: Adwaithan's short line "അപ്പോൾ ഇതോ?" came out too slow, almost whispered; and the steel sugar tin on the counter moved by itself during the shot.
Cause: The delivery said "slow and dry" and the tone line "slow, dry, innocent-sounding": the model read slow plus dry as quiet. The sugar tin was named only as set dressing, with no stillness lock, and a hand action on the same counter (turning a tumbler) let nearby steel objects drift.
Rule: A short teasing line is written with its volume and pace in plain words (normal, clearly audible, medium natural pace, about N seconds) and never "slow", "dry", "soft" together. Every small object near a hand action is locked still by name in PHYSICS and POSITIVE LOCKS; only the object the hand touches may move.
Checklist line: Audible short lines and still props (L183): no "slow and dry" for a short line, volume and pace written plainly; every small prop near a hand action is named as still in PHYSICS and the locks, and only the touched object moves.
