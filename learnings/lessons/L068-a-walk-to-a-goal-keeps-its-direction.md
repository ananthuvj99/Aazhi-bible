**L68 — A walk toward a goal keeps its direction across the cut** (s13-sh02 v1) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s13-sh02-v1-wrong-direction.png`

Symptom: in shot 1 Antony walks away from the view along the quay toward his uru (warehouses right, water and uru left). Shot 2 put the camera at the uru looking back along the quay. In the take he came from the far side, walked past the uru, and its bow faced the wrong way: the geography read as a different place and he no longer seemed to be going to his boat.

Cause: the plate for shot 2 (panel 13B) was drawn looking the opposite way along the quay. A reverse angle on a walk turns the goal around in the frame; the model kept the plate's layout and lost the link to shot 1.

Rule: when a character walks toward a goal across several shots, keep the same screen direction: the goal stays ahead, the same things stay on the same side (here: uru and water left, warehouses right). Prefer following from behind, or from the same side of the line of action. If the only plate looks the other way, mirror it left-right (when it has no lettering) or make a new one. Name the sides in the prompt in capitals and repeat them in POSITIVE LOCKS. Use a reverse angle only on purpose, after a shot that shows the turn.
