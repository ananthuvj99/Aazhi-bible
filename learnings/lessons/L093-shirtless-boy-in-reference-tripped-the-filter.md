**L93 — A shirtless boy in a reference image, with close wording about him, tripped the content filter** (s16b-sh05 v6) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh05-v6-content-filter.png`

Symptom: Higgsfield's content filter blocked the generation before rendering ("a reference image or a phrase was flagged").

Likely cause: `@crewextras` (first version) showed line-up man 3, a boy of about 15, without a shirt, beside adult men; the prompt described him sitting, "clapping on his knees" and "smiling up at" Antony, plus "slaps his thighs" (L72).

Rule:
1. Never attach a reference image that shows a child without a shirt; crop him out or leave him out.
2. Keep children out of crowd and party shots with adults unless the story needs them, and then fully clothed, at a distance, with plain wording (no body or touch words).
3. When the filter blocks, check the reference images first (who is in them, what they wear), then the L72 word list.

Checklist line: item 110 in `rules/pre-delivery-checklist.md`.
