**L155: the location sheet came out as a pasted backdrop, the eye close-up turned horror, and the cuts got hit sounds** (s16b-sh39 v1)

Symptoms: (1) in the front mid shot the house and beach were the `@houseofantony` sheet image itself, flat and over-bright behind Rosamma, like a green screen; the whole take also took the sheet's warm golden grade. (2) "the sea reflected small in the pupil" gave a glassy eye filled with the sea: a horror look. (3) Hard cuts came with hit/whoosh sounds.
Fixes: (1) say the location sheet is used only for how the place looks, built as a real place at real distance from this shot's own camera, never pasted as a flat backdrop; add a BACKGROUND block (real depth, soft focus, same light as the person, never brighter or sharper than her); state the grade in words (here natural, slightly cool daylight, no golden haze) so the sheet's grade is not copied. (2) For emotional eye shots show both eyes, natural, with only a soft catchlight; never ask for reflections in the eye. (3) In AUDIO: one continuous ambience across the cuts; no whoosh, hit or impact sound on any cut or whip pan.
