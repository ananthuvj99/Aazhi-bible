**L139: The crescent moon stood upright; in Kerala skies it lies nearly horizontal** (review of the generated cut, 2026-11-11)

Symptom: The crescent moon in the night shots stands upright, like a "C". Ranjith wants it lying horizontally, the way it looks from the Kerala coast.

Cause: The prompts said "half moon" or "crescent moon" and nothing about its tilt, so the model drew the familiar upright crescent of picture books. At Kerala's latitude (about 9 degrees north) the crescent lies close to flat.

Rule:
1. Write the moon's shape as geometry: "a thin crescent moon lying almost flat, its lit edge along the bottom, both horns pointing up", plus where it sits ("high on the RIGHT").
2. Do not compare it to another object ("a boat", "a smile"): a comparison word is drawn as the thing itself (L126).
3. Keep the same moon (shape, tilt, side, height) in every shot of the night; add it to the night-rescue distance sheet's light table.
