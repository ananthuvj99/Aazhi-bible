**L76 — Say which garment is on top, and steer with both hands from behind the wheel** (s21-sh02 v2) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s21-sh02-v2-vest-over-shirt-one-hand.png`

Symptom: (1) Aashaan came out with the white vest looking worn over his cream shirt. (2) He stood beside the wheel at the left, one hand on it, looking away to the side: nobody steers a boat like that.

Cause: (1) "a cream shirt worn open over a white sleeveless vest" names both garments with no clear order; the model layered them the wrong way. (2) The camera was "just behind his right shoulder" and the action had him leaning to look at a compass, so the model put him off to the side of the wheel with one hand.

Rule:
1. Layering: write the outer garment first and say where the inner one shows: "the cream half-sleeve shirt is the outer layer, open down the front; the white vest is underneath it and shows only at the open neck". Lock it in POSITIVE LOCKS.
2. Steering: the helmsman stands squarely BEHIND the wheel, centred on it, facing forward, BOTH hands on the spokes (about ten and two), eyes forward through the windows; turning the wheel is hand over hand. No glances that turn his body away.
3. Frame helm shots from directly behind and a little above, wide enough to show both hands on the wheel and what he steers toward (the bow, the sea, the watchman at the bow).
