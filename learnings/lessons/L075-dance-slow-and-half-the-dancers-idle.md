**L75 — The dance came out slow, and half the crew stood instead of dancing** (s16b-sh02 v1) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh02-v1-babu-twice-extra-young-man.webp`

Symptom: of the crew written as dancing, about half only stood or walked around; the ones who moved danced slowly and stiffly, nothing like real men dancing fast to a beat.

Cause: (1) The circle was given a speed of "walking pace, about 3 km/h", and the model treated that as the speed of the whole dance. (2) The holds at the start and end were written as "Hold", which the model read as everyone pausing. (3) The moves were given once, to some of the men, in one beat; the others had no movement written for the rest of the clip, so they stood.

Rule:
1. Give a dance its tempo in numbers: the beat in bpm, steps per second, how fast the circle turns (about 6 km/h for a fast circle dance), "real time, not slow motion".
2. Every dancer is named with his own continuous moves, and "dances non-stop from the first frame to the last". The opening and closing holds keep the camera still while the dancing continues: never write "Hold" alone in a dance shot.
3. Describe the energy as visible body action: feet stamping on every beat, knees lifting high, arms thrown up and down fast, shoulders shaking, chests heaving, sweat running, breath panting.
