**L110 — The model copied the plain grey-box boat and dropped the carved details of the uru sheet** (s16b-sh10 v1, partial)

Symptom: camera, faces and bokeh followed the blocking, but the deckhouse had bare posts, plain beams and bare rails; the balustrades, shuttered windows and studded planks of the @uru sheet were missing.

Cause: the blocking set is a low-poly boat; the video is the strongest reference, so its simple shapes were reproduced and the sheet was treated as a loose hint.

Rule: in a blocking prompt say that the grey-box boat is only a placeholder for positions and camera, and spell the sheet's details in words in the MOTION REFERENCE, the @uru line, the PERFORMANCE (what the view shows between people) and the locks. Add detail to the blocking set only if that is not enough.

Checklist line: item 129 in `rules/pre-delivery-checklist.md`.
