**L159: An English paraphrase of the line anywhere in the prompt can make the platform speak English**

What happened: Antony's line "ബാബുവേട്ടാ, അങ്ങോട്ട് നോക്ക്!" came out in English. SCENE CONTEXT said he "tells Babu to look" and the intent line said he "makes him look at the burning boat"; Malayalam was named only in AUDIO.

Rule: never describe what is said, in any block (L29); say "says one Malayalam line". Name Malayalam in SCENE CONTEXT, DIALOGUE RULES, AUDIO, CONSTRAINTS ("no English words anywhere in the audio"), POSITIVE LOCKS and the intent line, and set the platform's dialogue language to Malayalam before generating.
