**L142: The moon was ahead-right in the bow view and also behind the deckhouse in the aft view; one moon cannot be in both places** (full-moon plates, 2026-11-11)

Symptom: in the bow view (looking forward) the full moon is high on the right. The aft view (looking back at the deckhouse) also showed a full moon in the sky. Ranjith caught it: if the moon is already ahead of the boat, how can it be behind the deckhouse too?

Cause: each plate was given a moon "on its own side", copied from the old half-moon plates, with no single position for the moon in the sky. Both old plates already had this error.

Rule:
1. For each night, fix one place for the moon relative to the boat (for SB027–SB032: high, ahead, to the right of the bow) and write it in the sequence's continuity sheet.
2. Work out every view from that one place: a camera looking toward it shows the moon and its path on the water; a camera looking away has the moon behind it, out of frame, with no moon path on the sea and the light coming from behind the camera.
3. Before making or attaching a plate, check its moon against the sheet. The same applies to the sun in day scenes.
