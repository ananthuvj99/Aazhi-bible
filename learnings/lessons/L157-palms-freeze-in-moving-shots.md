Symptom: In 1A v17, v19 and v24 (aerial flight from the sea to the house) the coconut palms stayed nearly still, even with "every frond moves", a 30 km/h wind and a palm-motion reference video attached.
Cause: in a long, fast camera move the model spends its motion on the camera and keeps the far trees still; 20–70 cm of frond movement is invisible at 50–500 m, and gentle words ("gently", "trunks stay firm") read as calm.
Rule: when foliage must move, (1) put the wind first in SCENE CONTEXT; (2) end the shot with a locked-off hold of 4–5 s where only the world moves; (3) put a palm close to the camera (fronds hanging into a top corner, a few metres away) so the motion is big in frame; (4) write a strong, gusty wind with large movement (tips about 1 m, crowns tossing, trunks bending a little), never "gently" or "firm"; (5) a CONSTRAINTS line naming the frozen-palm failure.
Checklist line: Foliage that must move: wind in the first line, a near palm or branch big in frame, a 4–5 s locked-off hold, strong movement words with sizes, and the frozen-foliage failure named in CONSTRAINTS (L157).
