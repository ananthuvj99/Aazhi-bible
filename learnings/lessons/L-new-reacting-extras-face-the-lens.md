**L-new — Laughing extras turn round to the lens** (s11-sh01 v4) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s11-sh01-v4-faces-to-lens-front-table.png`

Symptom: all eight children, seen from behind, turned round and smiled into the lens, and Clara looked back over her shoulder at the back of the room, although only four children were meant to turn, toward the door.

Cause: "turns her head sideways to the right" and "giggle" never said what each face points at, so the model turned every face to the viewer, the usual pose for a laughing group. Nothing kept the back four still, and Clara's look was written as "toward the door" without saying where her eyes stay.

Rule: every reacting extra gets a target and a profile: "turns her head to her right toward the door; face toward the door in profile, the back of her head toward the view". The extras who do not react are told what they keep doing ("keep writing, heads bowed"). The main character's look names who she looks at and where her eyes stay. POSITIVE LOCKS repeats who turns and where they look.

Checklist line: item 78 in `rules/pre-delivery-checklist.md`.
