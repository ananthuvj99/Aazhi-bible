**L154: Rosamma has a second sheet (maroon house dress); the prompt still described her saree**

Symptom: s16b-sh39 attached Aswanth's new `@rossama-v2` (maroon floral Kerala nightie), but the prompt's references and physics still described the saree and pallu from the old card.
Cause: the prompt was written from the old card; the new sheet was not checked.
Fix: before writing, open the sheet the creator attaches, not only the card. Describe the clothes on that sheet, and make the physics match the garment (a loose cotton dress billows and presses in the wind; a saree pallu lifts). Rosamma's card now has both looks.
