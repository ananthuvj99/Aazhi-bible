**L167: "camera in front of the swimmer, the fire behind the camera" made him swim away from the fire** (s24-sh03 v1.1)

Symptom: the model still showed the burning boat in the frame, so it put it behind Antony, and he swam toward the camera, away from it.
Cause: the goal (the fire) was placed out of frame behind the camera; the model keeps important references in frame and moved it behind him.
Fix: keep the goal in frame and give screen direction: track side-on, the swimmer moving screen left to right toward the goal ahead on the right, where he came from (the uru) behind him on the left, his face seen as he turns to breathe; repeat the direction in the locks and match it to the previous shot. Never put the goal behind the camera.
