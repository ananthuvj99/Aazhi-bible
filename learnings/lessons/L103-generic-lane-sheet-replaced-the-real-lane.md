**L103 — The close cut on the lane jumped to a different village street** (2B v5) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/2B-v5-lane-changed.jpg`

Symptom: in CUT 2 Antony walked between pink and blue cottages with nets on poles — the @villagelane sheet — with no laterite wall, no house and no sea; it did not read as the lane beside his house a moment after the wide.

Cause: @villagelane was attached in full mode for the close cut and the cut had no start image, so the model rebuilt the sheet's street (L87, L53: a sheet copies its whole place).

Fix: for a close cut that continues a wide, describe the background from the wide (behind him the laterite wall, the blue house, palms, the sea at the right) and attach no other location sheet; better, make a start still for the close cut from the wide (L61).
