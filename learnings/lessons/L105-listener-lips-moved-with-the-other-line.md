**L105 — Antony's lips moved with his mother's line** (2B v6)

Symptom: in the wide, while Rosamma called her line from the door, Antony — walking toward the camera, face visible — moved his lips as if he were saying it.

Cause: two speakers in one generation, and the listener's face was the biggest face in frame during her line; the model put the voice on the most visible mouth. Nothing locked his mouth closed in that cut.

Fix: while one person speaks, write the listener's mouth closed in that beat ("Antony's mouth stays closed; only Rosamma's lips move") and keep the listener's face away from the camera or small during the other person's line; give the line to the speaker whose face is clearest, or split speakers into separate generations (L58).
