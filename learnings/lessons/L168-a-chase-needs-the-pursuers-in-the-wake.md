Symptom: the three motorboats ran side by side, level with the uru and off to one side; the shot read as boats travelling together, not a chase. The light was warm and glassy, with no tension.
Cause: "loose arrowhead, all heading screen right" gave no line of pursuit; nothing tied the boats to the uru's wake or to the gap closing, and a calm golden dawn read as peace.
Rule: in a chase, the pursuers ride directly in the target's wake, nose to tail or in a tight wedge on the same line, aimed at its stern; state the gap in boat lengths and that it closes; at least one man on the target looks back at them. Give tension through the weather (grey, wind, chop, spray), never with a golden glassy sea.
Checklist line: chase shots: pursuers in the target's wake on the same line, aimed at its stern, gap in boat lengths closing; a look back from the target; tense weather, no calm golden glass.
