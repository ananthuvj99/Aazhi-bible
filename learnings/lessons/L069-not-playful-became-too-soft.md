**L69 — "Not playful" turned into a mumble**
To stop a playful delivery, v1.1 said "low and easy, half a sigh, no laugh" — and "ഓ, ഈ അമ്മ!" came out so soft it barely registered.
Rule: when calming a line down, keep the volume: say "clear, full ordinary voice, every word heard, never mumbled", give the stress word by word ("ഓ" short, "ഈ" long, "അമ്മ" with the double "മ്മ" held, falling), and name the feeling positively ("fond exasperation") instead of only listing what to avoid.
