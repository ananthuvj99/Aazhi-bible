**L171: any mention of the wheelhouse put Aashaan inside it, even with the sheet removed** (s24-sh08 v1, v2)

Symptom: Aashaan was asked to stand OUTSIDE, in front of the wheelhouse; both takes put him inside at the wheel, with the windows round him.
Cause: Aashaan + "wheelhouse" (sheet or words, "windows", "wheel") is a strong pair: the model puts the helmsman at the helm.
Fix: when Aashaan or anyone is on the open deck, do not mention the wheelhouse, its windows or the wheel at all; place them by the mast or the bow and give an open background ("the rigging and the dark sea behind; no room, no walls, no windows"). L165 (describe the windows) applies only when the deckhouse front must be seen and nobody stands near it.
