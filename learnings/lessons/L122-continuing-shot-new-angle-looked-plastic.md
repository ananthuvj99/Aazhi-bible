**L122 — A shot seconds after the last one, from a new angle with only a reference image, came out as a different, plastic scene** (s23-sh06 v6, failed)

Symptom: after the dark, real s23-sh05 frame (the four frightened men at the rail, the carved deckhouse, one lantern), the next shot showed a plain low wall and empty sea, bright even studio light, plastic skin, Antony standing stiff and half smiling, a whole burning boat in view, and it ran 5 s instead of 8. It did not look like the same film.

Cause:
1. A new camera angle with the previous frame only as a reference image: the model kept the faces but rebuilt the set, light and mood.
2. Calm words: "Resolve: he has decided", "firm", plus the dialogue block's "soft, even frontal light" made a calm, evenly lit face. Nothing said fear.
3. The platform duration was left at 5 s.

Rule:
1. A shot that happens seconds after the last one uses the last frame as the START image on the SAME camera (push in, pan or tilt from there); a new angle only when the story needs it, and then the reverse is planned with its own start frame (L109).
2. When the creator wants it for continuity, the listeners may stay in frame while one person speaks (the start frame shows them); lock their mouths closed and keep the speaker's face clear.
3. Write the feeling in every beat and in PERFORMANCE and LOCKS ("fear in every face, nobody smiles"), never only as one abstract word.
4. Light: "exactly the start image's light; no extra light, no bright faces, no plastic skin"; after the dialogue block add that the frontal light is only the practical source (the lantern).
5. Set the platform duration (8 s) every time (L71).
