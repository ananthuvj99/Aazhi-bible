**L175: "low wooden rail with turned posts" drew a carved balustrade behind the speaker, and the moon came into the sky** (s24-sh12 v4)

Symptom: in a dialogue close shot at the main-deck rail, a carved turned-post railing and a raised platform appeared behind Antony, unlike the uru; the moon showed as a bright disc in the sky.
Cause: "turned posts" belongs to the deckhouse balustrade, not the main-deck side rail; "cool moonlight on the sea" let the model draw the moon.
Fix: for the main-deck side rail write "a plain, low, solid wooden bulwark with a flat, smooth brown top rail, no posts, no carving" (L152) and say what is behind the speaker ("only this plain rail and the open sea"); in night exteriors write "the sky is empty: no moon anywhere in it; moonlight only as a sheen on the water".
