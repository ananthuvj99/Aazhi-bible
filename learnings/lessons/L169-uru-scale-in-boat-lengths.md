Symptom: the uru came out like a ship beside tiny motorboats, about 16 times their length instead of 3; the "straight down" camera came out as a high three-quarter view close to the uru, with the horizon in frame.
Cause: "a large cargo vessel" pushed ship scale, and the near uru in an oblique view grew further by perspective; the top view needs a start frame or it drifts to the oblique view the model prefers.
Rule: give the uru's size against what is beside it ("the uru is only three motorboat lengths long; a wooden boat, not a ship") and never call it large, a vessel or a ship; keep the uru and the boats at the same distance from the camera (or the boats nearer); a strict top-down view needs a start frame.
Checklist line: the uru's size is written in lengths of the nearest boat or person, with no "large / vessel / ship"; the camera distance does not make the uru near and the others far.
