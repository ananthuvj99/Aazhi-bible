**L165: from outside, the wheelhouse front came out as barred wooden windows; from inside it has three glass windows** (s16b-sh43 v1)

Symptom: in an outside shot on the main deck, the front wall of the deckhouse (the wheelhouse front) came out with rows of wooden bars and slats. The approved inside view (`@wheelhouse`, Aashaan at the wheel) shows three large rectangular glass windows in thick dark-brown wooden frames looking over the deck to the bow.
Cause: the outside prompt did not describe the deckhouse front, so the model filled it with the carved lattice of the rest of the deckhouse.
Fix: in every outside shot where the deckhouse front is in frame, attach `@wheelhouse` (background only) and describe the wall: three large glass windows side by side in thick dark-brown wooden frames, rain or light on the glass, the top of the wheel just visible inside; no bars, slats or lattice on that wall.
