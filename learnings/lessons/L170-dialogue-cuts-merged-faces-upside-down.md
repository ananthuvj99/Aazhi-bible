**L170: a five-shot dialogue scene came out as one wide shot, the lying speakers' faces upside down** (s24-sh07 v1)

Symptom: four lines planned as close-ups after an opening wide came out as one continuous wide shot; the two men lying on the deck had their heads toward the camera, faces upside down; nobody's lips readable.
Cause: the opening wide set the framing and the model kept it (L50, L109); "medium close-up" was not strong enough; "from above" on a lying man puts his face upside down.
Fix: for dialogue, start straight in close-up (no establishing wide in the same generation), write "every shot a TIGHT FACE CLOSE-UP of the one man who speaks, never a wide shot", name a portrait lens (85 mm); for a lying speaker, put the camera low beside his shoulder "so his face is UPRIGHT in the frame, never upside down"; lock both. Hang lanterns on a named hook (L166).
