**L83 — Nobody stands like a statue: every person on screen is alive the whole shot** (s21-sh02 v4, about 30% good, taken to the edit)

Symptom: the setting and the night look were good, but Antony at the bow stood rigid like a statue for the whole shot, and Aashaan turned the wheel in stiff, mechanical moves. No real person stands frozen for 4–8 seconds.

Cause: the prompts wrote holds as "Still." and "he stays there, still… hold", and gave the background figure one small action ("shifts his weight") and nothing else. The model read "still" literally and froze the body; a single scripted move between freezes looks robotic.

Rule (every shot, every person in frame, including small background figures):
1. Never write "still", "frozen", "motionless" or "stays there" for a person. A hold is a quiet beat, not a freeze: the camera holds, the person keeps living.
2. Give each person continuous small life for the whole clip: breathing that moves the chest and shoulders, weight shifting from foot to foot, small head turns and glances, hands adjusting (a grip, the rail, the face, the clothes), blinking, the body riding the boat's roll.
3. Movements overlap and flow; no move starts from or ends in a freeze. Say "relaxed, loose, natural, never stiff or mechanical" in PERFORMANCE.
4. Background figures get the same: at least two small natural movements spread across the shot, plus breathing and sway.
5. On a boat, everyone sways with the roll: knees soft, weight moving with the deck.
