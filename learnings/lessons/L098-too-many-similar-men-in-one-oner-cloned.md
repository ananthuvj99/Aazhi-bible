**L98 — Eight references and eight men in one long oner: Raghavan and Babu were cloned** (s16b-sh05 v10) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh05-v10-contact.png`, `…-v10-two-raghavans.png`

Symptom: in the second half two grey-haired men with red checked towels and white vests danced (two Raghavans), two men wore Babu's head towel, and Suresh appeared both at the rail and in the ring.

Cause: eight references (Antony, four crew sheets, `@crewextras`, `@steelplate`, `@uru`) and eight men in one 15 s move, with three of them in near-identical white vests (Raghavan, the stocky man, the tall man). With that many similar faces and costumes the model reused the strongest looks. The "only one who…" markers (L95) were present and still not enough at this load.

Rule:
1. Keep a generation to about six references and five or six people; when a beat needs more people, split it into its own shot (L37, L58).
2. Do not put several men in the same costume type (white vest + white mundu) in one frame; give extras clearly different clothes or leave them out of that shot.
3. A long walk and a group action are two set-ups: generate them separately and cut in the edit.
