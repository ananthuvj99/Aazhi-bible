**L131 — Every uru deck shot attaches the bow plate from the start; Aswanth had to ask for it twice** (s16b-sh20 v1, s16b-sh23 v1)

Symptom: after L116 made `@urubowplate` (the clean bow plate from the approved prayer take), the seabird shot and the flare shot were still delivered without it, and Aswanth had to ask each time: "add the bow plate too, otherwise the location will get changed".

Cause: the plate was treated as optional, attached only when the bow is "in the middle of the frame", and left out to save an attachment.

Rule: on every shot set on or around the uru deck (night song, life on the uru), `@urubowplate` is in the first delivered prompt, attached background only, with a reference line ("the bow of our uru exactly as on this plate, seen …") and a lock ("our bow exactly as on @urubowplate wherever it is in view"), even when the bow is only at the edge of the frame or seen from far away. To save an attachment, drop something else.

Checklist line: item 151 in `rules/pre-delivery-checklist.md`.
