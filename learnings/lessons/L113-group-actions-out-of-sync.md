**L113 — A group moved in unison: each person needs their own action and their own timing** (s23-sh05 v2, approved, flagged)

Symptom: the take came out well, but the three crewmen woke, got up and went to the rail all together, at the same moment and in the same way, which looks staged.

Cause: one ACTION beat gave all three men the same verbs in the same time window ("they get up quickly and hurry to the rail").

Rule (adds timing to L92, which covers placement):
1. When two or more people react, give each person his own beat with its own start time, offset by 0.5–1.5 s: the first reacts at once, the next hesitates, the last is slowest or does something else first.
2. Give them different actions, not the same verb: one sits up and stares, one fumbles for his towel or shirt, one stays crouched a moment before standing.
3. Different speeds and paths: one hurries, one walks, one stops halfway; they end at different distances and in different poses (L92).
4. Never write "they all", "together", "at the same time" for a group action unless unison is the point.
