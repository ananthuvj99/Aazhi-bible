**L116 — The bow behind the actor was invented: an open hold with cross beams instead of the closed blue bow of the earlier takes** (s16b-sh15 v2, partial)

Symptom: Sasi's expression, the photograph and the framing were right, but the bow behind him was a different boat: a narrow pointed bow with an open hatch and cross beams. In the approved prayer takes (s16b-sh07) the bow is a closed blue triangular bow deck with red-brown bitts and big rope coils.

Cause: the shot looked toward the bow with only `@uru` attached. The four-view sheet shows the bow tiny and from far away, so the model made up the close view. The earlier takes had the blocking video to hold the shape. The registered plate `@urudeckc2platenight` shows yet another bow (dark, balustrade rails, two bitts), so it does not match the song footage either.

Rule: once a set has been seen in an approved take, every later shot that looks at it attaches a plate made from that take (a clean, empty frame of that view, registered with its own ID, background only), and repeats its features in words in the location line and the locks. If no matching plate exists, make one before generating, or frame the shot so that part of the set is out of view.
