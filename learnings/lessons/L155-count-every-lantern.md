**L155: Two lanterns appeared where the start frame has one** (s23-sh01 v5)

Symptom: Aswanth's frame has one small lantern on the deck beside the rope coil. The take showed two lanterns.

Cause: the prompt mentioned "a small lantern" in MEMORY and "the lantern" again in LIGHTING and PHYSICS without a count, and an earlier keyframe had briefly put a lantern on a wall shelf. The model read it as more than one light source.

Rule:
1. Every practical light is counted and placed in its own block (THE ONE LANTERN): its size, where it stands, "there is only ONE lantern in the whole shot: none on the rail, the posts or the walls".
2. POSITIVE LOCKS repeats the count: "Exactly ONE lantern, on the deck by the rope coil."
3. Keyframe QC counts every lamp against the start frame (L151).
