**L141: The final scene (a boat on fire, a man struggling in the water) had calm music and calm people** (review of the generated cut, 2026-11-11)

Symptom: In the final scene a nearby boat is on fire and someone is struggling in the water, but the background music does not fit, there are no shouts and no emergency sounds, and the characters do not hurry toward it.

Cause: The standard "sound effects only, no music, steady ambient that does not build" block was used unchanged, so the platform filled the gap with its own calm cue. The action described looking and pointing, not urgent movement.

Rule:
1. For emergency scenes, the AUDIO block lists the danger sounds with times: the fire roaring and crackling across the water, timber cracking, a man splashing and choking far off, men shouting in Malayalam off screen (overlapping, words not clear), running bare feet on the deck, a rope thrown and paid out.
2. Music for these scenes is Ranjith's call (this review asked for dramatic music): when wanted, name it exactly, e.g. "tense low drums and strings, a fast pulse building, no melody, no song". When not wanted, keep the no-music block and say "ambient builds with the danger".
3. Write urgency into every body: running, pushing past each other, grabbing the rail and the rope, pointing and shouting between beats; nobody stands still and watches.
