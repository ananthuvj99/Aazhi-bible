**L97 — The boom from the vessel sheet came down across the dance floor, and the dancers stood about instead of circling** (s16b-sh05 v9) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh05-v9-boom-across-deck-no-ring.webp`; how it should look: `scenes/showreel/approved/s16b-sh02-v4-frame.webp`

Symptom: a long wooden spar crossed the deck at waist height right through the space round the mast, so there was no room to circle; the men stood scattered, Babu held the plate instead of Raghavan, and Suresh stood at the rail instead of dancing.

Cause: `@uru`'s sheet shows the sail's long spar running from the mast toward the deckhouse, and the prompt never said the space round the mast is open. The approved master (s16b-sh02 v4) had a clear deck round a bare mast with the rope coil at its foot, but that was written in words there and not here. With "four men dance in a ring" said only once, the model staged a loose group.

Rule:
1. For action round a structure, describe the space as in the approved shot: "the mast stands alone in an open, clear deck about 5 m across; the coil of rope is wound round its foot; nothing crosses the deck between knee and head height" (L63).
2. Restate the ring in ACTION and POSITIVE LOCKS ("dancing in a tight ring right round the mast, moving counter-clockwise, from the first frame"), and say who holds each prop and whose hands are free.
