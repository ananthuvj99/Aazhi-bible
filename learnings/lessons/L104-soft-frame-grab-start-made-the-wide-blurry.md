**L104 — The long shot came out blurry** (2B v5) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/2B-v5-wide-soft.jpg`

Symptom: the wide of the house was soft and smeary; faces and hands at 25 m were mush.

Cause: the start image @2bwidestart is a soft frame grab (image QA noted it), and the prompt asked for "soft haze" on top; the slow push added more smear.

Fix: give wides a sharp start image (an upscale or a clean still at full resolution) and write "crisp, sharp detail across the whole frame, deep focus; light haze only far away at the hills" instead of "soft haze".
