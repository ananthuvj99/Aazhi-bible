**L94 — The listener stood like a statue while the other person spoke** (2A-3 v2.6, approved anyway)

Symptom: Antony's line, wave and exit worked, but Rosamma stood almost motionless at the door the whole time; Ananthu will fix it in the edit.

Cause: her beats were written as states ("tense, lips closed", "tightens her grip") rather than as continuous small actions, so the model froze her. Same family as L83 (no statues).

Rule: in any shot with two or more people, everyone who is not speaking has a relevant, continuous small action in every beat, tied to the moment — e.g. a mother holding the plate shifts it in her hands, glances down at the food and back up, takes a half-step forward, her chest rising with a sigh, her lips pressing as she swallows a word. Keep their mouth closed and their movement below the speaker's in size, but never still.

Checklist line: every non-speaking person in frame has a relevant small action in every beat (no one only "stands", "waits" or "listens").
