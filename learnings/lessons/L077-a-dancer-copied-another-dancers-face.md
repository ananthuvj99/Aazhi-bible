**L77 — In a group of moving men, one dancer copied another's face (two Sasis)** (s16b-sh02 v3) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh02-v3-sasi-twice-land-on-horizon.webp`

Symptom: two men side by side had Sasi's long curly hair and full beard; five men came out where four were written (Sasi twice, plus Suresh, Babu and Raghavan).

Cause: "exactly four men, each appears once" was written, but the men had no fixed places in the circle. In a fast, crowded dance the model filled the circle with the most striking face (long hair and beard) twice. The four sheets were also all described in the same costume words (off-white shirt, white mundu), so the men were easy to merge.

Rule:
1. Give every person in a group a fixed place and order ("evenly spaced, a quarter of the circle apart, in this order: …") and keep the order in every beat and in POSITIVE LOCKS.
2. Describe each man by what only he has, including what he does NOT share: "Sasi is the only man with long hair or a beard; the other three have short hair and no beard".
3. Keep the head count low and give each man a different action (one carries the plate), so no two men look or move alike.
