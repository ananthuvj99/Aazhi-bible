**L73 — A bath in the sheet's costume, and a bath kept below the frame gets reinvented** (2-bath v1.1)

Symptom: Antony bathed wearing the costume from the `@antonyhome` sheet (the white cotton vest), and he did not bathe the way the prompt asked: the mug-from-the-bucket pours did not come out as written.

Cause: (1) The prompt says "100% matches the reference" and never says the vest is off, so the sheet's costume wins (L41, L64). (2) The scoop from the bucket happened "below the frame" in a shoulders-up shot: the model cannot see the bucket or the mug going into it, so it invents its own version of the bath.

Rule:
1. When a scene changes what a character wears from their sheet (bathing, sleeping, changing), say where the sheet item is and what is worn instead, in the reference line, in every ACTION beat and in POSITIVE LOCKS ("his white cotton vest hangs on the coconut-leaf screen beside the towel; he wears only the checked mundu"). Keep it filter-safe (L72): no "bare", "shirtless" or "naked"; describe what is worn and where the vest is.
2. Any action the creator cares about happens inside the frame: the frame is wide enough to show the object and the hand using it (the bucket on the slab, the mug going into it). Never write a key action as "below the frame".
3. Physical reactions are written as visible movements with timing (shoulders jerk up, a full-body shiver, the head shakes the water off), not as a feeling.

Checklist line: costume changes from the sheet are stated (where the item is, what is worn) in reference, every beat and locks, filter-safe; every action the creator asked for is inside the frame with its object visible.
