**L80 — A small personal moment played too fast: he combed and left before we could enjoy it** (2-mirror v3)

Symptom: the take was right and "kind of cute" — he came to the mirror, combed, walked away — but it went by too quickly; no time to see him look at himself and smile.

Cause: 12 s held a walk-in, a pick-up, combing, a pan, putting the comb down and a walk-off; every beat got about 1–2 s, so the looks and smiles had no room.

Rule: for a quiet character moment (grooming, looking at himself, a private smile), use the full 15 s and give each look and each smile its own beat of at least 1.5 s, written as a pause ("he stops combing, looks at himself, a small smile"). Keep the number of actions low.

Checklist line: quiet character moments give every look/smile its own beat of ≥1.5 s; use 15 s rather than packing 12 s.
