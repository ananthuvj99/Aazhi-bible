**L114 — When a take worked, the retake changes only the one thing that was wrong** (s23-sh05 v3, failed)

Symptom: v2 had worked; the only note was that the three crewmen woke and moved in unison. v3 rewrote the whole action (new end positions, Aashaan moving to the doorway, Antony stopping elsewhere, shorter scene text). The take lost what v2 had: the wheel showed very big in the deckhouse windows, nobody was readable at the start, and Antony came out of the deckhouse instead of running in from the bow.

Cause: too many changes at once; every rewritten sentence is a new chance for the model to go wrong, and trimming to fit the length cut lines that were holding the set together.

Rule:
1. For a retake of a take that mostly worked, start from that prompt word for word and change only the sentences the creator's note is about. List the changed sentences in the shot's History.
2. Never trim working lines to make room; trim elsewhere or keep the new detail short.
3. A person not in the start frame keeps the entry the working take used (L100); the deckhouse doorway is Aashaan's only.
