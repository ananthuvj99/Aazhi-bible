**L168: the uru showed its stern with the propeller out of the water; the dialogue two-shot came out side by side** (s24-sh05 v2)

Symptoms: (1) swimming "toward the uru", the model showed the uru's stern straight on, with the name board and the propeller and rudder sitting above the water. (2) "over Selvan's shoulder" still gave two men side by side, Antony in profile, so the line did not read as said to Selvan.
Fix: (1) whenever the uru is in frame, write that it sits low and heavy in the water, the propeller, rudder and bottom of the stern always under the water, never visible; and say which side is seen ("side-on, its long left side, never its stern"). (2) For a line said to someone: "the two FACE EACH OTHER, never side by side; the camera behind the listener, his back to the camera; the speaker faces the listener and so the camera".
