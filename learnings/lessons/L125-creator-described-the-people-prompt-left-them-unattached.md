**L125 — The creator clearly described who is seen and what they do; the prompt replaced that with a shortcut (people in words only, no IDs) and the take came out wrong** (s16b-sh18 v1)

Symptom: Aswanth narrated the shot plainly: Balan slides the window and sees the people from the earlier prayer shots sitting round the mast, with Babu and Raghavan taken out because they are cooking. The prompt did not attach those people. It wrote "five small seated men" in words, on the prompt writer's own judgement that far figures need no sheets. The result: dark figures in black, not our characters, in a clump, and a wasted generation.

Cause: the prompt writer (Claude) overrode a clear description with its own shortcut and did not go back to the earlier shots to collect who those people are and which IDs they use. The decision was not shown to the creator as a choice before he spent the credits.

Rule:
1. When the creator clearly describes the event and how he wants it, the prompt applies it exactly as told. His description is the specification, not a suggestion.
2. Any people he refers to from earlier shots ("the people sitting and praying", "the same crew") are identified from those shots: open the earlier shot files and prompts, list each person, take the exact IDs used there (on the creator's own account), and attach and place every one of them in the new prompt. Remove only the people he says to remove.
3. If a rule or a risk (too many references, far figures) argues for doing it differently, say so and ask BEFORE writing the prompt. Never change what he asked for silently, and never bury the change in a note after the prompt.
4. The delivery lists "People in this shot" with each ID and where it came from, so the creator can check it against what he said in one look.
