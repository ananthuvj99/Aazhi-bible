**L87 — A "background only" plate of another part of the boat was copied as the set: the two men ended up at the bow** (s16a-sh01 v3) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16a-sh01-v3-bow-behind-them.webp`; where they should be: `scenes/showreel/approved/s16b-sh04-stern-roof-position.png`

Symptom: Aashaan and Antony were meant to sit on the raised deck on the stern-cabin roof, but the take put them in front of the bow: the tall stem post and the bow rails stood right behind them.

Cause: `@urudeckc2platenight` (the bow looking forward) was attached "background only, for the open night sea and sky". The model does not take only the sea from a plate; it copies the place, so the bow's stem post and rails became their background. The text never described what really stands behind them on the stern roof.

Rule:
1. Never attach a plate of a different spot as "background only" just for its sea or sky. Attach a plate only when it is shot from where this camera stands (L53); otherwise attach none and write the background in words.
2. Describe what is directly behind the people from their real position (here: the roof's own railing and the empty sea astern, high above the water), and say how high they are, so the model places them correctly.
3. Check the place against a wide shot of the location (the approved wide `s16b-sh04` shows them on the stern roof), and when a new position matters, make a start still first (L61).
