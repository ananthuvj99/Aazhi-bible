**L145 — Balan's face came out polished; the five planned cuts became one tracking shot; several strangers wore Suresh's green mundu; "coiling a rope" became pulling a wire** (s16b-sh33 v3, partial)

Symptom: Balan's face looked smooth and glossy, like a retouched photo; the 20 s scene played as one continuous tracking shot instead of five cuts; two or more men wore green mundus, so Suresh lost his marker; Balan's rope task read as him pulling a coil like a wire, on the quay.

Cause: no words about real skin for a character whose sheet face is clean and smooth; a long prompt (about 7,700 characters, eight references) with five cuts collapsed into one move (L50, L109); the strangers' clothes were not given colours; a hand task with an object the model does not know well.

Rule:
1. Every prompt with Balan (`@balan-at-20s`) carries the BALAN'S FACE block: pores, uneven tone, his acne marks, sweat and oil sheen, fine lines, stubble shadow, matte skin, never polished or cosmetic, harsh light showing the texture (Aswanth's standing rule).
2. Write cuts as "THREE SEPARATE SHOTS, each from a completely different camera position; never one continuous moving shot", keep a scene to three cuts in about 12 s, and move extra beats (a close-up reaction) to their own short generation.
3. When a character's marker is a colour, give the crowd's clothes other colours: "no other man wears green".
4. Give characters simple, familiar tasks (sipping tea, wiping the face) rather than handling objects the model may misread.
