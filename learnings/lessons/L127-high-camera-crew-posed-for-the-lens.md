**L127: A high camera over a group, with "they look up toward Antony", made every man look up into the lens and smile, like a group photo** (s13-sh03c v1)

Symptom: the high shot from the rigging came back with Aashaan, Babu, Sasi and Antony standing still, faces up to the camera, smiling, as if posing for a photo. Antony had no bag. The gangplank was a different plank from the one in s13-sh02.

Cause:
1. The camera was high above them and the prompt said "they look up toward Antony" and "a big grin". "Look up" plus a camera above them = look at the camera.
2. A greeting for every man ("raises a hand", "waves", "grins") turned the work into a welcome line-up.
3. @antonybag was left out because the @antony20 sheet was thought to carry the bag; it does not.
4. The reference image (s13-sh04) did not show the gangplank, so the model invented one.

Rule:
1. Reveal a group the way a person sees it: follow behind the character who arrives, at his eye height, and let the group open up in front of him. Avoid high or overhead cameras for reaction beats.
2. Each person keeps working. At most one or two people glance at the newcomer, and only briefly. Write where each glance lands on the deck ("at Antony's face, left of the lens") and add the lock "nobody looks at the camera, nobody poses, nobody stops working to smile".
3. Whenever Antony is seen full length or from behind, attach @antonybag (its card says so) and write the bag on his LEFT shoulder in POSITIVE LOCKS.
4. A shot that shows the gangplank or another part of a set seen before uses the frame that shows it (approved/s13-sh02-frame.jpg for the gangplank) as its reference image.
