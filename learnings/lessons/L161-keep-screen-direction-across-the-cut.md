**L161: Keep screen direction across a cut: same arm, same side, same way he faces**

What happened: in the s23-sh02d close-up Antony faces the camera and points with his LEFT arm to the RIGHT of the frame. The next shot (Babu's reaction) had him with his back to the camera pointing INTO the frame, slightly left. Ranjith: "the transition looks odd".

Rule: across a cut in the same moment, a person keeps facing the same way relative to the camera, points with the same arm, and points to the same side of the frame; what he points at sits on that side. Stay on the same side of the line between the people and what they look at. Check the cut by putting the last frame of one shot next to the first of the next.
