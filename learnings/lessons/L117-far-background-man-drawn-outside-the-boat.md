**L117 — A man written as "standing far back at the right-hand rail, out of focus" was drawn outside the boat, beyond the bulwark** (s16b-sh10 v2)

Symptom: in the 360 turn round the praying ring, a small figure of a man appears beyond the bow bulwark, as if standing outside the hull over the sea.

Cause: the prompt kept one extra man "standing far back at the right-hand rail, out of focus" for continuity with the wide shot. In a close view with heavy bokeh the model had no deck space to put him, so it placed a small soft figure behind the rail line. A vague far-away person is a free-floating element.

Rule: in close shots keep only the people who are near the camera. Leave a far background person out of the prompt and out of the blocking unless the shot is wide enough to show the deck under his feet. Add the lock "every person is on the deck, inside the rails; beyond the bulwarks only sea and sky".
