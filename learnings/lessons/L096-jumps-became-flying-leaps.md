**L96 — "Jumps into the ring" became a flying leap high above the deck** (s16b-sh05 v8) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh05-v8-high-jump.webp`

Symptom: the young crewman joined the dance with a huge mid-air leap, legs tucked, far higher than a person can jump, which read as AI.

Cause: the prompt said he "whoops and jumps into the ring". The model exaggerated "jumps" into an action-film leap.

Rule: avoid "jumps", "leaps" and "springs up" for ordinary people; write the real move with its size: "steps quickly into the ring with a small hop, feet never more than a hand's width off the deck", "stamps", "bounces on bent knees". Say that feet stay close to the planks and the body has real weight.
