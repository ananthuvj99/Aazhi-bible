**L136: Clara's "നീ എന്നാ പോയിട്ട് വാ" came out too soft; the feeling did not reach the audience** (review of the generated cut, 2026-11-11)

Symptom: Clara tells Antony "നീ എന്നാ പോയിട്ട് വാ" (go then, and come back). Her voice is so soft that the emotion of the goodbye is lost.

Cause: The delivery stacked quiet words: "soft and warm, unhurried, gentle, falling". The voice model turns each one into less volume until the line is nearly a whisper, with nothing left to express.

Rule:
1. Write the feeling as something you can hear: a normal speaking volume (never whispered), a catch or break in the voice on the key word, a small rise and then a fall, a breath before the line, eyes that fill.
2. Never stack softness words ("soft, gentle, quiet, low"). Use at most one, and pair it with "clearly heard".
3. Put the emotion in the intent line as well ("let it be tender and aching, holding back tears"), and pick the scene's emotion from the platform list.
