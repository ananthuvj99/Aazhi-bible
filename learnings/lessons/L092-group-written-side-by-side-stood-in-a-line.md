**L92 — Watchers written "side by side" stood in a straight line and looked AI-made** (s16b-sh05 v5) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh05-v5-wrong-direction-watchers-in-a-line.webp`

Symptom: the four watching crewmen stood shoulder to shoulder in a neat row along one rail, all facing the same way, which read as staged and artificial.

Cause: the prompt said "stand side by side … in this order", which the model drew literally as a lined-up row (like the line-up sheet they came from).

Rule: place a group the way real people stand: split them across the space (some on the left, some on the right), at different distances and spacings, in different poses (one leaning on the rail, one sitting on a coil of rope, one half-turned to a friend). Never write "side by side in a row" or "in a line" for people who are not meant to be lined up.
