**L164: "lying on the deckhouse roof" put the men on the open foredeck, and the deck lost its mast base and boom** (s16b-sh41 v1.1)

Symptom: Sasi and Suresh came out lying on the open foredeck near the bow, with no mast, no thick cylindrical mast base and no boom on the deck; Aswanth had asked for the top level of the deckhouse. A generation (credits) lost.
Cause: "deckhouse roof" was a short phrase with no place on the boat; the bow plate and the foredeck words in the prompt pulled the men forward; the deck's fixed fittings were not listed, so the model dropped them.
Fix: whenever people are placed on the uru, name the exact place from the @uru sheet and describe it (for the top deck: the uppermost level of the two-level deckhouse at the back, a small plank floor inside a low carved balustrade, a ladder opening at its front), add "never on the main deck or the foredeck", and when any part of the main deck is in frame list its fixed fittings: the tall mast with its thick wooden cylindrical base at the centre of the deck, the long boom along the centre line, the rigging lines, two pairs of red-brown bitts and rope coils near the closed blue bow. Check the placement against the sheet's top view before delivery.
