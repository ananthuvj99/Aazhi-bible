**L108 — "Just inside the doorway" walked Antony into the room; a push through the class turned the pupils to the lens** (3tease v3) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/3tease-v3-walked-in-faces-to-lens.jpg`

Symptom: Antony walked from the door across the front of the room to the board; as the camera pushed up the aisle, the nearest pupils turned round and smiled into the lens.

Cause: the prompt placed him "just inside the doorway" and never said he stays; the start frame already showed him a step in, so the model kept him walking. The camera travelled up the aisle between the pupils, so the nearest faces turned toward it (L66), and "smile behind their hands" / "amused" invited the turn.

Fix: anchor a person who must not move: "stays exactly where the start frame shows him, takes no step further in", every beat and POSITIVE LOCKS, with a small action on the spot (L94). Keep the camera locked off when extras are close to it; give every pupil a task (writing, whispering to a neighbour) with the back of the head toward the view, and no smiling or reacting words when they should not react.
