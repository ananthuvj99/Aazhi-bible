**L153: "a plain bamboo rod" came out as a modern fishing rod with a spinning reel** (s16b-sh37 v1.1)

Symptom: Antony held a modern rod with a black spinning reel and line guides; in 1998 on a Kerala uru the crew fish with a hand-made stick.
Cause: the word "rod" pulls in a modern sport-fishing rod; the prompt did not exclude the reel.
Fix: never write "rod". Write "a hand-made fishing stick": one straight wooden stick cut from a branch, line tied straight to its tip, extra line wound on the thick end, no reel, no handle, no rings, no metal; the hook a plain hand-forged dull steel hook tied with thread. Lock it in POSITIVE LOCKS. Same for every period prop: describe how it was made, not its modern name.
