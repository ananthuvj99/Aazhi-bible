**L128: Antony came up a gangplank on the SIDE of the uru, but the take walked him in through the stern and straight down the deck toward the bow** (s13-sh03c v2)

Symptom: the gangplank became a short ladder into the stern end; Antony walked straight along the centreline toward the bow, as if he had come from behind the boat. There was open water on both sides and no quay, as if the uru were at sea. The bag hung at his right hip on a strap across his body.

Cause: the prompt said "steps onto the deck ... looking forward along the main deck toward the bow" and never said that he arrives over the SIDE rail, which way the bow points from the quay, or that he has to turn. Given the shortest version, the model lined the gangplank up with the deck.

Facts for this uru at the Cochin quay (s13-sh00 to sh02, cameras.md): the bow points back toward the view of s13-sh01, opposite to the way Antony walks along the quay. The gangplank rises from the quay to the side rail beside the deckhouse. Looking forward to the bow from the deck, the quay is beyond the LEFT rail (as in c2-bow). Looking aft, it is beyond the RIGHT rail (c1-aft).

Rule:
1. Whenever someone boards or leaves by a gangplank, write the side: "over the side rail, the quay behind him, the far rail and the water ahead of him".
2. Write the turn as a separate beat: "he turns to his LEFT to face the bow". The camera turns with him (it pans or arcs). Never let the camera look along the deck from the gangplank.
3. For any moored shot, write which rail the quay is beyond: when the deck view faces the bow, the quay is beyond the LEFT rail, with the gangplank's top at that rail.
4. The bag strap goes over his LEFT shoulder only and the bag hangs at his LEFT hip; never across the body.
