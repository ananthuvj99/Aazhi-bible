**L149: Raghavan's towel keeps ending up on his head, because Babu wears the same red-and-white checked towel on his head** (s21-sh04 v2 and v3 takes)

Symptom: in three generations (the s21-sh01 start frame, the s21-sh04 v2 take, the s21-sh04 v3 take) Raghavan wears his towel over his head or eyes. His sheet shows it round his neck. With two head towels, Raghavan reads as a second Babu.

Cause: both men carry the same red-and-white checked towel. The prompts said "towel round his NECK" for Raghavan and "towel tied on his HEAD" for Babu, but the model treats one pattern as one prop and puts it in one place, on the head. Sleeping poses make it worse: a towel over the face is a common sleeping image.

Rule:
1. When two people share an accessory pattern, write each one's placement with something visible next to it: Raghavan "towel draped round his neck and down his chest, his grey hair uncovered"; Babu "towel tied round his head".
2. A sleeping Raghavan lies with his face and grey hair uncovered; never "towel over his eyes".
3. Image QA and take QC check every towel's place for every man, every time.
