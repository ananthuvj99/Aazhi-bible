**L112 — A teacher's close-up with "teasing / playful" words and the class behind her was rejected by the filter** (3door-b1b v2.8)

Symptom: the platform rejected the prompt before rendering; nothing was made. The merged 3door-b1m with the same wording failed the same day.

Likely cause (exact trigger unknown): the class of children is in the background (@tuitionboardclass, "pupils in school uniform bent over their slates") while the text is full of flirt-coded mood words about the adult in frame: "teasing", "teasing him back", "playful", "a smile in her voice", "softly so the class does not hear", "a young woman", "arm's length", "natural skin". Children in the same prompt as these words adds up (L107, L93).

Rule (adds to L107):
1. With children anywhere in frame or in a reference, never use "tease/teasing", "playful/playfully", "flirt", "a smile in her voice", "so the class does not hear", "young woman", "arm's length", or any "skin" word (STYLE: "rich, true tones", not "skin tones"; drop "natural skin").
2. Write the mood as "light-hearted", "amused", "a small smile"; distances in metres; the children only as "the class at their desks".
3. If a prompt with children is refused, reword these first; then take out reference images one at a time.
