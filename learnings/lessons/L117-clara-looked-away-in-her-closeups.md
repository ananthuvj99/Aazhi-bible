**L117 — In single close-ups of a seated talk, Clara looked somewhere else instead of at Antony** (3tree-a v2.3)

Symptom: the cuts, voices and pronunciation were good, and Antony looked at Clara, but in Clara's close-ups her eyes wandered off while she asked her questions, so she did not seem to be talking to him.

Cause: her close-ups said only "her eyes on Antony just to the right of the lens"; the listener is out of frame, so the model had no anchor for where he is, and her first beat ("looks from the bag to his face") started her eyes away from him.

Rule:
1. In every single close-up, add an EYELINE line: where the other person sits ("just off the right edge of the frame"), the camera "right beside the listener's head", the speaker's head turned a little toward them, eyes "straight into his eyes, about 15 degrees right of the lens", and what the eyes never do ("not left, not down, not out to the sea, never into the lens").
2. Start each close-up with the speaker already looking at the listener; put any glance away (at a prop) in the wide shot, not in the close-up.
3. Repeat it in PERFORMANCE and POSITIVE LOCKS: "Clara always looking at Antony, Antony always looking at Clara."
