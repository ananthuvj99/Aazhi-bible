**L140: After "ആശാനേ, അവിടെ നോക്ക്! ഒരാൾ വെള്ളത്തിൽ!" the next shot jumped him to the rail, and standing to sitting happened in a blink** (review of the generated cut, 2026-11-11)

Symptom: Right after Antony calls out that someone is in the water, the next shot already has him standing at the edge of the boat; a change from standing to sitting also happens too abruptly. The movement feels sudden and unnatural.

Cause: Each shot was written from its own story beat. The next shot's start pose was not taken from the last pose of the shot before, and the change of pose was written as one word ("he stands", "he sits") with no time given to it.

Rule:
1. The start pose of every shot equals the end pose of the shot before (where he stands, standing or sitting, which hand on the rail). Write it in CONTINUITY and FIRST FRAME (L85, L118).
2. If the pose has to change, the change is shown on screen as its own beat of 1.5–2 s, with weight: "he pushes up from the deck with his right hand on the rail, gets his feet under him, rises, takes two quick steps to the rail".
3. If the change happens off screen, plan a cutaway between the two shots (another face, the fire) so the jump is covered, and note it in the edit plan.
