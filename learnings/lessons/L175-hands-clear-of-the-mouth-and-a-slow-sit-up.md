Symptom: 1B-3 v8.2: Antony kicked his legs up and swung off the cot in a rush; a hand came up to his nose and mouth right before the line; he spoke half-standing and side-on; the checked mundu read as grey trousers; small figures appeared outside the window.
Cause: too many actions packed before the line (sit up, swing, head in hands, mundu knot, stand) with hand business near the face; the stand-up at the end pulled him out of the MCU; "mundu" alone was read as trousers; the window view had no "nobody outside" lock.
Rule: before a line, the body action is slow and simple (a sit-up over 3 s, legs off the cot one at a time, no kicking); the hands finish all face business at least 1.5 s before the line and rest below the chest (on the knees or in the lap) for the whole line, with nothing between the mouth and the lens; the speaker stays seated or standing still, never rising during or right after the line; a mundu is written as a cotton cloth wrapped at the waist like a long skirt, never trousers; a window view says nobody is outside.
Checklist line: Hands clear of the mouth (L175): the hands leave the face at least 1.5 s before any line and rest below the chest through it; pre-line action is slow and simple; the speaker does not rise during or right after the line; the mundu is a wrapped skirt-cloth, never trousers; window views lock "nobody outside".
