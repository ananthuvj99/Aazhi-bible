**L150: In a POV shot with nobody in frame, a "hand rising with a torch" became the viewer's own hand by the camera, and the far boat came close** (s23-sh03 v4)

Symptom: the prompt was Antony's view over the rail, with a hand and torch rising beside the burning vallam 500 m away. The take shows a hand in Antony's striped sleeve, holding a burning torch, reaching in from beside the camera over the uru's rail. The burning vallam is big and close, the palette brown-dark, and none of the crew are seen. Ranjith wanted the view from the uru with the crew in it.

Cause: in a frame with no people, the only hand the model can place is the viewer's. Attaching @selvan's sleeve and writing "a hand rises" made the hand the subject, so the model brought it close. A 4° lens and "a third of the frame wide" also pulled the boat close.

Rule:
1. Nothing far away is described as a body part. At 500 m a man in the water is only what the eye would see, such as a small torch light; his hand or face is shown only in a shot near him.
2. Show who is watching. A view toward something far keeps the watchers in the foreground (backs or profiles at the rail), so the distance reads and nothing is handed to the camera.
3. Lock it in POSITIVE LOCKS: "no hand, arm or prop near the camera; every hand in the foreground stays on the rail".
4. 500 m is 500 m in every shot, and the boat is still clearly a boat. Get its size from the lens (about 12° makes it about a tenth of the frame), not from moving it closer.
