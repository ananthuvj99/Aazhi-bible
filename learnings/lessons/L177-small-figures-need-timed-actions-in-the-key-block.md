Symptom: Raghavan and Babu stood like statues at the uru's top rail through the whole take, even though the prompt said they grip the rail, cling through the heel and look back (Ranjith: "raghavan and babu stands like statue"). It happened before in s20-sh01 too (L83).
Cause: they are small figures far from the camera; actions written only inside the long timeline, as small verbs ("grip", "look back"), are dropped for distant people while the big action (the uru's turn, the wave) takes the model's attention.
Rule: when named people are small in the frame, give them big, whole-body actions (point with the whole arm, wave a towel overhead, run along the rail, jump, punch the air) with exact times, write them inside the "(most important)" block as well as the timeline, and say "never standing still, moving every second". If they still matter, frame them larger for that beat.
Checklist line: Do small or distant named people have big whole-body actions with times inside the most-important block, not only small verbs in the timeline?
