**L133 — The flare arced and bent over instead of bursting high; the old fishing boat came out brand new; the men looked like they were staring** (s16b-sh23 v2, failed)

Symptom: our signal flare left the pistol and curved over like a thrown object instead of climbing and bursting in the sky; the far fishing boat had a clean, freshly painted finish; the men stood still and stared instead of doing what people do to be seen.

Cause: "climbs" and "streaks up" did not fix the path; nothing said the boat was old; "watching" and "turn to it" gave no body actions; too many people and two flares split the model's attention.

Rule:
1. A rising object gets its path in plain words: "rises STRAIGHT UP, vertically, without curving or bending; at the top of its climb, high above, it bursts".
2. Anything that must look old is written old in detail: faded, peeling paint, rust streaks, stains, patches.
3. People reacting get actions, never a state: point, grab a neighbour's arm, lean out, jump, cup the hands and call, wave in big arcs; "never staring or frozen".
4. A shot that keeps failing gets simpler: fewer people, one effect instead of two, and a close reaction cut to carry the emotion.
5. A STRICT REALISM block when the creator asks for it.
