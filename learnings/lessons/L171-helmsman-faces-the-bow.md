Symptom: in the wheelhouse, Aashaan came out standing between the wheel and the front windows, facing aft, with the bow behind him: the wheel and the helmsman were turned the wrong way.
Cause: the start frame looked forward at the front windows; to show the speaker's face the model kept that background and turned the man and the wheel round. A helmsman's face toward the camera and the bow behind him cannot both be true.
Rule: the helmsman always stands behind (aft of) the wheel and faces the bow. To show his face, the camera stands between the wheel and the front windows, looking aft: the back wall and the side door are behind him, the front windows and the bow are behind the camera and never visible behind him. If the shot starts on a forward-looking frame, the reverse angle needs its own start frame (shot/reverse-shot), not a camera swing.
Checklist line: helm shots: helmsman aft of the wheel facing the bow; a face shot of him has the back wall behind him and no front windows; never the bow behind a helmsman facing the camera.
