**L81 — A prop written "at the bottom edge" of one close-up was drawn in front of the lens in every cut, held by nobody** (s16b-sh03 v4) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh03-v4-raghavan-plate-at-bottom-edge.png`, `…-sasi-plate-at-bottom-edge.webp`; how it should look: `scenes/showreel/approved/s16b-sh02-v4-plate-hold.webp`

Symptom: in the POV face close-ups the steel plate sat at the bottom of the frame in every cut, as if the viewer were holding it, even in Sasi's cut; in Raghavan's own cut he was not holding it at all (one hand up, the plate floating in the foreground).

Cause: (1) The reference line said "its rim just at the bottom edge of his close-up" and was not limited to one cut, so the model put the plate at the bottom edge of every cut. (2) In POV cuts the model treats the bottom of the frame as the viewer's hands, so a prop "at the bottom edge" became the viewer's prop. (3) How Raghavan holds the plate was never written; only "in his left hand".

Rule:
1. Limit a prop's reference line to the cut it is in ("cut 1 only") and say the other cuts have no prop.
2. Never place a prop "at the frame edge"; frame wide enough to show the whole prop and the hand holding it, and describe the hold exactly (which hand, where on the body, which way it faces, how the other hand uses it), copied from the approved shot.
3. In POV cuts write "nothing between the lens and the man; the watcher's hands and body are never in frame".
