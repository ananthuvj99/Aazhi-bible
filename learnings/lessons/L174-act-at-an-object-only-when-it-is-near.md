Symptom: the five vallams sat on the horizon in the start frame and stayed on the horizon for the whole take, never getting nearer; the snags happened right behind the uru with a net that seemed to trail from the uru's own stern, so the uru looked as if it was dragging a net to catch its pursuers.
Cause: far objects at the horizon are treated as fixed background and keep their distance; the model will not close 50–60 m of sea in a few seconds. With the nets far away, it invented a net where the action had to happen, next to the uru, and "float lines reaching toward the uru's course" read as a line joined to the uru.
Rule: when an action must happen at an object (a net, a boat, a rock), that object is already close in the start frame, within about two seconds of travel of the people who meet it. Make or edit the start image so it is there. State whom a net belongs to: each net is tied to its own vallam, lies across the water between the vallams, and nothing trails from the uru.
Checklist line: Is everything the action meets already close in the start frame (about two seconds of travel)? Is it stated that the nets belong to the vallams and nothing trails from the uru?
