**L162: A person who points looks where he points**

What happened: Antony's left arm pointed straight out to the right at the burning boat, but his head was turned the other way, toward Babu. Ranjith: "his hand goes straight and his head turns left toward the burning boat, right?" The start frame had him looking at Babu while pointing.

Rule: when someone points at something, his head turns toward it and his eyes run along his arm: gaze, fingertip and target on one line, in the start frame and in EYELINES ("looks along his own pointing arm at ..."). He may glance at the person he shows it to only before he raises the arm or after he lowers it.
