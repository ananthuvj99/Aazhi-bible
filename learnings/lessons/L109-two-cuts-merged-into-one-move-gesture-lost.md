**L109 — The two cuts became one continuous push; the close-up and Clara's gesture never happened** (3tease v4.1) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/3tease-v41-no-cut-profile-no-gesture.jpg`

Symptom: 12 s, no cut. The camera, meant to be locked off in CUT 1, travelled up the aisle the whole time and ended in a side two-shot at the door: Clara in profile facing Antony, both in frame. No reverse close-up from the doorway, and no eyebrow raise or hand "what?" — she only stood and looked.

Cause: the platform merged the two cuts into one camera move (L37, L50 again): it bridged CUT 1's wide to CUT 2's framing by travelling, so the camera position of CUT 2 (outside the door, facing Clara) was never reached. Clara stayed in profile, so her face gesture could not read, and the time went on travelling instead of the beat. "Antony out of frame" lost to the continuous move, which kept him in.

Fix: a beat that needs a different camera position — especially a reverse angle or a close-up with a specific face or hand gesture — is its own generation (5–8 s), started from its own start frame showing that framing. Keep the gesture as the single action of that short shot. What worked here (pupils writing with their backs to the view, Antony staying at the door) stays in the wide.
