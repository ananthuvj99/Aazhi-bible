**L174: the target landed in the wrong man's line of fire** (s24-sh11 v3)

Symptom: Selvan fires at the patrol boat; the diesel can stood between him and the boat, so his own shots seemed to set it on fire.
Cause: "beside him, bullets hit the can" did not say where the can is relative to the two guns; the model put it in front of his barrel.
Fix: in any exchange of fire, place every object relative to both shooters ("at the far back end of the boat, BEHIND him, on the side away from the enemy; never between him and the enemy"), keep it out of the shot where he fires ("only open water and the enemy in front of his barrel"), and show the hit in its own shot with the bullets visibly coming from the enemy's side (the searchlight).
