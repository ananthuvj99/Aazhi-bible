Symptom: The take looked good overall, but Adwaithan put the tea glass down and slid it toward Antony far too fast, a rushed, mechanical hand move.
Cause: The prompt packed three hand actions (jug down, glass placed, glass slid 20 cm) into one second (2.0–3.0 s). The model played all three at that speed.
Rule: Every small hand action with an object gets its own beat of about 1–1.5 s: place it gently, let it settle, then move it slowly and smoothly, the hand relaxed. If the beats don't fit before the line, lengthen the clip (8 → 12 s) rather than squeeze them.
