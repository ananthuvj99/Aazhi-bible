**L160: Name the hand from the start image, and make it make sense**

What happened: the start image shows Antony pointing with his LEFT arm, but the prompt said "right arm". Ranjith: "look at the left hand which is pointing... does it make sense?"

Rule: before writing any hand, look at the start image and name the hand it shows (left/right from the person's own body: a person facing the camera has his left arm on the right of the frame). Give each hand a job that fits the blocking: here Babu is on Antony's right, so his RIGHT hand grips Babu's shoulder and his LEFT arm points out to sea. Carry the same hands into every later shot of the beat and lock them in CONSTRAINTS ("points with his LEFT arm only").
