**L160: Name the hand from the start image, and make it make sense**

What happened: the start image shows Antony pointing with his LEFT arm, but the prompt said "right arm". Ranjith: "look at the left hand which is pointing... does it make sense?"

Rule: before writing any hand, look at the start image and name the hand it shows (left/right from the person's own body: a person facing the camera has his left arm on the right of the frame). Give each hand a job that fits the blocking: here Babu is on Antony's right, so his RIGHT hand grips Babu's shoulder and his LEFT arm points out to sea. How to read it: if you can see the shirt front or the face, the person faces the camera and his left arm is on the RIGHT of the frame; if you see his back, his left arm is on the LEFT. My QC of @s23sh02estart got this wrong (the shirt front was visible, so the pointing arm was his right); Ranjith caught it. Carry the same hands into every later shot of the beat and lock them in CONSTRAINTS ("points with his LEFT arm only").
