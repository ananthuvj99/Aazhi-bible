**L90 — The front door closed itself: a door state said once loses to the house sheet** (2A-3 v2.2)

Symptom: in the wide front-of-house shot the blue front door was closed, though the shot before showed it open with Rosamma in the doorway. The line also sounded artificial.

Cause: "the open blue front door" appeared only inside the @housefullview reference line; the sheet's own door state won. The delivery note asked for "loud enough to carry", which pushed the line into a performed, announcing voice.

Rule: a door, window or gate state that must match the previous shot is written in the reference line, LOCATION, FIRST FRAME, every ACTION beat and POSITIVE LOCKS ("the blue double front door stands wide open, both leaves back, the dim passage visible"). For family dialogue, write the voice as ordinary speech at that distance (W6), never "loud", "carry" or "announce".

Checklist line: door/window/gate states carried from the previous shot are stated in reference, location, first frame, every beat and locks; family lines are written as ordinary speech, not projected.
