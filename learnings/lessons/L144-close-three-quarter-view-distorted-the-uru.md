**L144 — A close, low side view drew the uru short and stubby with a towering bow, standing still; the dolphin "transition" never covered the frame** (s16b-sh31 v1, partial)

Symptom: from one metre above the water, the uru came out as a short, deep boat with an enormous curved bow taking a third of the frame, a tiny deckhouse, the man standing near the bow; the boat did not move; the dolphin crossed the lower part of the frame without filling it, so there was no transition, and the time of day never changed.

Cause: "about 12 m off, level with the middle" with no proportions let the model make its own boat; a close camera near the water exaggerates whatever is nearest (the bow); "glides past" and "travelling alongside" gave no visible motion cue; the transition was only implied by "the frame goes dark", and the creator's idea (night before, morning after) was not in the prompt.

Rule:
1. For the whole boat in frame, give its proportions in metres (length, deck height above the water, bow height, the deckhouse's share of the length, the mast position) and ask for "a true side profile, broadside on, natural lens, about 30 m away".
2. Give motion a visible cue: the bow wave, the wake, the water sliding past underneath.
3. A transition is written as a hidden cut: "at 5 s the dolphin's body fills the whole frame; hidden behind it, a hard cut to part 2", with what changes on each side (night before, morning after).
