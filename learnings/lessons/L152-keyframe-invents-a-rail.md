**L152: A close-up keyframe invented a different rail (raw brown slatted planks), my QC passed it as "minor", and the take copied it** (s23-sh06 v11)

Symptom: in the s23-sh06 take, the rail behind Antony is raw brown planks with open slats, like a fence. In every approved take of this deck, the uru's rail amidships is a solid grey, weathered timber bulwark with iron bolt heads and a broad flat top.

Cause: the close-up keyframe was generated from the character sheet and a far view of the fire, with no reference for the rail. QC noticed the difference and called it minor, because "the reference image carries the rail". The video model copied the keyframe instead. The prompt also described the rail wrongly ("turned wooden posts").

Rule:
1. Every surface in a keyframe (rail, deck, wall, door) is checked against the approved takes of that place. A surface that does not match fails QC and is fixed before delivery, never marked "minor".
2. Any keyframe that shows part of the set is made with an approved frame of that set attached as a reference, even in a close-up.
3. The MEMORY Place line describes each surface from the approved takes, not from memory: here, "a solid grey weathered timber bulwark with iron bolt heads and a broad flat top".
