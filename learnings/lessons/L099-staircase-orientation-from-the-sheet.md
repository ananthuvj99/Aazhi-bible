**L99 — The deckhouse staircase came out along the side of the deckhouse instead of running forward** (s16b-sh05 v10) — evidence: `scenes/showreel/evidence/s16b-sh05-v10-side-stairs.png`; the sheet: `scenes/showreel/approved/uru-stairs-run-forward.png`

Symptom: in the `@uru` sheet the staircase runs from the deckhouse roof straight down toward the bow (fore and aft). In the take, Antony was beside the deckhouse's side wall with the stairs at the left edge, walking out along the side deck.

Cause: the prompt said only "the short staircase from the deckhouse roof", with no orientation. The model picked a side stair.

Rule: describe fixed structures with their orientation from the sheet ("the staircase runs straight down from the front edge of the deckhouse roof toward the bow; going down it he faces the mast and the bow") and, for a start on stairs, make a start still from the sheet first (L61).

Checklist line: item 116 in `rules/pre-delivery-checklist.md`.
