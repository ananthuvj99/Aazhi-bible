## The uru at night
- Hull: grey-blue painted planks, heavily weathered, long rust-brown streaks, red-oxide below the waterline; a high pointed stem (rc-001.0s, rc-069.0s).
- Bow: the closed blue triangular bow deck, red-brown bitts in pairs, big coils of coir rope (rc-062.0s, rc-036.0s), as on @urubowplate.
- Rails: red-brown wooden rails on turned posts (balusters) around the bow and along the deck (rc-008.0s, rc-018.0s).
- Mast: one tall, rust-brown weathered mast with a boom and rigging, standing on the forward deck; rope wound round its foot (rc-018.0s, rc-064.0s, rc-069.0s).
- Deckhouse: grey weathered timber, two levels, a balustraded roof deck; rust-streaked posts at its front corners where Antony sits (rc-056.5s, rc-060.0s).
- Deck: brown wooden planks, warm where lantern light falls.

## Light (the most important difference from our night)
- **Low-key and deep:** a very dark navy sky with stars and the Milky Way (rc-064.0s); a dark sea; the moon makes a bright silver path on the water but the deck is mostly in shadow. Not the bright "readable blue" of our full-moon takes.
- **Warm practical lights carry the faces:** a caged glass lantern on the rail top (Aashaan, rc-008.0s); a hurricane lantern on a round brass tray at the mast foot, with the crew in a circle round it (rc-018.0s, rc-036.0s); a bare bulb in the deckhouse over a Divine Mercy picture (Aashaan praying, rc-047.0s); a lantern beside a rope coil by the deckhouse (rc-060.0s). Warm, about 2000-2800K, against the cold blue night.
- **Moon:** a round moon in most shots (rc-001.0s high behind the uru seen from ahead; rc-018.0s low on the right horizon, looking forward over the bow). One shot has a crescent (rc-004.0s): treat that as an exception. Ahead and to the right of the bow, as in our sheet (L142).

## People at night
- **Antony:** pale blue-grey and white vertical-striped short-sleeve shirt, thin chain; keeps watch sitting at the front of the deckhouse, forearms on the rail, looking out (rc-056.5s, rc-060.0s); also standing on the deck looking at the sea (rc-004.0s).
- **Aashaan:** cream shirt over a white vest, white towel on his left shoulder; leans on the rail by the caged lantern (rc-008.0s); prays in the deckhouse under the bulb (rc-047.0s); at the wheel inside, chin on his hand, the windows showing the bow and the sleepers (sheet, 66 s).
- **Balan:** a grey-blue shirt in this light; sleeps inside the deckhouse (rc-006.5s); watches the crew from the deckhouse (rc-016.0s); smokes at the deckhouse side rail (rc-054.0s).
- **The crew:** eat rice and fried fish on banana leaves in a circle round the mast lantern (rc-036.0s, rc-038.0s); later **sleep on the forward deck between the mast and the bow, wrapped in white sheets, about seven men**, among the bitts and rope coils (sheet 64 s, rc-066.0s aerial; sheet 60 s three men on the bow deck with a lantern).

## What differs from our SB027-SB032 night (to decide before the reshoot)
1. Light: theirs is low-key with warm practicals; ours is a bright, readable blue full moon.
2. Sleepers: theirs on the forward deck under white sheets (about seven); ours three men on mats on the main deck in front of the deckhouse.
3. Antony's shirt: theirs pale blue-grey and white stripes; ours grey-beige stripes.
4. Lanterns: theirs on the rail top and at the mast foot; our L-rule said "no lantern on the rails".
5. Antony's watch: theirs at the deckhouse front and on deck; ours at the bow by the stem.
6. Balan's shirt: theirs reads grey-blue at night; his sheet is light blue.

## What to learn for prompts (from the cut, with W25)
- Short shots, many close-ups of hands and faces (rice on a banana leaf, a hand on a fried fish), each with one warm practical light in frame.
- Every shot has a motivated light source you can see (lantern, bulb).
- Aerials and wide shots keep the uru small in a dark sea with its wake; the mast and rigging give scale.
