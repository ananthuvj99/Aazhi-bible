# MEMORY — SB042, the hill above the coast (Jeena Johnachan, 2026-11-25)

Goes into every SB042 prompt right after SCENE CONTEXT, with the person lines of whoever is in frame.

```text
Place: the rounded grassy top of a low laterite hill above the Alappad coast, 1998: short gold-green grass, red laterite earth and loose red stones, one cashew tree; below, coconut palm tops, a pale sand strip and the Arabian Sea at the horizon to the west.
Light: late afternoon, the sun about 30 degrees up over the sea, about 4000K: warm rims on hair and shoulders, soft sky light on the faces.
Clara: about 20, sky-blue cotton saree with a thin white border, the pallu over her LEFT shoulder, one long thick plait down her back, a small gold cross on a thin chain, wooden-bead bangles on her right wrist, thin gold bangles on her left.
Antony: 24, short-sleeve shirt with thin lavender-grey and white stripes, green-grey checked mundu, thin silver chain, light stubble, no sandals.
```

Positions: Antony on the inland (east) side, Clara on the sea (west) side; Clara looks just LEFT of the lens in her singles, Antony just RIGHT of the lens in his. SB043 (Jaison and Balan watching from the foot of the slope) sees this crest, the cashew tree and the couple from about 120 m below.
