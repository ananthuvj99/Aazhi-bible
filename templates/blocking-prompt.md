# Prompt template — shot generated from a 3D blocking video (W19)

Use when a shot is driven by a grey-box playblast attached as the video/motion reference. The video carries camera, timing and positions; the text carries identity, style, faces and sound. Keep it short (about 5,000 characters).

```
MOTION REFERENCE
The attached video is a grey-box blocking of this exact shot. Follow it exactly for the camera path, framing, timing, <key structure, e.g. the staircase> and every person's position and movement. Replace the simple shapes with the real people and the real <place> described below. The stand-ins: <colour/shape> = <person>; <colour/shape> = <person>; …

SCENE CONTEXT
<time, year, place>. One continuous shot, no cut. <what happens, in story words, 3–4 sentences>. Nobody speaks / <who speaks>.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@<id>: <age, the ONLY one who… marker, costume as worn, prop and hand>. 100% matches the reference.
… (one line per sheet; the location/vessel line describes the structures with their orientation from its sheet)

CAMERA
<time range>: <hand-held, walking behind…, natural sway, a little behind his pace>.
<time range>: <smooth, fluid, gliding … at one constant speed; even and continuous, no jerks, no pauses, no change of speed>.
(No equipment words, L46.)

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, <N> seconds, real time, exactly following the attached video. No cut.

<ACTION STYLE, e.g. DANCE>
<the style in words: how these people really move; tempo in numbers; small human-sized moves; faces never to the lens>.

PERFORMANCE
<faces and reactions the boxes cannot show, with timing: e.g. as the camera passes in front of him his face slowly opens into a warm smile>. Exactly <N> people, each appearing once.

LIGHTING
<sources with Kelvin and direction>.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No singing, no words, no riser or swell. <timed sounds>.

STYLE
Photoreal 1998 Kerala period feature film. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: wide dynamic range, clean low-noise shadows, soft highlight roll-off, natural colour and true skin tones, gentle filmic contrast, fine organic texture. Nothing of the grey-box look remains.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Camera path and timing exactly as the attached video; <smoothness lock>. <identity markers in one line each>. Exactly <N> people, each once, each a different face. <place locks>. Runs the full <N> seconds.
```

Attach: the playblast as the video reference + every sheet named above; remove every other attachment (L88).
Blocking rules: L96 (human-sized moves), L97 (open action space), L99 (orientation from the sheet), L105 (human camera); show the playblast to the creator first; only the approved final goes in the repo.
