# The burning vallam: Selvan's fishing boat on fire (SB029–SB033)
Platform ID: `@burningvallam` · Image: `prop.png` (Kling Omni Image, 2k, 2026-11-07; chosen by Ranjith over a GPT version)

## What it looks like
- A small traditional Kerala wooden fishing vallam, about 7 m: long, low and narrow, flat sides, low pointed ends with no raised or carved prow, no sail, no mast, no cabin.
- Weathered white hull with a faded blue band along the side, dark tarred wood showing through.
- Inside: heaped fishing nets and long wooden oars/poles lying across the hull.
- Burning along its length, strongest at the right-hand end in the image: flames about two metres high there, a column of dark smoke lit orange from below, sparks, a long orange reflection on the water.
- No people on it, ever, in any shot. The storyboard never shows what happened to it.

## Not part of the prop
- The moon in the image (a crescent) is NOT part of the prop: the night sky follows the scene's plates (half moon high on the RIGHT looking forward from the bow).
- The flames sit in an even line along the gunwale in the image; write "ragged, uneven flames" in prompts.

## Where it is
- Off the uru's bow, ahead and to the LEFT of the stem post looking forward (SB028 light, SB029 boat), about half a kilometre away when first seen.
- SB030 a hand rises in the water near it; SB031 the crew at the rail; SB032 Antony jumps; SB033 he brings Selvan back to the uru. The fire can burn lower as the scenes go on, never out, until the rescue ends.

## When to attach
- Every shot where the burning boat is on screen, SB029 to SB033.
- Far shots: lock its size ("small and far on the horizon at the left, about half a kilometre off") so it is not pulled closer.
- Close rescue shots (SB032–SB033): full size, burning lower, a few metres from Antony and Selvan in the water.
