# The hero uru — Antony's cargo vessel (1998)
Platform ID: `@uru` · Image: `prop.png` (cropped from panel 13B "Approach" of Ranjith's harbour sheet v2; 467 × 329 px, the largest copy available)
Tracker note: the location tracker calls the hero uru `@Uru2`. This is the vessel Ranjith chose on 2026-11-06 (the one in panels 13B Approach and 14 Boarding); if a different `@Uru2` image exists on the platform, tell Ananthu before using it.

## What it looks like
- A Beypore uru (wooden dhow), never a fishing boat (`rules/prompt-rules.md`).
- High, sharply curved wooden bow with a tall stem post; iron fastenings and rust streaks down the planks.
- Weathered blue-grey planked hull, red-oxide paint below the waterline, scuffed and patched.
- One tall wooden mast with rope rigging; heavy coir mooring rope from the bow to the quay.
- Aft: a carved wooden deckhouse with two railed decks (turned balusters), in warm brown wood; coils of coir rope and cargo on deck.
- Length roughly 25–30 m; the deckhouse top stands well above a man on the quay.
- The stern is painted "COCHIN" in white capital letters (Ranjith, 2026-11-07, as in panel 15). Write it exactly in shots that see the stern; no other lettering anywhere on the boat.

## The wheel
- Steered from a big spoked wooden wheel INSIDE the lower level of the deckhouse, behind the front windows; the helmsman looks out over the deck and bow to the sea (Ranjith, 2026-11-07). A hurricane lantern hangs from the ceiling beam there at night; a brass compass sits on a shelf. Never a wheel on the open deck.

## Where it lives
- Moored at the far end of the stone quay in the Cochin harbour (`@harbourc1plate` shows it small, bow toward the view; `@harbourc2plate` shows it close from the quay).
- Antony walks to it (SB019, SB020), boards it by a gangplank (SB021) and sails on it (SB022 on).

## When to attach
- Every shot where this uru is on screen. When it is small in the distance, lock its size in the prompt ("small, at the far end of the quay") so it is not pulled closer.
