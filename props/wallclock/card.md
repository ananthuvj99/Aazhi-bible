# Wall clock — Antony's room
Platform ID: `@wallclock` · Sheet: `sheet.png` (front, three-quarter, side, dial and bezel details)

## Look (from the sheet)
- Old round wall clock, thick black-painted wooden bezel, worn and chipped with a crack at the lower left, brown wood showing through.
- Aged cream dial with water stains; bold black numerals 1–12; the word "SCIENTIFIC" printed above the centre; two small winding holes below the centre.
- Black spade-shaped hour hand and long thin minute hand on a small brass centre boss.
- A small brass hinge clip on the right side of the bezel; a small blue enamel maker's plate on the bezel below the 6; a wooden pendulum box below the clock.

## Placement
- Hangs on the **front wall of Antony's room, next to the front window, near the outer corner** — above the **foot** of the cot. (Marked by Ananthu: `locations/antony-room/clock-placement-marked.png`.)
- Lying on the cot he sees it by lifting his head off the pillow and looking down past his feet.

## Time rule
- The sheet shows the hands at about 2:15. Always state the hands explicitly in the prompt (L3) so the model does not copy the sheet's time.
- Showreel Scene 1: a quarter to eight (short hand just before the 8, long hand on the 9).
