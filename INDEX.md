# INDEX — every asset in the AAZHI bible

Read this first. One row per asset, from every creator. A new asset gets its folder, its `card.md` and its row here in the same commit.

Status: `approved` · `waiting for approval` · `retired`

**How to attach.** IDs are exactly as registered on the platform: the sheet name with no spaces, hyphens or underscores. Mode **full** means the action happens there; **background-only** means it is seen through a door or window or beyond, written as "(shot N only, background only) … the camera never goes there". Labelled (mapped) sheets carry printed words that can end up in the video, so never attach them. House sheets go only on shots where the camera is outside looking at the house (L35). What each camera position sees behind and through doors is in each location's `cameras.md`.
