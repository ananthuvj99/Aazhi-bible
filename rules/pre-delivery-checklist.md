# Pre-delivery checklist — run on every prompt before it is handed over

Built from lessons L1–L26. Every item must pass; show the result table with the prompt.

## Who and where
1. **Entrances:** every character who enters is shown IN their origin room in the first frame (visible through a doorway) and walks out of it. Name the doors that stay empty. (L26)
2. **Exits:** state the exact door/route and screen side. (L16, L26)
3. **Speaker direction:** the camera stands where the speaker's face already points (a door, a person, a task). Eyes may be on the task; face within 30° of the lens. (L16, L25)
4. **One speaker per shot;** everyone else's mouth closed. While a line is spoken, only the speaker's face is in frame: clean singles, never over-the-shoulder; the listener gets a separate silent shot. (L5, L34)

## What they look like
5. **Wardrobe** block lists every item, plus what is NOT worn. (L20)
6. **Footwear locked** whenever the sheet differs from the scene (Rosamma barefoot indoors). (L26)
7. **Hair state** carried over (messy until he bathes/changes). 
8. **Mood carry-over:** the character's face at the start matches how they left the previous shot. (1D v2)

## The set
9. **Props only from cards or approved shots;** exact counts; who holds what in which hand; where it ends up. (L1, L17)
10. **No material names that are objects** ("coconut-shell spoon" → coconut). (L1)
11. **Wall-by-wall:** which wall holds each prop AND which walls don't. (L2, L23)
12. **Furniture orientation per camera position** ("runs sideways across the frame, long side to camera, head at screen-right"). (L22)
13. **Bedding/dressing continuity** from the approved generation, not from older prompts. (L20)
14. **Clock/time:** each hand spelled out. (L3)
15. **Barred windows / roofs:** no camera passes through; cut instead. (L4, L12)

## Camera, light, sound
16. **Lines in MCU** (face ≥ 1/3 of frame height); wide shots for blocking only. (L21)
17. **Light direction checked per cut:** a bright door/window behind the subject is a rim light, not the key; speaking faces get soft frontal light. (1D v2)
18. **Sunrise inland, sunset over the sea.** (L11)
19. **Holds:** 2–3 s still at the start and after the last line/action. (L14)
20. **Sound effects only, no music.**
21. **Dialogue:** Malayalam script only; one scene emotion from the platform's list; never inside a line; no line crosses a cut; every word on camera. (L7, L8, L24)

## References
22. **Tags exactly as registered** (no underscores: @hallclean). (L19)
23. Tag what is on screen AND the location the camera stands in when its walls frame the shot. Never tag what isn't there. 
24. **Clean sheets only,** never mapped. 

## Scope (L27)
25. **Nothing off-screen in the prompt:** no rooms, props or people the camera does not see; no global house layout; continuity back-story stays in the ledger.
26. **Location line per cut**, saying where it is and where it is NOT.
27. **References assigned to cuts;** a reference used in one cut is named with "cut N only".
28. **One location per generation when there is dialogue;** split at location changes.
29. **Room contents:** "this room contains only: …" for every room on screen.
30. **Word scan:** search the prompt for every off-screen prop name, including hidden ones ("o'clock" contains "clock"). Describe time by light, never by clock words, except in the clock insert.
31. **Background-only rooms** (seen through a doorway): leave untagged by default; if tagged, mark "(cut N only, background only) … the camera never enters it; all action stays in <room>".
32. **Language lock (L28):** SPOKEN LANGUAGE line under SCENE; "speaks only Malayalam" in each speaker's reference; "in Malayalam" on every speaking ACTION line; Malayalam-only AUDIO header; lock in POSITIVE LOCKS.
33. **No English paraphrase of any line (L29):** SCENE/ACTION say only "says one Malayalam line"; no translations or summaries of what is said anywhere in the prompt.
34. **Pacing (L30):** ≤ 3 shots per 15 s; "runs the full N seconds"; unhurried actions with breathing room; listener keeps doing their task.
35. **Start positions (L31):** characters begin where the dialogue happens; no cross-room travel in a dialogue shot.
36. **Reverse-angle background (L32):** name what is directly behind each person in frame from the house map; tag it background-only.
37. **Reference-ID audit:** derive needed IDs per shot from `INDEX.md` + the location's `cameras.md` sight-lines (incl. backgrounds behind people and through doors); add missing, remove wrong, exact spelling, scope + mode on each.
38. **Emotion (L33, updated):** one emotion tag for the whole scene, before the first line; none on other lines or ACTION beats; pauses as timing.
39. **Clean singles (L34):** while a line is spoken, nobody else's face is in frame.
40. **Exterior sheets only for looking AT the house (L35);** never when the camera looks out from inside.
41. **Eyeline (L36):** listener off-screen beside the lens in the speaker's eyeline; nobody behind the speaker; EYELINE block written.
42. **One shot per generation (L37)** unless the user asks for multi-shot; 5–8 s each.
43. **No background voices during dialogue:** ambient in dialogue shots has no murmuring/talking crowds — use object sounds.
44. **Silent reactions closed-lip:** laughs and smiles in non-speaking shots are closed-lip ("breath of a laugh through the nose").
45. **QA runs on the FINAL version** of every prompt, after all edits.
46. **Interior through windows/doors (L38)** matches the moment.
47. **Extras roster + gaze (L39).**
48. **Blank boards/papers/signs (L40).**
49. **ROOM LAYOUT block + positions relative to the character; list surface contents exactly (L41).**
50. **One camera axis per room (L42);** no reverse room angles for doorway views.
51. **Crowds (L43):** few extras, from behind, camera above heads, empty seats stated.
52. **Re-check L37 before sending:** one set-up per generation when geography or crowds matter.
53. **Natural DOF (L44):** no shallow DOF/portrait blur in dialogue singles; backgrounds readable.
54. **Off-lens eyeline (L45):** eyes ~20° off the lens on the other person; never into the lens.
55. **No equipment words (L46):** no tripod/rig/dolly/gimbal/mic; 'locked-off, perfectly steady view'; singles 'with X completely out of frame'; WHAT IS IN FRAME line.
56. **Audio line:** "No music at all — no score, no background music, no song" + a timed list of sound effects covering the whole clip.
57. **Bedding (L47):** slept-in, crumpled; no cover sheet in the bedding's pattern.
58. **Landmarks (L48):** count + one position for each; say what fills the other places.
59. **No sound design (L49):** no riser/whoosh/swell/transition sounds; ambience at a steady level.
60. **Body under cloth (L49):** name the parts the cloth rises over; a raised knee; feet as bumps; never flat.
61. **Every generation carries one registered location tag** (background-only if only a corner is seen) so the planner never invents a location (L19); close-ups never get a wide plate (L50).
62. **No duplicate props (L51):** don't attach a prop sheet for a prop the room sheet already shows.
63. **Start frames (L52):** a shot that continues another attaches its last frames as the start image.
64. **Plate = camera position (L53):** the attached plate must be shot from where this shot's camera stands; inside action never uses a from-outside plate.
65. **15 s maximum per generation (L58)**; dialogue scenes with two speakers are split into one generation per speaker.
66. **Natural acting (L54):** the speaker has a real task near the line (kept below the face) and a human beat before it; what they talk to is just off frame; "never looks into the camera at any moment".
67. **Off-screen person's direction (L55):** name which side of the screen they are on, and name the tempting wrong direction ("never toward the window").
68. **Calls to another room (L56, L57):** the speaker turns toward where the other person is; loudness is written in words ("loud enough to carry, with a smile in her voice") — no [shouts].
69. **Count the doorways (L59):** when a room has several doors, say how many and which one is meant; start camera moves from the right space.
70. **Continuity frames that show people (L60):** keep them in the shot on purpose with their new action stated, or don't attach the frame.
71. **New camera angle (L61):** make a still of the new angle first and use it as the start frame; never attach a frame from the old angle.
72. **Family dialogue (W6):** no emotion tag; describe ordinary speech ("normal voice at kitchen distance, the way people really talk at home, not dramatic").
73. **House plan (L57):** count the passage doors ("exactly two bedroom doors facing each other…"); @houseclean / @hallmapped are never attached.
74. **Camera (Sony Venice 2):** STYLE names "Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera" once, followed by the look line from `rules/prompt-rules.md`; nowhere else; WHAT IS IN FRAME keeps "nothing from the making of it".
75. **Plate furniture in the path (L63):** look at the attached plate; any furniture on the path or spot where the action happens is either wanted or named away: open path in the reference line, open floor with a size and "the only furniture" in the room block, and in POSITIVE LOCKS. A person in the plate standing elsewhere is placed in words.
76. **Props not on the sheet (L64):** a carried prop the character sheet lacks is described in full (size, colour, material, strap, shoulder, where it hangs) in the reference line, in every ACTION beat the character is in, and in POSITIVE LOCKS.
77. **Natural walk (L65):** every walk or step names heel to toe, weight shift, relaxed shoulders, arm swing or hand position, slight head bob and what clothes and carried things do; holds include breathing and a slight weight shift.
78. **Reacting extras (L66):** every extra who reacts has a look target and 'in profile, back of the head toward the view'; the ones who don't react keep a named task; the lead's look names who she looks at.
79. **Carry looks forward (L67):** for each character, read the ledger's carry-forward lines and the end frames of their previous scenes; every prop, costume, injury or state they gained is in the prompt (reference line, each beat, locks) and its registered ID is attached; a frame from another place is never attached for it.
80. **Screen direction to a goal (L68):** a walk toward a goal across shots keeps the goal ahead and the same things on the same sides; the plate is checked (mirrored or remade if it looks the other way); the sides are named in the prompt and in POSITIVE LOCKS.
81. **Reference images scored (aazhi-image-qa):** every image attached to a prompt that was made, cropped or mirrored from @harbourc4plate on (older ones when next touched) has a continuity score in its card (80 or more), and any drift it lists is written away in the prompt.
82. **Listener (W8):** in two-person dialogue the listener has a physical action around the line (not in the speaker's frame), mouth closed; consider a cut back for their reaction.
83. **Silent tail after the last line (L70):** a shot whose line ends the dialogue has its line end at least 2.5 s before the clip ends, a silent beat in ACTION, "after the line, nobody speaks" in AUDIO and the tail in POSITIVE LOCKS; mid-exchange shots need only a short beat.
84. **Duration on the platform (L71):** shots are 8 s (12 or 15 s if long), not odd lengths; the delivery's first setting, in bold, tells the creator to set the platform's duration to that length.
85. **Filter-safe wording (L72):** no 'barefoot/bare', 'sleeveless', 'grin', moving clothing, 'brush-off', 'off-face'; costume described as worn.
86. **Storyboard with every scene (Ranjith, 2026-11-07):** a scene's prompts are delivered with its storyboard sheet (one panel per shot, plate or start image, beats, timing, IDs), saved in `scenes/showreel/storyboards/`.
87. **Reference sweep (Ranjith, 2026-11-07):** every registered image matching the shot (vessel, deck/sea plates, characters, props, crew, earlier frames) is attached when in frame, or its absence is explained (L50, L51, L61); nothing that exists is left to be invented.
88. **Costume off the sheet + action in frame (L73):** when a scene changes what the sheet shows (bathing, sleeping, changing), say where the sheet item is and what is worn, in the reference line, every beat and POSITIVE LOCKS, filter-safe (L72); every action the creator asked for happens inside the frame with its object visible (never "below the frame").
89. **No line-up with its own members (L74):** a group/line-up sheet is never attached in the same generation as individual sheets of people who appear on it; cast only sheeted people, state the exact head count ('each appears once, each a different face'), give each one unique marker, and fill empty deck space with named objects.
90. **Intent line per spoken line (W13):** the prompt ends with one line per spoken line: When @<character> says "<exact Malayalam line>", let it be in a <tone> tone; the tone matches PERFORMANCE and is not an emotion tag.
91. **Dance tempo and every dancer moving (L75):** a dance has its tempo in numbers (bpm, steps per second, circle speed about 6 km/h, real time), every dancer has his own continuous moves and 'dances non-stop from the first frame to the last', and holds keep only the camera still, never the dancers.
92. **Layering and helmsmen (L76):** the outer garment is named first and the inner one only where it shows; a helmsman stands squarely behind the wheel, centred, facing forward, both hands on the spokes, framed from behind wide enough to show both hands and what he steers toward.
93. **3D blocking with the storyboard (Ranjith, 2026-11-07):** every scene's storyboard has a grey-box 3D blocking panel (tools/blocking3d.py) with each person, key prop and camera position; the prompts' positions match it.
94. **Fixed places in a group (L77):** every person in a group has a fixed place and order kept in every beat and in POSITIVE LOCKS; each is described by what only he has and what he does not share ('the only man with long hair or a beard; the other three have short hair').
95. **Where the scene is (L78):** the background matches the storyboard's place; never carry a background over from another scene's frame; open-sea scenes say 'open sea in every direction, one clean, empty horizon' and attach a registered at-sea plate (background only).
96. **Hand props (L79):** every hand prop the action depends on (mug, ladle, comb, rope) has its own registered prop ID attached, with the hand, its state (full/empty) and size, in the reference line, every beat and POSITIVE LOCKS; it is the only copy in frame (L51).
97. **Quiet moments need time (L80):** in a grooming or private moment, every look and smile gets its own beat of at least 1.5 s; use the full 15 s rather than packing 12 s.
98. **Props in multi-cut and POV shots (L81):** a prop's reference line names the one cut it is in and the other cuts have none; never 'at the frame edge': the whole prop and the holding hand are in frame, with the hold described from the approved shot; POV cuts have nothing between the lens and the person.
99. **Camera-side placement (L82):** a person seen from behind is placed relative to the camera ("between the camera and the wheel, his back to the camera, the wheel in front of him"), never "behind the <object>"; the face is locked unseen; if the plate has no foreground room, a start frame with the person in place is made first.
100. **No statues (L83):** no person is written "still", "frozen" or "stays there"; every person in frame, background figures too, has continuous small life (breathing, weight shifts, head turns, hand adjustments, sway with the boat) across the whole clip; holds are quiet beats, not freezes.
