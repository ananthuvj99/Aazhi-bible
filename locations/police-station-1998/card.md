# The police station, 1998
Made 2026-11-24 with Nano Banana 2.1 (Higgsfield) from Ranjith's two references (a Kerala police station building; a station's working room) and the SB049 storyboard panel (`sb049-panel-reference.jpg`, the SI's office). All three spaces share one look: weathered cream exterior with terracotta bands; inside, faded sea-green lime wash with a dark green dado, red-oxide floors, dark wooden furniture, files tied in red tape, Gandhiji's portrait. No readable text anywhere: every signboard, plaque and number plate is blank (the first exterior drafts had lettering and were edited).
