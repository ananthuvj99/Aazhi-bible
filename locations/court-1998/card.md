# The magistrate's court, 1998

A small mofussil court: plain white-washed walls, slightly worn; two old ceiling fans on long rods; tall windows with thin faded floral curtains on the RIGHT wall, bright daylight; worn red-oxide floor. At the far end a long raised reddish-brown polished wooden bench on a low platform with three high-backed dark chairs (the magistrate's in the middle), files in red tape, a brass bell; an old pendulum wall clock and Gandhiji's black-and-white portrait on the wall above; a dark wooden door at the right of the back wall, a doorway at the far left. Below the bench the clerk's table with ledgers and a black typewriter. In front: the wooden DOCK with turned railings at the LEFT (a slatted enclosure behind it), the WITNESS BOX at the RIGHT, the advocates' table with two cane-seat chairs in the middle. Foreground: dark wooden public benches either side of a central gap behind a turned wooden railing with round finials. No readable text, no flags, no crest.
