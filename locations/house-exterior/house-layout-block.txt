HOUSE LAYOUT
A small single-storey house in three parts, front to back. Front: an open veranda with four turned white pillars and the blue front door at the centre. Middle: a straight central passage running from the front door to the back of the house, with one bedroom on each side — Antony's bedroom on the left as you walk in from the front door, his mother's bedroom on the right; each bedroom door opens off the passage near the front end. Back: the kitchen, running the full width of the house, entered through open blue double doors at the end of the passage, with the wood-fire stove in its back-left corner and a barred window with white shutters in the back wall looking out to the sea. Each bedroom has a barred window onto the front veranda and another in its outer side wall. Inside Antony's bedroom: the door is in the wall shared with the passage, near the front; the cot stands along the opposite outer wall under the side window, its head end toward the back of the house; the wooden shelf with the alarm clock hangs on the back wall directly above the head of the cot; the cloth hooks are in the back corner beside it; the grey metal trunk sits on the floor against the back wall. Pale blue walls, deep blue base band, red-oxide floors, exposed wooden rafters and clay tiles in every room.
