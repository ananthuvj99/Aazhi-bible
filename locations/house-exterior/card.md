- Two front windows, one per bedroom: blue iron bars, white wooden shutters.
- Kitchen has side doors west and east, each onto a small red-oxide platform.
- Sandy yard; low laterite rubble compound wall on west, east and north; coconut palms at the corners; small green shrubs at the veranda corners.
