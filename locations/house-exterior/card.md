## Orientation
- House faces **SOUTH**. Morning sun from the **EAST** (screen-right when looking at the front).
- West half = Antony's room (screen-left of the front door from outside). East half = Rosamma's room (screen-right).
- Kitchen across the full rear (north).

## Front elevation (from `@housefullview` / `@houseout`)
- Single storey, pale sky-blue walls, deep blue base band, white trim.
- Hipped Mangalore clay tile roof, terracotta; a lower tiled section at the rear (kitchen) with a small chimney at the rear-left (north-west, above the aduppu).
- Front veranda across the facade: four turned white pillars, red-oxide floor and step edge, blue plinth.
- Centre: one blue wooden double door; one small wooden cross on the wall above the door; one small framed holy picture on the wall to the right of the door.
- Two front windows, one each side of the door: blue iron bars, white wooden shutters folded open.
- Veranda furniture: one wooden easy chair (left/west of the door), one long wooden bench (right/east).
- Central red-oxide entry steps.

## Side and rear elevations
- Each side wall: one barred window with white shutters, plus one smaller barred window without shutters toward the rear (kitchen).
- Rear wall: one brown wooden door at the centre, two small barred windows, a clothesline with white and blue cloths, a stone platform with a clay pot and two steel cans, a stack of firewood beside the door.

## Environment (from `@houseout`)
- The house stands on a **red laterite headland** above the beach. The cliff edge drops to the sea on the **east** side (screen-right from the front).
- A low **laterite block compound wall** runs across the front with an open gap (no gate) on line with the front steps; the yard inside is packed red earth.
- In front of the wall: soft pale sand.
- Screen-left (west): a sandy lane running down past neighbouring tiled cottages, a wooden electric pole.
- Screen-right (east), below the cliff: a curving sandy beach with a row of wooden fishing boats pulled up, gentle surf; a **white church with a tall spire** on the shore further along; green hills behind.
- Tall coconut palms around the house, two framing the front.
- Early-morning look: golden haze, soft mist in the palms.
