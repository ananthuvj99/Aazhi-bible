# Rosamma's room (LOC-ANTONY-HOUSE-1990_MOT-01)
Platform ID: `@ammaroomclean` · Labelled: `@ammaroommapped` (never attach)

East half of the house, east of the central passage.

## By wall (camera at south wall looking north = sheet view)
- **West wall (screen-left):** blue wooden door at the south end, to the central passage.
- **North wall (back):** one wooden shelf with folded cloths (white, red/maroon, yellow, a small striped stack); one wooden crucifix; one framed picture of Mother Mary (Immaculate Heart), to the right of the crucifix; one grey metal trunk on the floor, west side.
- **East wall (screen-right):** cot along the wall, head at the north end; one barred window with a blue frame.
- **South wall:** front window with bars and white shutters (behind camera).

## Bedding
- Wooden rope-strung cot, cream bedsheet with thin red and blue stripes, one checked pillow.

## Notes
- This is the only room with the crucifix and Mother Mary picture.
- The top-down house cutaway shows an extra frame on the east wall; the room sheet does not. Follow the room sheet.
