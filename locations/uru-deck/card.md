## The wheel (decided by Ranjith, 2026-11-07, option C)
The uru is steered from a big spoked wooden wheel INSIDE the lower level of the deckhouse, just behind its front wall, so the helmsman looks out through the front windows over the deck and the bow to the sea. Never a wheel on the open deck.

## Geography (keep it)
The uru lies with the quay on one side: looking aft (c1) the quay is RIGHT; looking forward (c2) the quay is LEFT. The deckhouse is aft, the bow and its mooring bitts forward.
