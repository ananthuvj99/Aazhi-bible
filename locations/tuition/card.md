## Building
- Single long room, faded pale-yellow lime-washed walls with damp stains, moss and peeled patches; a deep green plinth along the base outside, a deep green band along the base of the walls inside.
- Low hipped roof of weathered Mangalore clay tiles with moss, sloping down on all four sides; eaves with exposed rafter ends. Inside: dark wooden rafters, hip rafters from each corner to a short ridge beam, tile undersides visible.
- Front long wall, left to right: three equal barred window openings (dark wooden frame, vertical wooden bars, no shutters, no glass), then at the right-hand end the green wooden door in a green frame, single panelled leaf, opens inward, hinged on the right (chalkboard) side. A small blank wooden board above the door. Two worn stone steps in front of the door.
- Back long wall: three barred window openings, evenly spaced.
- Both short end walls: plain, no openings.

## Inside (from the top view)
- Chalkboard: large, blank, wooden frame, centred on the inside of the right end wall (the door end).
- Teacher's table centred in front of the board, about 1.2 m out from it; one wooden chair between the table and the board. On the table only a small stack of exercise books and two sticks of white chalk. No bell.
- Four wooden desk-bench rows, evenly spaced, each running across the full width of the room with about 30 cm left at each end; the bench is on the side away from the board, so the children face the chalkboard. A wider open space between the last row and the teacher's table.
- Red-oxide floor, worn and patchy.
- Through the door from inside: the sunlit sandy yard with dry leaves, the steps, a low laterite wall, palms, small tiled houses.

## Around it
- Packed pale sandy yard with dry leaves and weeds, a low red laterite brick boundary wall on both sides, tall coconut palms, a wooden electric pole at the left of the front, small tiled cottages in the distance at the left.

## Sight-lines
