# Clara's tuition centre (Scene 3)
IDs: `@tuitionout` (exterior in surroundings) · `@tuitionfullview` (elevations) · `@tuitionclean` (inside, toward chalkboard) · `@tuitiondoor` (inside, toward the door) · `@tuitionmap` (labelled top-down — never attach)

## Building
- Single long room, faded pale-yellow lime-washed walls with damp stains and moss, a deep green band along the base, low gabled Mangalore tile roof with moss, exposed wooden rafters inside.
- Front long wall: three large barred window openings with vertical wooden bars (no shutters), and at the right-hand end the green wooden door (opens inward), a small blank wooden board above it, two stone steps.
- Back long wall: three matching barred window openings.
- Both short gable walls: plain, no openings.

## Inside
- Chalkboard: large, blank, wooden frame, on the short wall at the door end.
- Teacher's table in front of it with one wooden chair; on the table a small brass bell, a stack of books, white chalk.
- Four long wooden desk-benches facing the chalkboard.
- Red-oxide floor.
- Through the door from inside: the sandy yard with dry leaves, a low laterite wall, palms, small houses and water in the distance.

## Around it
- Packed sandy yard with dry leaves, low laterite boundary wall, tall coconut palms, a wooden electric pole, small tiled cottages; a glimpse of water to one side.

## Sight-lines
- Outside on the steps looking in through the door: the chalkboard and the teacher's table are straight ahead/just inside; the benches are to the side.
- Inside the door looking out: yard, laterite wall, palms, houses — no tuition sheets needed (L35).
