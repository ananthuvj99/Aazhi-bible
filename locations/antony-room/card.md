# Antony's room (LOC-ANTONY-HOUSE-1990_ANT-01)
Platform IDs (2026-11-04): `@antonyroom` (four-view sheet) · `@antonyroomc1plate` (C1, straight down) · `@antonyroomc3plate` (C3, corner view) · `@antonyroomc4plate` (C4, from the passage through the door). Old `@antonyroomclean` / `@antonyroommapped` are replaced — do not use.

West half of the house, west of the central passage.

## By wall (camera at south wall looking north = sheet view)
- **West wall (screen-left in sheet view):** cot along the wall, head at the north end; one window with a blue frame and iron bars above the cot's head end.
- **North wall (back):** one wooden shelf with a row of books, one steel tumbler and a small wooden object; one row of hooks at the north-west corner with a navy cloth and a white cloth; one grey metal trunk on the floor, east side of the wall.
- **East wall (screen-right):** one blue wooden door at the south end, opening to the central passage.
- **South wall:** front window with blue iron bars and white shutters (behind camera in the sheet view).

## Bedding and floor
- Wooden rope-strung cot, always made up with a faded blue checked bedsheet covering the whole rope frame, one blue checked pillow (as in approved 1A). Antony sleeps on top, no cover sheet (L20).
- From the doorway the cot runs sideways across the frame, long side to camera, head end at screen-right (L22).
- Red-oxide floor; pale blue walls with deep blue base band; wooden rafters and clay tiles visible.

## Not in this room
- No crucifix, no religious pictures, no mirror.
- **Wall clock** (`@wallclock`, added by Ananthu): on the front wall beside the front window, near the outer corner, above the foot of the cot. See `props/wallclock/card.md`.

## Light
- Morning: warm slanting sun through the south front window. West window gets no direct morning sun.
- All windows are barred — no camera move through a window (L4).
