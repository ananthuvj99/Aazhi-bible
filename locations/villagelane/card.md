# Village lane
ID: `@villagelane` · Sheet: `sheet.png` (three views)
- Narrow lane of pale sand and red earth between tall coconut palms.
- Cottages: a faded blue cottage with dark fishing nets hanging on bamboo poles beside it; a cream cottage; a faded pink cottage. All clay-tiled.
- Low mossy laterite walls, a wooden picket gate, woven bamboo/palm fences, wooden electric poles.
- One view opens at the end of the lane onto the beach and the sea.
