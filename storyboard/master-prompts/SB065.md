SCENE CONTEXT
Morning, 1998, an old jail on the Kerala coast, the covered veranda beside the mess shed, just after breakfast. A young prisoner was refused food at the counter minutes ago; an older prisoner from another cell sits down near him and quietly shares his own meal. One continuous locked medium close-up of the older man, Varkey, 12 seconds. Varkey speaks one short line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

Beyond the step the yard is bright and dusty with one old tree.
Light: mid-morning sun outside; the veranda in soft shade under the roof.
People in frame: only Varkey. Varkey: about 61, thin and slightly stooped, light brown skin, a long lined face, short grey-white hair receding at the temples, a short neat grey-white beard, thick grey eyebrows, calm dark eyes; a pair of round thin-wire reading spectacles folded in his shirt pocket; a plain off-white prison-issue half-sleeve cotton shirt with two buttons, soft from years of washing, and an off-white cotton mundu folded to the knee; barefoot. One of him, never a copy.
