## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
A dark night out on the open sea off Kerala, about 1985, in a dream, seconds after the far light went out. A father and his son of about 10 are alone in a small wooden vallam, the father still holding the boy's wrist; a big wave rises out of the dark behind the boat, strikes it broadside, rolls it over on its side, and throws the two of them out into the sea. Emotion: scared. The shot in one sentence: the same locked, low side view of the whole small boat across the water as the shot before, the wave rising behind it and breaking over it. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the dream at sea; the same words in every shot of SB012–SB013)
Place: open sea at night, no land anywhere, a dark navy sea with a long low swell and small breaking caps; a very dark navy sky with a few faint stars, no moon.
Our boat: one small plank vallam about 7 m long and 1.2 m wide, low and narrow, black-tarred planks with a faded blue band along the top edge, a raised curved bow at the right, two wooden thwarts, two long wooden oars lying along the bottom, a coil of coir rope, one brass hurricane lantern standing on the bottom boards near the stern at the left.
The other boat: gone; only dark water at the right of the frame on the horizon where it went down.
People in frame: the boy Antony, about 10: lean, sun-browned, short black hair with a cowlick at the crown, a faded sky-blue half-sleeve cotton shirt with one button missing, khaki half trousers, a thin black cord with a small aluminium cross medal. His father, about 40: tall, broad-shouldered, sun-darkened, short black hair greying at the temples, thick black moustache, a faded white cotton vest, a faded blue checked lungi tied up at the knee, a faded white thorthu tied round his head, a thin silver chain with a small cross.
NOW: the boy kneels on the bottom boards just behind the bow, his right hand on the gunwale, staring at the empty horizon at the right; the father on one knee behind him, his LEFT hand still round the boy's LEFT wrist, staring the same way.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
None attached (after SB012 is approved, its last frame goes in the start slot and covers 0.0s). The boat, the father, the boy and the sea are described in full in this prompt; build them only from these words. Exactly two people in the shot, each once.

THE WAVE (most important)
One wave, built by the second, a real ocean wave, not a wall and not a tsunami. 0.0s–2.5s: hold: behind the boat, about 25 m beyond it, the sea slowly heaves up into a dark ridge across the whole width of the frame, black with a grey sheen, rising above the horizon line; nobody in the boat has seen it. 2.5s–4.0s: it rises to about 3 m high, about twice the height of the father kneeling, its top thinning and turning white, the white crest curling over from the left of the frame toward the right as it peels; it towers behind the two figures. 4.0s–4.6s: the father turns his head over his left shoulder toward it, his eyes go wide, and he pulls the boy's wrist hard toward his own chest. 4.6s–5.4s: the crest breaks onto the far side of the boat: a mass of white water slams the far gunwale, the boat heels toward the camera about 50 degrees, the lantern tips and goes out, the oars slide. 5.4s–6.5s: the father and the boy are thrown out of the boat together over the near gunwale, the father's arm still round the boy, and disappear into the white foam on the near side of the boat, about 2 m from it, never toward the lens. 6.5s–7.5s: the boat rolls back upright, half full of water, low in the sea, spinning slowly, both oars floating beside it. 7.5s–10.0s: hold: white foam spreading and fizzing on the black water where they went in, the swamped empty boat rocking, the wave's back rolling away toward the camera as a low hump that lifts the frame. Nobody surfaces in this shot. Avoid: a cartoon wall of water, slow motion, the two flying high through the air, a splash on the lens, any limb near the camera.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 10 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 10 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–10.0s: exactly the camera of the shot before: on the water about 7 m off the boat's left side, about 1.2 m above the sea, level, looking straight across the boat; lens about 35mm; a wide shot: the whole vallam across the middle of the frame, bow at the right, stern at the left, the father and the boy full figure; the wave rising beyond the boat across the whole width of the frame. Sharp: the boat, the two people and the wave crest. The camera rides the sea: it rises and falls gently with the swell from 0.0s to 7.0s and lifts about 1 m as the wave's back passes under it at 7.5s–9.0s, settling again by 10.0s; otherwise locked. No zoom.
Action as written in THE WAVE.

EYELINES
The boy: 0.0s–4.6s at the empty horizon at the right of the frame, gaze level, head in profile to frame right; at 4.6s he looks up and back over his left shoulder at the crest above him for a fraction of a second as he is pulled. The father: 0.0s–4.0s at the empty horizon at the right, level; 4.0s–4.6s over his left shoulder up at the crest behind the boat, high, eyes wide; 4.6s–5.4s down at the boy. From 5.4s no face is visible. Nobody looks at the camera.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
The frame rises and falls about 20 cm with the swell every 5 seconds, then lifts about 1 m as the wave passes under it; a small jolt and a short blur in the frame at 5.0s from the impact; spray drifting across the frame at 5.2s without touching the lens; a sharp exposure dip at 5.0s as the lantern goes out, so only the cold night is left; a focus breath onto the foam at 6.0s; small white drops falling back onto the water until 8.0s.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The wave is about 3 m high and about 30 m long from trough to trough, travelling about 25 km/h from beyond the boat toward the camera; its crest breaks because it steepens. About 2 tonnes of white water strike the far gunwale; the boat, about 400 kg, heels about 50 degrees toward the camera and the people inside fall toward the low side because of the tilt and the water's push; the father's weight about 75 kg and the boy's about 30 kg land in the sea together with one wide splash. The swamped boat rights itself because its wooden hull floats, sitting low, half full of water. The lantern goes out when water covers it. Foam fizzes and spreads for several seconds. Wind about 25 km/h tears spray off the crest. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Real people overwhelmed in a second: no heroic poses, no dives. The father's only movement is to hold on to his son; the boy is pulled into his father's chest. No two people move at the same moment before the impact; after it, the sea moves them. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin, five fingers on every hand. No posing.

LIGHTING
Low-key night. 0.0s–5.0s: exactly one warm light, the brass hurricane lantern on the bottom boards near the stern, about 2000K, warm on the inside of the boat and on the two figures, lighting the underside of the curling crest faintly orange-gold for a moment; the rest cold, dim starlit night about 7000K, the wave dark navy-black with a grey sheen and a white crest. 5.0s: the lantern goes out. 5.0s–10.0s: only the cold night about 7000K: the white foam the brightest thing in the frame, the boat a dark shape on dark water. Never flat, never bright blue, no moon, no lightning.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design: no riser, no boom, no whoosh, no slow-motion sound.
No spoken words and no voices; nobody speaks; only a sharp breath.
0.0s–2.5s: wind over the water, the slap of the swell on the planks; 2.5s–4.6s a deep, growing roar of the wave building behind the boat; 4.3s a short sharp in-breath from the father; 5.0s the crash of breaking water on the wood and the hollow knock of the hull, the oars clattering; 5.6s the plunge of two bodies into the sea under the roar; 6.5s–10.0s the hiss and fizz of foam, water sloshing inside the swamped boat, the wind.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation around the lantern before it goes out, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Low-key deep navy night, white foam; no dream filters.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of shot: a calm, slow mood in an emergency (L141) — the wave is fast and loud; things growing or jumping in size between shots (L156) — the boat and the people keep the size of the shot before; a body or splash coming at the lens (L150); a second lantern (L155); a boat that changes shape (L110) — the same low black plank vallam; shore lights, palms or land at sea (L78); a cut type wrong — no cut at all. Exactly one boat, one wave, two people. The other boat does not reappear. No lettering. No cut, no zoom.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly two people, each once, both in the boat until 5.4s, both gone into the foam on the near side by 6.5s, together. Exactly one small vallam, one wave about 3 m high breaking from behind it, one lantern that goes out at 5.0s. The empty swamped boat afloat at the end. Nobody speaks. Runs the full 10 seconds.
```
