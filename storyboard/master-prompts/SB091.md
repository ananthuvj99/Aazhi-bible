SCENE CONTEXT
Dusk, January 2005, a small rented room close to the shore on the South Indian coast. The emotional situation: Antony is alone and has decided to go on without meeting anyone; he folds the dry shirt the fisherman gave him, then sits and draws, from memory, the small old church his teacher once described. The shot: one locked medium-wide view of the room, 15 seconds. Nobody speaks. Emotion: thoughtful.

MEMORY (the same room in every shot of this scene; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: a room about 3 m by 3.5 m: lime-washed white walls gone grey-yellow, with damp stains near the floor; a red-oxide cement floor; one single wooden cot against the left wall with a thin cotton mattress and a faded green bedsheet; one small square wooden table 70 cm wide under the window at the right, with one wooden chair; one open window in the right wall with two wooden shutters folded back and three vertical iron bars, through which the sea and the sky are seen; one bare tungsten bulb, 40 watts, hanging on its flex from the ceiling, switched on; one nail on the wall with nothing on it. On the table: one ruled notebook open, one short yellow pencil, one steel tumbler of water. Nothing else in the room: no pictures, no calendar, no television, no religious items.
Light: dusk outside, the last blue light over the sea, the sun already down; the single bulb warm inside.
People in frame: Antony only: about 31, lean, brown skin; short black hair; a full black beard about 3 cm hiding most of a pale scar on his LEFT jawline (on the right of the frame when he faces us); a plain dull-olive half-sleeve cotton shirt; faded dark-grey cotton trousers; barefoot indoors, his black rubber sandals side by side by the door.
NOW: Antony sits on the edge of the cot, facing the camera at about 30 degrees, with a faded maroon-and-brown checked half-sleeve shirt spread open across his knees; both hands on it.
