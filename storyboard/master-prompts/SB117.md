## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Evening, 2026, a senior police officer's house in Kollam, Kerala: Ramachandran's drawing room. Swathi has come in person to face her brother. After years of silence she asks him the one question. One continuous medium close-up of Swathi alone, 12 seconds. Swathi speaks one line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Ramachandran's drawing room, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a large formal drawing room: off-white walls; a dark polished teak cabinet behind Swathi at the right with glass doors, holding brass trophies and framed group photographs too small and soft to read; on the wall behind her at the left, one large framed photograph of a man in police uniform shaking hands at a ceremony, soft, no readable words; a heavy maroon velvet curtain at the far left, drawn; grey granite floor. A brass standing lamp out of frame at the right.
Light: evening; warm 2800K light from the brass standing lamp at the right; cool 4500K white ceiling light, dimmed, from above.
People in frame: only Swathi.
Swathi: about 50, slim, wheatish-brown skin with fine lines around her eyes and mouth and slight dark circles; black hair with grey streaks at the temples, centre parting, tied in a low bun; small gold stud earrings; a thin black-bead and gold chain; a plain pale lavender cotton saree with a thin dark-purple border, pallu over her LEFT shoulder and tucked at the waist, matching blouse; one thin gold bangle on her RIGHT wrist, a black-strap wristwatch on her LEFT wrist.
Off screen (never seen): Ramachandran stands 2 m in front of her, just to the right of the camera; his wife and Swathi's son stand behind the camera.
NOW: Swathi stands upright in the middle of the room, both hands at her sides, the fingers of her right hand closed lightly; her eyes on her brother.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Swathi is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one woman in frame, standing in the middle of the drawing room, who speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

THE DISTANCE BETWEEN THEM
Two metres of grey granite floor between Swathi and her brother, which she does not cross. She has come in at the door behind the camera and stopped exactly here, her feet together, 1 m in front of the teak cabinet. She does not step forward, does not point, does not touch anything in the room. Her stillness in his room is the whole picture: one woman standing where she chose to stop.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera stands 1.6 m in front of Swathi at about 1.5 m height, just left of where Ramachandran stands, on an 85mm lens, so her gaze falls just right of the lens. Medium close-up: the top of her head to her upper chest, her face about 40% of the frame height, a little left of centre; the teak cabinet soft behind her at the right; the framed ceremony photograph soft at the left. Sharp: her eyes and face; the room soft. The camera is locked.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Swathi stands still, looking at her brother just right of the lens; her breath is long and controlled; her lips pressed.
3.0s to 3.6s: she breathes in once, deeply; her chin lifts about 3 degrees; her head comes to rest.
3.6s to 7.0s: she speaks her one Malayalam line, clear and hard, head steady, hands still at her sides.
7.0s to 12.0s (hold): she keeps her eyes on him; her breath comes faster for two breaths, then slows; her right hand closes into a fist and opens at 9.0s; she does not look away. The shot ends on her face.

HER EYES (most important)
Only small, physical things: her eyes do not blink from 3.0s to 8.0s; the lower lids tighten; the muscles at the corners of her jaw stand out once before the line; her nostrils flare on the breath in; after the line the rims of her eyes redden and fill, but no tear falls. Her voice does not break. No shouting, no finger pointing, no slap, no walking forward, no hand to the chest, no glance at the camera.

EYELINES
0.0s to 12.0s: Swathi looks at Ramachandran, standing just right of the lens, level with her own eyes, head turned about 10 degrees to frame right, and holds that line through the breath, the line and the end hold. At 3.0s her chin lifts but her eyes stay on his. She never looks into the lens and never at the people behind the camera.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A slight drift of a few millimetres all through; 3.0s her deep breath lifts her shoulders 1 cm; 3.3s a small focus breath as her chin lifts; 7.0s a half-second of stillness after the line; 7.5s to 9.0s two quick breaths; 10.5s one slow blink, the first since 3.0s.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
Her breath lifts the pallu on her left shoulder 1 cm and lets it fall. The thin gold bangle on her right wrist slides 1 cm as the hand makes a fist. The velvet curtain hangs still; the air is still. Nothing in the room moves. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A sister who has carried this for years and rehearsed none of it: low and hard, controlled, every word placed. Real unretouched skin, pores, lines, five fingers on each hand, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no melodrama.

LIGHTING
Warm 2800K light from the brass standing lamp at the right, softened to an even frontal light on her face; cool 4500K dimmed ceiling light from above on her hair and shoulders; the brass trophies catch small highlights in the cabinet behind; deep shade at the curtain. Never flat, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh, no hit, no transition sound. The room only.
0.0s to 12.0s: still room tone; the faint tick of the brass lamp cooling; 3.0s her breath in; 7.0s to 12.0s room tone and her breath, quickening and slowing.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Swathi speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam Malayalam accent; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
3.6s to 7.0s — Swathi: [angry] "എന്റെ മോൻ മരിച്ചെന്ന് എന്നോടു പറഞ്ഞത് നീയല്ലേ?"
Delivery: about 3.4 seconds, seventeen syllables, firm and even, medium volume, the stress on "നീയല്ലേ", a tiny gap before it, the pitch rising slightly at the end; the voice never breaks.

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation around the lamp highlights, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Warm lamp brass against dark teak and pale lavender.

CONSTRAINTS
Swathi alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no shoulder, hand or head of her brother, his wife or her son at the frame edges. No readable words, names or emblems in the photographs, trophies or anywhere. No pointing, no stepping forward, no tears running down, no shouting. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No lettering or subtitles anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Swathi, once. One framed ceremony photograph, one teak cabinet, one curtain, each fixed. She stands in one place all through, feet together. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 3.6s to 7.0s, head steady, eyes just right of the lens. Locked 85mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let the question come out as years of silence spent in one breath: no tears allowed, every word aimed and held.
```
