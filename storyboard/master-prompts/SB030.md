```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Late at night, 1998, the open Arabian Sea off Kerala, on the forward deck of our wooden cargo uru. Seconds after a torch light by the far burning vallam went out: a hand had risen from the black water near the fire and sunk again. Antony, at the left-hand bulwark with the woken crew, turns and calls down the deck to Aashaan, who has just stepped out of the deckhouse. One continuous shot, 8 seconds, a medium close-up of Antony. He speaks one line, in Malayalam. Spoken language: Malayalam only.

MEMORY (the same night in every shot; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: our uru as in Aswanth's night: brown plank deck; the solid weathered bulwark about 0.9 m high with a broad flat brown wooden top rail and red-brown wooden bitts in pairs, big coils of golden coir rope over the bow; one tall rust-brown mast with rope wound round its foot on the forward deck; the grey two-level weathered timber deckhouse aft; the bow as on @urubowplate.
Light: a low-key night: very dark navy sky with a few stars, a dark navy sea with slow swells; cold blue-grey night light about 7000K; one small brass hurricane lantern on the deck by a coil of coir rope at the right-hand front corner of the deckhouse, warm 2000K, behind the camera; a small lamp at the top of the mast. Never flat, never bright blue. No moon in frame.
People in frame: Antony: 24, lean, sun-browned, slim face, short black hair, light stubble, thin silver chain; pale blue-grey short-sleeve cotton shirt with thin white vertical stripes, sleeves rolled once; green-grey checked mundu; barefoot. The only face in frame and the only person who speaks.
Fire: one small Kerala fishing vallam burning 500 m away, ahead and to the LEFT of the uru: beyond Antony's LEFT shoulder, at the right of the frame, a small far blaze sitting on the horizon line.
NOW: Antony standing at the left-hand bulwark, his LEFT hand on the top rail, his body turned three-quarters toward the camera; Babu, Raghavan and Sasi crowding the bulwark just out of frame at the left; Aashaan just out of the deckhouse door, behind the camera and slightly to its left, out of frame.

ACTIVE REFERENCES

Face, hair and build as on the sheet; shirt as in MEMORY. 100% matches the reference.

THE BULWARK AND THE FAR FIRE
The bulwark: solid weathered timber planks painted blue-grey long ago, worn to bare grey wood at the edges, about 0.9 m high, topped by a broad flat brown wooden rail about 18 cm wide, rubbed smooth and dark by hands; one red-brown wooden bitt about 40 cm tall stands on it at the right of the frame with a turn of coir rope round it. Antony's LEFT hand rests flat on the rail top beside the bitt. The fire: the 9 m vallam at 500 m through a 50mm lens is about a fortieth of the frame wide, a small orange blaze on the horizon right of his left shoulder, a thin smoke plume leaning right, a short broken orange streak on the water below it. It never grows, never comes closer.

ANTONY'S CALL (most important)
The hardest thing is a real shout from a frightened young man that stays a clean, front-facing medium close-up. 0.0s–1.0s: he is still turned out to sea, his face in three-quarter profile toward the right of the frame, eyes locked on the fire, mouth open on a hard breath. 1.0s–2.5s: he throws his LEFT arm out over the bulwark toward the fire at the right of the frame, fingers spread, and looks along the arm, gaze, fingertip and fire on one line (L162); the arm drops back to the rail top at 2.5s. 2.5s–3.0s: his head snaps round toward the camera, eyes landing just left of the lens on Aashaan; chest heaving. 3.0s–5.4s: he shouts the line, head steady, face within 20 degrees of the lens, mouth fully visible, neck tendons tight, brows up and drawn together, the whites of his eyes showing. 5.4s–8.0s: mouth closed, breathing hard through the nose; at 6.0s his head turns back to the fire at the right, his LEFT hand gripping the rail top. No cliché: no hand cupped at the mouth, no theatrical lean, no fist in the air, no tears.

FORMAT MODE
21:9 cinematic widescreen. One continuous shot, 8 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. Runs the full 8 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s to 8.0s: the camera stands on the deck about 1.6 m from Antony, inboard of him and a little aft, lens height 1.4 m (just below his eye line), looking forward and out to the left at him with a 50mm lens; he fills the middle of the frame from mid-chest up; behind him, soft: the bulwark top, the bitt, the dark sea and the tiny far fire at the right. Locked, riding with the boat; no push, no pan. Sharp: Antony's eyes and mouth; the fire a soft orange point. 0.0s–3.0s he points and turns as written above; 3.0s to 5.4s he speaks; 5.4s to 8.0s he looks back to the fire and holds.

EYELINES

Aashaan and the crew are off screen and never seen.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.
Only Antony's lips move. The line is spoken in Malayalam, never in English.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A faint steady drift in the frame from the swell all through. 1.2s: a tiny shake as his arm whips out. 2.6s: a small focus breath as his face turns toward the lens. 3.0s–5.4s: his chest heaves between words. 6.0s: one hard breath out through the nose. The far fire flickers and the exposure on his cheek lifts a hair at 4.5s.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The uru, about 30 tonnes of wood, rises and settles on a long low swell about once every five seconds with a 2 degree roll; the horizon behind him tilts gently. A breeze of about 10 km/h from ahead lifts his collar and a strand of hair. His outstretched arm, about 3.5 kg, is held up by effort and trembles slightly. The far smoke bends right in the wind. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Frightened and urgent: a young man who has just seen a man drown in the dark, shouting for help. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin, sweat on the upper lip, five fingers. No posing.

LIGHTING
As in MEMORY: cold blue-grey night about 7000K; soft, even frontal fill on his face from the deck side (the lantern's warm 2000K glow from behind the camera, low and diffuse), so the mouth reads clearly; a faint 1800K orange edge on his left cheek and shoulder from the far fire; the sky and sea deep navy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, and no cinematic sound design: no riser, no whoosh, no swell, no rumble, no transition sound. Ambient at a steady level that does not build.
0.0s–8.0s: water along the hull, the timbers creaking, the breeze; faint far crackle of the fire; Antony's hard breathing before and after the line.
Spoken language: Malayalam only, not English and not a translation. Antony says the exact Malayalam words in quotes below, in everyday Kollam coastal pronunciation, and nothing else. Lip-sync exact; only Antony speaks. The line is spoken once, exactly as written. Emotion tag for the scene, written once: [shouts]
3.0s to 5.4s: Antony, Kollam coastal accent, a young man's hoarse full-voiced call down the deck, quick, stress on "അവിടെ", a short catch before "ഒരാൾ", rising and cracking on "വെള്ളത്തിൽ". About 2.4 seconds.
"ആശാനേ, അവിടെ നോക്ക്! ഒരാൾ വെള്ളത്തിൽ!"

STYLE
Photoreal 1998 Kerala period feature film, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation around the far fire, visible skin texture and pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Low-key deep navy night as in Aswanth's night. Every frame looks like the film itself, with nothing from the making of it.

CONSTRAINTS
Exactly one face in frame, Antony's; Aashaan, Babu and the crew stay out of frame; nobody else's mouth is seen. Antony points with his LEFT arm only, out to the right of the frame, and looks along it (L160, L162); the arm never comes toward the lens and never covers his mouth. The fire stays tiny, 500 m away, at the right (L156). No lantern in view (L155). No moon. No hand near the camera (L150). No English words anywhere in the audio; Malayalam only. One line, once. No cut, no zoom. No lettering anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One continuous 8-second locked medium close-up of Antony at the left-hand bulwark, 50mm. Exactly one person in frame, once. Exactly one far burning boat, tiny, at the right. He points with his LEFT arm at 1.0s, turns to just left of the lens at 2.5s, and says "ആശാനേ, അവിടെ നോക്ക്! ഒരാൾ വെള്ളത്തിൽ!" once, in Malayalam, 3.0s to 5.4s, head steady; then looks back to the fire. Only Antony speaks, only in Malayalam. Runs the full 8 seconds.
INTENT: a young man's terror breaking into a cry for help, his voice cracking with the fear that someone is dying out there in the dark; spoken in Malayalam with a Kollam coastal accent.
```
