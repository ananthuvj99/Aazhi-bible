SCENE CONTEXT
Late at night, 1998, the open Arabian Sea off Kerala, on the deck of our wooden cargo uru, now brought in close to the burning fishing vallam. Emotion (platform tag, written once): scared. Antony climbs onto the left-hand bulwark and dives head first into the dark sea toward the fire; the other men stay at the rail, watching, and nobody follows him. One continuous shot, 12 seconds, seen from the deck forward of the men, looking aft along the rail. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the same night in every shot; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: our uru as in Aswanth's night: grey-blue weathered hull with long rust streaks; the solid weathered bulwark about 0.9 m high with a broad flat brown wooden top rail; brown plank deck; one rust-brown mast with a boom and rope wound round its foot, behind the camera; the grey two-level weathered timber deckhouse aft with three glass front windows in dark wooden frames, a dark doorway at its left, a balustraded roof deck; the wheel only inside, behind the middle window. The water is about 2.2 m below the top of the bulwark.
Light: a low-key night: very dark navy sky with stars, dark navy sea; cold blue-grey night light about 7000K; one brass hurricane lantern on the deck by a coil of coir rope at the right-hand front corner of the deckhouse, warm 2000K; the burning vallam's big orange light, about 1800K, now close enough to light the men's faces and the bulwark from the right. No moon in frame.
People in frame (each exactly as on his sheet, one of each, never a copy): Antony: 24, lean, short black hair, thin silver chain, pale blue-grey short-sleeve shirt with thin white vertical stripes, green-grey checked mundu, barefoot. Balan: about 35, oiled hair combed back, thin moustache, blue full-sleeve shirt that reads grey-blue at night, two pens in the left pocket, white mundu, black shoes. Raghavan: about 60, thin, grey hair and moustache, white sleeveless vest, red-and-white checked towel round his NECK, grey hair uncovered, white mundu. Babu: about 40, heavy-set, moustache, red-and-white checked towel tied on his HEAD, off-white half-sleeve shirt, grey-brown checked lungi, a thin red thread on his RIGHT wrist. Sasi: about 35, long black curly hair, full black beard, off-white shirt, white mundu. Suresh: about 30, curly hair, moustache, cream shirt with rolled sleeves, green small-check mundu. Aashaan: about 60, grey hair combed back, grey moustache, cream shirt over a white vest, white towel on his LEFT shoulder; small, inside at the wheel.
Fire: the burning vallam about 40 m off the uru's left side, abreast of the men: beyond the bulwark at the right of the frame, a whole boat clearly readable, flames along its length, smoke rising, its orange reflection on the water.
NOW: the six men in a row at the left-hand bulwark, each half a metre to a metre apart, from nearest the camera: Antony, Balan, Raghavan, Babu, Sasi, Suresh; Antony's right wrist just freed from Balan's grip; Aashaan at the wheel inside.

THE BULWARK AND THE DIVE (most important)
The bulwark: solid timber, about 0.9 m high, a broad flat brown top rail about 18 cm wide, wet with spray. The dive, as small real movements: 0.0s–2.5s: Antony, nearest the camera, puts both hands flat on the rail top, plants his right foot on a red-brown bitt at the bulwark's foot and pulls himself up, his mundu hitched and tucked at the knees, until he crouches on the rail top on both bare feet, toes curled over the edge, his left hand on the rail between his feet for balance. 2.5s–3.5s: he rises from the crouch, knees still bent, arms swinging back, his eyes on the fire. 3.5s–4.5s: he pushes off with both legs and dives head first out to the right of the frame, arms stretched forward together, body in a long diagonal arc out and down toward the burning boat; he leaves the frame at the lower right. Never a feet-first drop, never a flip, never a flying leap upward. 4.5s–5.0s: the splash: a white plume rises about 1 m above the bulwark top at the right edge and falls back. The men: 3.5s: Balan's right hand shoots out after Antony's ankle and closes on air 20 cm short, then stops on the rail; 4.6s: all five lean over the rail top on their forearms, staring down at the water; nobody climbs, nobody follows.

21:9 cinematic widescreen. One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. Runs the full 12 seconds.

In frame: the bulwark running from the right edge into depth toward the deckhouse; the six men in a row at it, Antony nearest at the right-centre, the others smaller one behind another, their faces in three-quarter profile turned to the right toward the fire; beyond the bulwark at the right of the frame the burning vallam 40 m off; at the back, the deckhouse front, its lantern at the right corner, Aashaan small behind the middle window. EYELINES
0.0s–2.5s: Antony looks out to the right of the frame at the burning boat, level with the horizon; the others look from the fire to Antony nearest the camera, each in turn. 2.5s–4.5s: Antony's eyes stay on the fire as he dives, along his outstretched arms; Balan's eyes on Antony's legs. 4.5s–12.0s: all five men look down over the bulwark at the water just right of the frame edge, about 2.5 m below, then slowly lift their eyes out toward the fire at the right as Antony swims away below. Aashaan, through the window: out at the fire, ahead-left. Nobody looks at the camera.

A slow drift in the frame with the swell all through. 1.0s: the frame shivers as Antony's weight lands on the bulwark. 3.5s: a tiny focus breath as he leaves. 4.6s: spray from the splash catches the firelight and the exposure lifts briefly. 6.0s: the men's breathing shows in their shoulders. 9.0s: the fire flares and every face brightens a little.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The uru, about 30 tonnes, rises and settles on a long low swell every five seconds with a 2 degree roll, slower now it is close and idling. Antony, about 62 kg, pushes off a rail 2.2 m above the water: the boat rolls very slightly toward the left side as his weight goes; he falls about 0.7 seconds before he strikes the water; the splash plume rises about 3 m from the surface and falls back as spray. The fire 40 m off throws a moving orange light that flickers on the men at about 3 to 6 times a second; smoke drifts right in a breeze of about 10 km/h. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Antony: a frightened young man doing it anyway, jaw set, one deep breath before the push. The others: shock and fear, each his own way, and none of them moves to follow. No two people move at the same moment. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin, five fingers. No posing, no heroic pose on the rail.

LIGHTING
As in MEMORY: cold blue-grey night about 7000K on the deck and the deckhouse; the burning boat's orange 1800K light from the right, flickering, lighting the right side of every face and the wet bulwark top, throwing long shadows to the left across the deck; the lantern's warm 2000K pool at the right front corner of the deckhouse; a dim warm glow behind the deckhouse windows round Aashaan. Sky and sea deep navy; the fire's reflection a broken orange path on the water.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, and no cinematic sound design: no riser, no whoosh, no swell, no rumble, no transition sound. Ambient at a steady level that does not build. No spoken words and no voices; every mouth stays closed; only breathing and gasps.

Photoreal 1998 Kerala period feature film, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation around the fire and the lantern, visible skin texture and pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Low-key deep navy night lit by one fire. Every frame looks like the film itself, with nothing from the making of it.
