```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Midday, 2026, a cleared plot inland from the Alappad coast in Kerala, where a row of small houses stands half built. The families were promised homes near the sea; this is not that place, and the demolition of their old homes is set for next week. Muthu has seen enough and calls the investor. One continuous medium close-up of Muthu alone, standing, 12 seconds. Muthu speaks one line in Malayalam into the phone; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (the replacement site, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a cleared plot of hard red laterite earth, dusty, with dry scrub and a few thorny bushes; behind Muthu a row of six small one-storey houses of bare grey concrete blocks, unplastered, flat concrete roofs with steel rods sticking up, empty window holes with no frames, doorways with no doors; a black plastic water tank on a rusted steel stand at the end of the row, its pipe hanging loose and connected to nothing; a rutted mud track running past; no sea anywhere, only a flat line of dry scrub and a few distant coconut palms on the horizon under a pale hazy sky.
Light: harsh midday sun almost overhead, a little behind the camera, 5600K, a white haze in the sky; short hard shadows.
People in frame: only Muthu.
Muthu: about 58, compact and strong, very dark sun-weathered skin with deep lines at the eyes, thick greying eyebrows; short curly hair, mostly grey; a grey-and-black stubble beard of a week; broad, rough hands; a faded olive-green cotton half-sleeve shirt with one chest pocket on his LEFT, sweat-darkened at the collar; a dark brown mundu folded up to the knee (below the frame); a black digital wristwatch with a rubber strap on his LEFT wrist; no chain.
Off screen (never seen): the old fisherman, his wife and the site supervisor stand on the mud track about 4 m behind the camera, a little to its right.
NOW: Muthu stands square to the camera between the camera and the row of houses, about 6 m in front of them; his hands at his sides; a small black smartphone with a cracked corner in his LEFT chest pocket.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Muthu is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one man in frame, standing on the red earth in front of the unfinished houses, who takes his phone out, calls and speaks. Nobody else is in frame; the houses behind him are empty.

THE UNFINISHED HOUSES
Six identical small houses about 5 x 6 m each, 3 m high, of bare grey hollow concrete blocks with grey mortar squeezed out of the joints, no plaster, no paint; flat roof slabs with rusty steel rods 30 cm long sticking up at the corners; empty window holes 1 x 1 m and doorways 0.9 x 2 m with no frames; heaps of sand and broken blocks at their feet. The water tank, 1000 litres, black plastic, sits on a rusted angle-iron stand 2 m high, its outlet pipe hanging open. They stand 6 to 25 m behind Muthu, soft, filling the right two-thirds of the frame behind him; the scrub and the empty horizon at frame left. No electricity poles, no road sign, no lettering anywhere.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera stands 1.6 m in front of Muthu at his standing eye height about 1.6 m, on a 75mm lens, its back to where the families stand, so Muthu's eyeline passes just to the right of the lens toward them. Medium close-up: from just above his head to the middle of his chest; his face filling about 40% of the frame height, a little left of centre; the unfinished houses soft behind him at the right, the scrub and the empty horizon soft at the left. Sharp: Muthu's eyes and face all through; the houses soft but readable as bare blocks and holes. The camera is locked; it only breathes.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Muthu stands still, looking just right of the lens at the families by the first house; his jaw works once; sweat at his temples.
3.0s to 5.6s: his right hand goes to his left chest pocket and takes out the phone; his eyes go down to it; his thumb taps the screen once; he raises the phone to his RIGHT ear and holds it there; it rings out of hearing.
5.6s to 6.2s: the call is answered; his eyes lift back to the families just right of the lens; his head comes to rest and stays still.
6.2s to 9.6s: he speaks his one Malayalam line into the phone, low and urgent, head steady, the phone at his right ear beside his face, his mouth fully visible.
9.6s to 12.0s (hold): he listens, eyes still on the families; one nod; he keeps the phone at his ear; his lips close and stay closed. The shot ends on his face.

THE CALL (most important)
His urgency is shown only by small, physical things: the thumb taps once without searching, as if the number is already there; the phone goes to the ear fast; the jaw sets; the eyes never leave the families while he speaks, as if he is speaking for them. The phone is held beside the face at the right ear, never in front of the mouth. The voice is low, not shouting. No pacing, no turning away, no hand on the hip, no wiping sweat theatrically, no glance at the camera.

EYELINES
0.0s to 3.0s: Muthu looks at the families just right of the lens, level with his own eyes, head turned about 10 degrees to frame right. 3.0s to 5.6s: his gaze goes down to the phone at chest height, head tilted down about 25 degrees. 5.6s to 12.0s: his gaze returns to the families just right of the lens, level, and holds through the line and the end hold. He never looks into the lens and never back at the houses.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
0.0s to 12.0s: a very slight drift of a few millimetres in the frame as the camera breathes in the heat; heat shimmer rising off the red earth behind him; 1.2s his breath in; 3.5s a small focus breath as he dips his head to the phone and returns at 5.8s; 4.4s the phone glass flashes in the sun and the exposure dips a hair; 10.0s his breath out.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
A hot, dry wind of about 10 km/h blows across the plot: it lifts thin dust off the red earth in low curls, flaps his loose shirt collar, and stirs the dry scrub; the loose pipe on the water tank swings 2 to 3 cm. Sweat shines at his temples and one bead runs 1 cm down at 8.0s. The phone weighs about 180 g in his hand. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Plain, direct, a working man who has made up his mind. Real unretouched skin with deep lines, stubble, sweat and pores, five fingers on each hand, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no action-hero urgency.

LIGHTING
Harsh midday sun at 5600K high and a little behind the camera, softened on his face by a pale bounce off the dry red earth so the face reads with soft, even frontal light; short shadows under the brow kept open; the concrete houses grey and flat in the glare; the hazy sky bright but held, not burnt white. Never cold blue, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh. Only the sounds of this empty plot.
0.0s to 12.0s: hot dry wind; dry scrub rustling; crickets; a far crow; no sea sound anywhere; 3.0s to 5.6s the shirt pocket rustles and the phone screen taps once; 9.6s to 12.0s wind, crickets, a faint unclear voice from the phone's earpiece.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Muthu speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal fisherman's Malayalam accent; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
6.2s to 9.6s — Muthu: [worried] "ഇവിടെ കാര്യങ്ങൾ മാറിയിട്ടുണ്ട്. ഉടനെ വരണം."
Delivery: about 3.4 seconds, two short sentences, low urgent volume, a short pause after the first full stop, pitch level and firm, the stress on "മാറിയിട്ടുണ്ട്" and on "ഉടനെ".

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation in the white sky, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Dusty red earth, bare grey concrete and olive-green palette under a pale white sky.

CONSTRAINTS
Muthu alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no other person at the frame edges or in the houses; no hand near the camera. No sea, no boats, no beach anywhere in frame. The houses are unfinished: no paint, no doors, no glass, no furniture. No readable screen on the phone; no lettering, no sign, no company board, no logo anywhere. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No subtitles anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Muthu, once. Exactly one phone (taken out once at 3.0s, held at his right ear from 5.0s to the end), six unfinished concrete houses, one water tank on its stand. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 6.2s to 9.6s, head steady, eyes just right of the lens. Locked 75mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let it sound like a man who does not waste words on the phone: low, certain and urgent, the alarm kept under control.
```
