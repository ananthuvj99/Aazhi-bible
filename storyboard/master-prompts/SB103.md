## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Evening, 2026, a shipping-company office in Kollam, Kerala. A successful businessman is committing more than he can afford to a project that is not yet funded, and is looking for a way to make the figures close. One continuous medium close-up of Balan alone at his desk, 12 seconds. Balan speaks one line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Balan's office, the same in every shot of this panel and of SB105)
Place: a large, old-fashioned office about 5 x 4 m: wood-panelled walls in dark teak to 1.2 m, cream paint above, a little yellowed; a heavy teak desk with a green leather inlay; behind Balan on the wall at frame left ONE framed old black-and-white photograph of a wooden uru under sail, 40 x 30 cm, with a small brass ship's barometer standing on the low cabinet below it; at frame right behind him a tall glass-fronted teak cabinet of box files with blank spines, and a large framed photograph of a cargo ship at sea, colours faded, no name readable on the hull; a window behind the cabinet with the blinds closed; evening.
Light: an evening office: a green-shaded brass desk lamp at frame right on the desk, warm 2700K; a weak overhead tube light at 4000K; the blinds dark.
People in frame: only Balan.
Balan: about 63, heavier than he was, a soft jowl, fair-brown skin with old faint pitting from acne on the forehead and cheeks, real pores, a light sweat sheen on the forehead; grey-white hair, still oiled and combed back with a side parting on his LEFT; a thin moustache now grey; reading glasses with thin gold frames low on his nose; a light-blue full-sleeve cotton shirt, sleeves buttoned at the cuffs, one chest pocket on his LEFT with two pens clipped in it; a gold wristwatch with a brown leather strap on his LEFT wrist; a gold ring with a small red stone on his right ring finger.
Off screen (never seen): the accountant stands at the side of the desk, just left of the camera.
NOW: Balan sits forward in a high-backed brown leather chair, both forearms on the desk; before him lie three stapled sheets of figures side by side and one grey desk calculator.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Balan is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one man in frame, seated at his desk over three sheets of figures, who takes a pen from his pocket, circles one figure and speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

THE THREE SHEETS
Three stapled A4 sheets of printed figures, each with a short handwritten column added in blue ink at the right, lying side by side on the green leather inlay at the bottom of the frame: the first for preparations, the second for land, the third for relocation. The numbers are tiny and soft; nothing is readable. One grey plastic desk calculator, 17 x 10 cm, with worn keys, sits at the frame right of the sheets. One of the two pens in his LEFT chest pocket (on frame right, since he faces the camera) is a black ballpoint; he takes it out with his RIGHT hand (on frame left), circles one figure on the third sheet in a single movement, and lays the pen across that sheet. Nothing is torn, signed or thrown.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera sits 1.7 m in front of Balan across the desk, at his seated eye height about 1.2 m, on a 75mm lens, directly beside where the accountant stands, so Balan's eyeline passes just to the left of the lens. Medium close-up: from just above his head to the middle of his chest, with the near edge of the desk and the top of the sheets at the bottom of the frame; his face filling about 40% of the frame height, a little right of centre; the uru photograph soft at the left behind him, the cabinet and ship photograph soft at the right. Sharp: Balan's eyes and face all through; the wall soft. The camera is locked; it only breathes.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Balan looks down at the three sheets over his glasses, moving his eyes slowly from the first sheet to the third; his lips move without sound as he adds; his forearms rest on the desk.
3.0s to 5.4s: his right hand rises to his left chest pocket, takes out the black pen, and with one movement draws a circle round a figure on the third sheet at the bottom of the frame; he lays the pen across the sheet; his hand returns to the desk.
5.4s to 6.0s: he takes off his glasses with his right hand and holds them folded on the desk; he raises his eyes to the accountant, just left of the lens; his head comes to rest and stays still.
6.0s to 10.0s: he speaks his one Malayalam line, easy and reasonable, head steady.
10.0s to 12.0s (hold): he keeps looking at the accountant, eyebrows lifted a little as if the matter is settled; one slow breath out; his lips close and stay closed. The shot ends on his face.

THE CIRCLE (most important)
The choice is shown only by small, physical things: the eyes travel the three sheets twice, then stop on the third; the circle is drawn once, firm, without hesitation; the glasses come off slowly and are folded with one hand. As he looks up, the face is pleasant and reasonable, a businessman solving a small problem; only a tightening at the corner of his mouth and one swallow give him away. No sweat dripping, no wiping the brow, no rubbing his temples, no evil smile, no glance at the camera.

EYELINES
0.0s to 3.0s: Balan looks down at the sheets at the bottom of the frame over his glasses, head tilted down about 25 degrees, eyes moving left to right across the sheets. 3.0s to 5.4s: on the third sheet at the bottom right of the frame, where he draws the circle. 5.4s to 12.0s: his gaze rises to the accountant just left of the lens, level with his own eyes, head turned about 10 degrees to frame left, and holds through the line and the end hold. He never looks into the lens.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
0.0s to 12.0s: a very slight drift of a few millimetres in the frame as the camera breathes; 1.0s and 2.0s his slow breaths through the nose; 4.0s a small focus breath as he leans 10 cm over the sheet and back; 5.6s the gold rim of the glasses catches the desk lamp and the exposure dips a hair; 5.8s one swallow; 10.4s his breath out.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS

The glasses fold with a small click. The ceiling fan out of frame is off; the blinds hang still; the desk lamp is steady. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Smooth, practical, reassuring; a man who has talked himself into it before he says it. Balan's face is natural and unretouched: pores, old acne pitting, a matte sweat sheen, never polished (L145). Five fingers on each hand, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no villain theatrics.

LIGHTING
The green-shaded desk lamp at 2700K, low at frame right, warm on the sheets and up under his face, opened up with a soft, even frontal fill at about 4000K from the room so his face is readable; the overhead tube faint at 4000K; the walls behind fall to warm brown shadow; the window blinds dark. Never flat, never cold blue, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh. Only the sounds of this office.
0.0s to 12.0s: quiet office room tone; a wall clock ticking out of frame; far traffic of the town in the evening; 3.0s to 5.4s the pen clicks, scratches a circle on paper, and is laid down; 5.6s the glasses fold with a click; 10.0s to 12.0s room tone and his breath.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Balan speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal Malayalam accent; voice Brad; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
6.0s to 10.0s — Balan: [thoughtful] "ആളുകളെ കുറച്ചുകൂടി കുറഞ്ഞ ചെലവിൽ മാറ്റിയാൽ കണക്ക് ശരിയാകും."
Delivery: about 4.0 seconds, one sentence at an easy, reasonable pace, medium-low volume, a small pause after "ചെലവിൽ", pitch rising slightly and settling on the last word, the stress on "കുറഞ്ഞ".

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation round the desk lamp, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Teak brown, cream and pale-blue palette with a pool of warm lamplight.

CONSTRAINTS
Balan alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no hand, shoulder or head of the accountant at the frame edges. Two pens in his LEFT chest pocket at the start, one after 3.0s. No 1998 look: no black hair, no young face. No readable numbers, words or ship name anywhere. Exactly one uru photograph and one brass barometer; no religious pictures, no lamp, no idol in this office. No cash, no cheque book, no laptop, no phone on the desk. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No lettering, no subtitles, no logo anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Balan, once. Exactly three sheets of figures, one calculator, one black pen (circle drawn once at 3.0s to 5.4s, then laid on the third sheet), one pair of gold-rimmed glasses (taken off once at 5.4s). One desk lamp, one uru photograph, one brass barometer, one ship photograph. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 6.0s to 10.0s, head steady, eyes just left of the lens. Locked 75mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let it sound sensible, almost kindly, like simple arithmetic; the harm is in how easily it comes out.
```
