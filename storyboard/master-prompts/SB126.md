## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Sunset, 2026, the Alappad coast, Kerala: the sea-facing veranda of the house where Antony, known here as Rayan, is staying. He sits alone at a small table with a parcel of food a friend packed for him; the cane chair beside him is empty, kept for an old friend who is gone. He opens the food, looks at the empty chair, then out at the sea, and answers a voice he remembers with two quiet words; then he begins to eat. One continuous medium close-up of Antony alone, with the empty chair beside him, 14 seconds. Antony speaks one short line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Rayan's veranda, the same in every shot of SB119 and SB126)
Place: a deep, plain veranda of a two-storey coastal house: a red oxide floor, cool and smooth; a white-washed wall behind Antony with one tall wooden window, its green shutters open, the room inside in shade; a square white pillar at the left edge of the frame; a low parapet at the veranda edge behind the camera; beyond it, behind the camera, sand, a few coconut palms and the open Arabian Sea. Two cane chairs side by side, the same pair as before: Antony sits in one; the other stands 0.7 m to his left (at the right of the frame), empty, turned a little toward the sea. Between Antony and the camera, at his waist, a small square teak table, about 60 x 60 cm, with the parcel, one steel tumbler of water and nothing else.
Light: sunset; the sun low over the sea behind the camera, about 5 degrees above the horizon, warm 3200K, falling straight onto Antony's face and the white wall; soft 6500K blue sky light filling from above; long warm light on the red floor.
People in frame: only Antony.
Antony as Rayan: about 52, lean and strong, sun-darkened brown skin with deep lines at the eyes and across the forehead; close-cropped black hair greying at the temples; a full, neatly trimmed salt-and-pepper beard about 2 cm long covering the jaw and chin, thicker on the LEFT jaw where an old scar lies hidden under it; deep-set, dark, steady eyes; a plain off-white linen full-sleeve shirt, the top button open, sleeves rolled to the forearm; charcoal cotton trousers; a plain steel wristwatch on his LEFT wrist (on frame right as he faces the camera); no chain, no ring.
NOW: Antony sits upright in his cane chair behind the table, facing the sea and the camera; the closed banana-leaf parcel lies on the table in front of him, tied with its strip of banana fibre; his hands rest on the table edge either side of it; his eyes on the parcel.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Antony, the veranda, the empty chair and the parcel are built only from the words in MEMORY and THE EMPTY CHAIR: the one bearded man in frame, seated at the small table, who opens the food, speaks once and eats. Nobody else is in frame.

THE EMPTY CHAIR
A cane armchair of woven pale-brown rattan on a dark-stained wooden frame, about 85 cm high at the back, 60 cm wide, with flat wooden arms, the weave worn darker on the arms; a thin faded cream cushion on its seat, smooth and unpressed. It stands 0.7 m to Antony's left, at the right of the frame, its seat level with his, turned about 20 degrees toward the sea; its back and one arm are in frame, softer than his face, the low sun lighting its weave. Nobody sits in it, nothing is placed on it, and it never moves. Nothing is set on the table in front of it.
The parcel: one banana leaf folded into a flat block about 22 x 15 x 6 cm and tied with one thin strip of dried pale-brown banana fibre; untied and opened, the leaf lies flat on the table, glossy green, with white rice, a spoonful of thick red fish curry and one piece of fried fish on it.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 14 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 14 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera stands on the veranda just inside the parapet, between the table and the sea, 1.4 m in front of Antony, at his seated eye height of about 1.05 m, on a 50mm lens, placed so that his gaze toward the sea falls just left of the lens. Medium close-up in the wide frame: Antony from the top of his head to his mid-chest at the left-centre of the frame, his face about 36% of the frame height; the near edge of the table and his hands at the bottom edge; at the right of the frame, 0.7 m beside him, the back and one arm of the empty cane chair, soft; behind both, the white wall and the tall green-shuttered window, soft; the white pillar soft at the left edge. Sharp: his eyes and face; the empty chair a little soft; the wall soft. The camera is locked; it does not move, zoom or follow.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Antony looks down at the parcel; with both hands he pulls the knot of banana fibre loose and lays the strip aside; he unfolds the leaf, one flap and then the other, the food showing at the bottom edge of the frame.
3.0s to 5.0s: his hands rest on the table; he turns his head to his left and looks at the empty chair beside him, at the right of the frame; he holds there.
5.0s to 6.2s: he turns his head back, slowly, and looks out at the sea, just left of the lens; his head settles and is still; a breath.
6.2s to 7.6s: he speaks his one short Malayalam line, quietly, to the sea, head steady, hands still.
7.6s to 14.0s (hold): he keeps looking at the sea for two seconds; at 9.6s he looks down at the food; with the fingers of his RIGHT hand (on frame left) he gathers a little rice and curry into a small ball on the leaf and at 11.5s lifts it and eats; he chews slowly, looking out at the sea again. The shot ends on his face toward the sea, chewing, the empty chair beside him.

HIS FACE AT THE SEA (most important)
Only small, physical things: when he looks at the empty chair his eyes stay on it for two full seconds, steady, and his lips part very slightly and close; as he turns to the sea his chest rises on one slow breath; after the line the corners of his mouth lift a fraction, about 2 mm, and stay; his eyes are wet at the lower lids but no tear falls; he blinks slowly at 8.4s; chewing is slow and real, the jaw moving under the beard. No crying, no smile with teeth, no nod to the chair, no hand reaching toward the chair, no glance at the lens.

EYELINES
0.0s to 3.0s: Antony looks down at the parcel on the table at the bottom edge of the frame, head tilted down about 35 degrees. 3.0s to 5.0s: he looks at the empty chair at the right of the frame, 0.7 m to his left, head turned about 60 degrees to his left (toward frame right), gaze at the height of its cushion. 5.0s to 9.6s: he looks out at the sea behind the camera, just left of the lens, gaze level with the horizon, head turned about 10 degrees toward frame left. 9.6s to 11.5s: down at the food on the leaf. 11.5s to 14.0s: back out at the sea, just left of the lens, level with the horizon. He never looks into the lens.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A slight drift of a few millimetres all through; 1.0s the dry fibre rasps as the knot gives; 2.4s the leaf crackles as it opens; 4.0s a small focus breath as he turns to the chair; 5.6s one slow breath in; 7.6s a half-second of stillness after the line; 9.0s the low sun brightens a fraction as a thin cloud slides off it, a slight exposure lift on his face; 11.5s a small focus breath as he lifts his hand to eat.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The parcel, about 600 g, sits flat on the table; the fibre strip, once untied, lies curled where he drops it; the leaf springs back a little at the folds as it opens and stays open under its own weight. The steel tumbler of water does not move; its surface trembles once when his hand touches the table at 3.0s. A sea breeze of about 12 km/h comes from the sea behind the camera, lifts a few hairs at his temples and the open collar of his shirt, and moves the loose end of the cushion's cover on the empty chair a little. The rice ball, about 30 g, holds together in his fingers. Long shadows of the palms behind the camera move slowly across the floor. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A man who has finished what he came to do and found that it did not give him back what he lost, choosing, quietly, to go on living. Still, plain, unhurried; eating with his right hand in the Kerala way, simply. Real unretouched skin, deep lines, salt-and-pepper beard, five fingers on each hand, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no speech-making.

LIGHTING
The low 3200K sunset sun from the sea behind the camera falls straight onto his face: soft, warm, even frontal light, with the sea's reflected glare adding clear catchlights in both eyes; a 6500K blue sky fill from above keeps the shadows under his brow cool and open; the empty chair's weave catches the same warm light; the white wall behind glows warm, a shade darker than his face; the room through the open window in shade. Never flat, never orange all over, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh, no transition sound. The veranda and the coast only.
0.0s to 14.0s: waves breaking on the beach about 60 m away, the sea wind, palm fronds rustling; far off, ordinary evening life returning: a church bell ringing a few slow strokes in the distance, children calling to each other without words on the beach, an outboard engine coming in to shore, a dog barking once; 1.0s the fibre rasping; 2.4s the leaf crackling; 3.0s the tumbler's faint ring; 11.5s to 14.0s his quiet chewing.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Antony speaks one short line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal Malayalam accent; voice Mahendran J; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
6.2s to 7.6s — Antony: [softly] "നോക്കാം, അച്ഛാ."
Delivery: about 1.4 seconds, five syllables, quiet, low and even; a small pause at the comma; the stress on "നോക്കാം"; "അച്ഛാ" held a little longer and falling gently at the end.

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the sunset glare in his eyes and on the white wall, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: warm sunset gold on white lime and pale rattan, green leaf, red floor, cool blue in the shadows.

CONSTRAINTS
Antony alone in frame from the first frame to the last; the empty chair stays empty: no person, no ghost, no shadow of a person, no transparent figure, no hand, nothing placed on it. No second plate, cup or parcel on the table; nothing set in front of the empty chair. His hands are still while he speaks. He eats with his RIGHT hand only. No spoon, no fork, no newspaper, no plastic, no foil. No chain, no ring. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No subtitles or lettering anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Antony, once. Exactly two cane chairs, one empty at the right of the frame. One small table, one banana-leaf parcel, one strip of banana fibre, one steel tumbler, each in its place. His watch on his LEFT wrist. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 6.2s to 7.6s, head steady, eyes just left of the lens toward the sea. Locked 50mm medium close-up. Runs the full 14 seconds.

INTENT: Let the two words be a promise made to someone who is not there, small and private, and let the first mouthful after them be the first step back into a life.
```
