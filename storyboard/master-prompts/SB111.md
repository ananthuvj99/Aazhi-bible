Light: morning daylight through the blinds at frame right, 5600K; a row of flat ceiling panels at 4500K, soft and even.

Jaison: about 52, medium-tall, a little heavy at the middle, medium-brown skin with large pores on the nose and a slight oily sheen on the forehead; black hair dyed and combed back, thinning at the crown, grey at the roots above the ears; a neatly trimmed dyed-black moustache, clean-shaven chin; heavy-lidded, watchful eyes; a crisp white cotton full-sleeve shirt, sleeves folded once at the wrist, top button open; a thin gold chain with a small gold cross at the open collar; a gold wristwatch with a metal strap on his LEFT wrist.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Jaison is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one man in frame, at the long table, who looks at the contract, pushes it back across the table, sits back and speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
