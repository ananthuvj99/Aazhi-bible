## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
A bright morning, about 1985, on the flat cement terrace of a fisherman's house on the Alappad coast, Kerala. Three children sit on the warm floor around one shared school notebook, drawing; the girl, Clara, watches the boy beside her draw and asks him one short question in Malayalam, teasing him. The shot in one sentence: a low, locked medium close-up of Clara across the open notebook, her face lifting from the drawing to ask her line. Clara speaks one Malayalam line; nobody else speaks.
Spoken language: Malayalam. Every spoken word in this video is Malayalam.

MEMORY (the childhood terrace; the same words in every terrace shot, SB002–SB003)
Place: a flat roof terrace about 6 m by 4 m: grey cement floor, sun-warmed, with dark rain stains and fine cracks; a low whitewashed plastered parapet about 85 cm high with a flat top, its whitewash flaking in patches; beyond the parapet, out of focus, the crown of a coconut palm and a band of blue sea; a coir clothesline across one corner with one white and one blue cloth.
Light: morning sun from behind the camera and to the right, low enough to throw soft shadows across the floor; open blue sky.
People in frame: Clara only (about 7): thin, wheatish-brown skin, thick straight eyebrows, a calm direct gaze, black hair in two short plaits tied with red cotton ribbon, a faded yellow cotton frock with small white dots and a round collar, a thin black cord with a tiny silver cross, barefoot. The two boys sit just outside the frame: Antony beside the lens at the left, Jaison beside the lens at the right.
NOW: Clara sits cross-legged on the cement floor behind the open notebook, leaning forward on both hands, looking down at the page where Antony's hand is drawing just below and left of the frame.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
None attached. Clara, the terrace and the notebook are described in full in this prompt; build them only from these words. Exactly one girl in frame.

THE SHARED NOTEBOOK
One school notebook of the 1980s, about 21 cm by 17 cm, open flat on the cement floor between the three children, its brown paper cover curled at the corners and a little torn at the spine; ruled pages gone soft with handling. On the left page, in grey pencil: a small fishing vallam with three stick-men and oars; on the right page a huge fish drawn across the whole page, bigger than the boat, with scales, a big round eye and a smiling mouth, still being drawn. One short yellow pencil about 9 cm, chewed at the end, in Antony's hand off frame. In this shot the notebook is only the lower edge of the frame, soft; the drawing's paper edge and a few pencil lines show; the children's hands never cover Clara's face.

CLARA'S FACE (most important)
Small, real, observable movements only. 0.0s–3.0s: her eyes down on the page at the lower left, following the moving pencil; her eyebrows rise a fraction each time the fish grows; the corner of her mouth twitches; she blows a loose hair off her forehead once at 1.8s. 3.0s–4.0s: she lifts her face, chin coming up about 15 degrees, and looks at Antony beside the lens at the left; her eyes narrow with a small, crooked half-smile; she takes a quick breath. 4.0s–6.8s: the line, head steady, mouth fully visible, a teasing lift on the last word. 6.8s–10.0s: she keeps her eyes on Antony, waiting, the half-smile widening, then glances down once at the giant fish and back up at him, lips pressed together to hold in a laugh. Avoid: a big cartoon grin, eye-rolling, hands on hips, a posed tilt of the head, looking into the lens.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 10 seconds, real time, no cut. 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 10 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–10.0s: the camera sits low on the terrace floor at the height of Clara's seated face, about 0.7 m above the cement, about 1.3 m in front of her, on the other side of the notebook, looking straight at her; lens about 65mm; a medium close-up: her head and shoulders and the top of her frock, her face filling about a third of the frame height, a little left of centre; the notebook's edge soft along the bottom of the frame. Sharp: Clara's eyes and mouth; the parapet and the sea behind her soft. Locked and still for the whole shot; no push, no pan.
0.0s–3.0s: hold; Clara watches the drawing. 3.0s–4.0s: she lifts her face toward Antony beside the lens at the left. 4.0s–6.8s: she says her Malayalam line. 6.8s–10.0s: hold; she waits for his answer, then glances down at the fish and back up. The boys never enter the frame; no hand enters the frame.

EYELINES
0.0s–3.0s: Clara looks down at the page, at the moving pencil just below and left of the frame, gaze about 30 degrees below the lens. 3.0s–6.8s: Clara looks at Antony's face just left of the lens, at her own eye height, about 15 degrees left of the lens, while she speaks. 6.8s–8.5s: still on Antony's face. 8.5s–9.0s: down to the fish on the page at the lower left. 9.0s–10.0s: back up to Antony just left of the lens. She never looks into the lens and never at Jaison's side (the right).

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.
While the line is spoken, Clara is the only person in the frame.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
Clara breathes visibly at 1.0s and 3.6s (a quick in-breath before the line); a very slight drift in the frame, a few millimetres, at 2.0s and 8.0s; a small focus breath as she lifts her face at 3.0s, settling on her eyes by 3.4s; a cloud edge dims the sun by a fraction at 7.5s and lifts again at 9.0s; a two-beat pause after her line before she glances down.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
A sea breeze of about 10 km/h across the terrace from the right: the loose hairs at her forehead and the ends of her red ribbons lift and settle every 2–3 s; the corner of the notebook page lifts about 1 cm at 5.5s and drops back. Her weight rests on her two hands on the warm cement; her shoulders rise a little with each breath. The clothesline cloths behind her sway slowly in the breeze, soft. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A real 7-year-old girl, unselfconscious, quick and sure of herself, teasing the boy the way a younger friend does. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin with pores and a little dust on her cheek, five fingers on each hand, milk teeth with one gap. No posing, no performing for the camera, no adult mannerisms.

LIGHTING
Morning sun about 5600K from behind the camera and to the right, high enough to light her face softly, with a warm bounce of about 4500K off the sun-warmed cement floor filling under her chin and eyes; soft, even frontal light on her face for the line; the parapet behind her bright but holding detail; sky clear pale blue. No hard shadow across her mouth.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design.
Spoken language: Malayalam only — not English, not a translation. Clara speaks the exact Malayalam words in quotes below, in everyday Kollam coastal Malayalam pronunciation, and nothing else. No English words. Lip-sync exact; every word on camera; the line is spoken once.
4.0s to 6.8s — Clara, about 7, a clear, light little girl's voice, teasing, quick, the pitch rising toward the end, a small stress on "ഇത്രയും", normal speaking volume to a boy sitting next to her. About 2.8 seconds.
[playful] "നീ എന്താ ഇത്രയും വലിയ മീനിനെ വരയ്ക്കുന്നേ?"
Ambient, steady: the soft scratch of a pencil on paper (0.0s–3.0s and again from 7.5s), the sea breeze, distant surf from beyond the parapet, crows calling from the palm, a cloth flapping on the line, faint voices from the lane far below.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the bright parapet edge, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: faded yellow, whitewash, grey cement, sea blue.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of shot: the listener appearing behind the speaker (L34) — the boys stay out of frame; the speaker looking into the lens; English speech when the scene is described in English (L159) — every spoken word is Malayalam; a hand or pencil passing in front of her face; an adult face on the child; modern clothes, plastic toys, ballpoint pens, printed cartoon covers. Exactly one girl in frame; the notebook stays where it lies. No lettering on the notebook cover. No cut, no zoom, no camera move.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Clara, about 7, two short plaits with red ribbons, faded yellow dotted frock. Exactly one notebook, its edge soft at the bottom of the frame. One line in Malayalam, spoken once, 4.0s to 6.8s; no English words. Low locked medium close-up, about 65mm. Runs the full 10 seconds.

INTENT: When Clara speaks her Malayalam line, let it be a little friend's cheeky, delighted teasing — she has caught him making the fish far too big, and she loves it.
```
