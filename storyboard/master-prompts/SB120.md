```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Late morning, 2026, a small rented house on the Kollam coast, Kerala: the front room where Clara and her grown son now live. Antony, known to her only as Rayan, has come as a friend and offered help. She has just said what she fears; he answers in two words. One continuous medium close-up of Antony alone, 10 seconds. Antony speaks one short line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Clara's rented home, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a small front room: walls painted pale blue, the paint faded and stained near the floor; a cement floor polished grey; a small square wooden table with a flowered plastic cloth between Antony and Clara; behind Antony, on the wall, ONE small wooden crucifix about 25 cm tall above ONE small framed picture of Mother Mary, 20 x 25 cm, and beside them a wooden window with the shutter open onto a narrow sandy lane in bright light; a folded cotton towel on a wall hook at the right. Plain, clean, nothing new.
Light: late morning; 5600K daylight from the open window behind Antony at the left and from the open door at the right of the room (behind the camera); warm 3800K bounce off the walls.
People in frame: only Antony.
Antony as Rayan: about 52, lean and strong, sun-darkened brown skin with deep lines at the eyes and across the forehead; close-cropped black hair greying at the temples; a full, neatly trimmed salt-and-pepper beard about 2 cm long covering the jaw and chin, thicker on the LEFT jaw where an old scar lies hidden; deep-set, dark, steady eyes; a plain off-white linen full-sleeve shirt, sleeves rolled to the forearm; charcoal cotton trousers; a plain steel wristwatch on his LEFT wrist; no chain, no ring.
Off screen (never seen): Clara sits on the other side of the table, 1.2 m from him, just to the right of the camera.
NOW: Antony sits on a plain wooden chair at the table, leaning slightly forward, both forearms on the table, his hands loosely together beside a steel tumbler of black tea; his eyes on Clara.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Antony is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one man in frame, seated at the small table with a tumbler of tea by his hands, who speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

THE TUMBLER OF TEA
One plain stainless-steel tumbler, 10 cm tall, 7 cm across, slightly dented near the rim, about two-thirds full of black tea without milk, standing on a small round steel plate 12 cm across, on the flowered plastic table cloth beside his RIGHT hand (on frame left, since he faces the camera). A thin wisp of steam rises from it at first and fades. He does not drink from it in this shot; at 2.0s he moves it 3 cm aside with two fingers so his hands rest clear, then leaves it.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 10 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 10 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera sits at his seated eye height, about 1.1 m, 1.4 m in front of him, just left of where Clara sits, on an 85mm lens, so his gaze falls just right of the lens. Medium close-up: the top of his head to his mid-chest, his face about 40% of the frame height, a little left of centre; the tumbler at the lower left edge; behind him, soft, the crucifix and the Mother Mary picture at the right of his head, the bright open window at the left. Sharp: his eyes and face; the wall soft. The camera is locked.
0.0s to 2.5s (hold): Antony listens, looking at Clara just right of the lens; still; one breath. At 2.0s his right hand moves the tumbler 3 cm aside.
2.5s to 4.0s: he holds her eyes; a small pause while he chooses not to say more; his head comes to rest.
4.0s to 5.2s: he speaks his one short Malayalam line, low and plain, head steady, hands still.
5.2s to 10.0s (hold): he keeps looking at her, open and still; his hands rest on the table; one slow blink at 7.5s; nothing more is added. The shot ends on his face.

HIS PLAINNESS (most important)
Only small, physical things: his eyes stay soft and level; the corners of his mouth relax after the line, not into a smile; his hands lie open-palmed on the cloth after 5.2s, fingers apart, nothing held; his breathing is slow. No reassuring touch, no hand on the heart, no head tilt, no shake of the head, no knowing look, no glance at the camera. He must look like a man who wants nothing back.

EYELINES
0.0s to 10.0s: Antony looks at Clara, seated across the table just right of the lens, slightly lower than him, gaze about 5 degrees down, head turned about 10 degrees to frame right. At 2.0s his eyes do not follow his hand to the tumbler; they stay on her. He never looks into the lens, never at the crucifix behind him.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A slight drift of a few millimetres all through; 1.0s his breath; 2.0s the tumbler slides on the plastic cloth with a soft scrape and a tiny focus breath; 3.5s the window light lifts a little as a cloud clears; 5.2s a half-second of stillness after the line; 7.5s the blink.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The tumbler with tea weighs about 250 g; it slides 3 cm on the plastic cloth under two fingers and the tea surface ripples and settles. The steam rises 5 cm and thins in the still air. A light breeze through the window moves the hanging towel 2 cm. The wooden chair creaks once as he leans. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Simple, unhurried, giving no weight to his own words; a guest in someone else's home. Real unretouched skin, pores, deep lines, grey in the beard, five fingers on each hand, eyes never on the lens. No posing.

LIGHTING
5600K late-morning daylight from the open window behind him at the left as a soft rim on his hair and shoulder; 5600K daylight from the open door behind the camera softened to an even frontal light on his face; warm 3800K bounce from the pale-blue walls; the crucifix and the picture in soft light behind him. Never flat, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh, no transition sound. The room and the lane outside only.
0.0s to 10.0s: a quiet room; through the window a bicycle bell far down the lane, a crow, a hen clucking; 2.0s the tumbler scrapes softly on the cloth; 5.2s to 10.0s the lane sounds and his breath.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Antony speaks one short line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal Malayalam accent; voice Mahendran J; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
4.0s to 5.2s — Antony: [softly] "ഒന്നും വേണ്ട."
Delivery: about 1.2 seconds, four syllables, low volume, plain and even, the stress on "ഒന്നും", the pitch level and closing down at the end.

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation at the bright window, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Faded pale blue, grey cement and soft daylight.

CONSTRAINTS
Antony alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no shoulder, hand or saree of Clara at the frame edge; nobody passes the window. Exactly one crucifix and one Mother Mary picture on the wall; no Hindu lamp, no idol, no tulsi. Nothing expensive in the room; no envelope of money, no cheque, no phone on the table. Antony about 52 with a full salt-and-pepper beard, never the young face, no scar visible. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No lettering or subtitles anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Antony, once. Exactly one steel tumbler on one steel plate, moved once at 2.0s and left. One crucifix, one Mother Mary picture, one window, each fixed. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 4.0s to 5.2s, head steady, eyes just right of the lens. Locked 85mm medium close-up. Runs the full 10 seconds.

INTENT: Let the two words be plain and complete, so that nothing in his face asks for anything after them.
```
