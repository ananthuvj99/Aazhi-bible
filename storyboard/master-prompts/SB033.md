SCENE CONTEXT
Late at night, 1998, the open Arabian Sea off Kerala, in the water beside our wooden cargo uru, with the fishing vallam still burning behind. Emotion (platform tag, written once): exhales. Antony swims back to the uru on his side, his arm across the chest of an exhausted, half-drowned man in a soaked blue camouflage uniform, holding the man's face above the water; at the uru's side a coir rope comes down from the rail, Aashaan directs from above, and two crewmen haul the two of them in against the hull. One continuous shot, 15 seconds, at water level. Nobody speaks: only breathing, gasps and coughing.

MEMORY (the same night in every shot; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: the open sea beside our uru as in Aswanth's night: the uru's left side, a grey-blue weathered hull with long rust-brown streaks and a red-oxide band at the waterline, rising about 2.2 m from the water to the top of the solid bulwark with its broad flat brown top rail; the sea black-navy, slow swells about 1 m from crest to trough.
Light: a low-key night: very dark navy sky with stars; cold blue-grey night light about 7000K on the water; the burning vallam's orange light, about 1800K, from the right, flickering on the wet faces and the hull; one warm hurricane lantern glow, about 2000K, above the bulwark where the men stand. No moon in frame.
People in frame (each exactly as on his sheet, one of each): Antony: 24, lean, short black hair plastered flat, thin silver chain, the pale blue-grey striped short-sleeve shirt soaked dark and clinging. Selvan: about 45, lean, dark-brown skin, short black hair greying at the temples plastered to his head, thick black moustache, light stubble; long-sleeve shirt in light-blue and slate-blue horizontal tiger-stripe camouflage, soaked darker, open at the chest; a small clear glass vial on a black cord at his neck; khaki webbing belt under the water. Aashaan: about 60, grey hair combed back, grey moustache, cream shirt open over a white vest, white towel over his LEFT shoulder. Babu: heavy-set, red-and-white checked towel tied on his HEAD, off-white shirt, a thin red thread on his RIGHT wrist. Raghavan: thin, grey hair uncovered, white vest, red-and-white checked towel round his NECK.
Fire: the burning vallam about 40 m behind the swimmers, at the right of the frame on the water, flames along its whole length, smoke rising, its reflection a broken orange path toward the camera.
NOW: Antony on his left side in the water, towing Selvan on his back, about 8 m from the uru's hull; Aashaan, Babu and Raghavan at the left-hand bulwark above, leaning out, lit by the fire.

The carry: Antony lies on his left side in the water, his left arm passed over Selvan's left shoulder and across his chest, the hand gripping under Selvan's right armpit; Selvan's back against Antony's chest, the back of his head on Antony's left shoulder, his face up, chin and mouth just above the surface, eyes half closed. Antony swims a side-stroke: his right arm sweeps forward and pulls back under the water, his legs scissor-kick; each kick moves them about 60 cm. Selvan's arms trail; one hand rises once and grips Antony's forearm. As small real movements: 0.0s–3.0s Antony's mouth comes clear of the water on each stroke, a gasp in, water running off his lips; Selvan coughs twice, water spilling from his mouth. 3.0s–8.0s the hull grows at the left; at 6.0s a coil of coir rope, about 2.5 cm thick, is thrown down from the rail, uncoils in the air and slaps the water 1 m ahead of Antony. 8.0s–11.0s Antony's right hand grabs the rope, wraps it once round his wrist; the rope goes taut and dripping from the rail; they are drawn in against the hull, Antony turning his back to the hull to keep Selvan off the planks. 11.0s–15.0s Babu and Raghavan haul hand over hand; the two bodies rise against the hull, water pouring off them, Antony's feet finding the red-oxide band, his left arm still locked round Selvan. FORMAT MODE
21:9 cinematic widescreen. One continuous shot, 15 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. Runs the full 15 seconds.
