## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Morning, about 1985, the landing beach of the Alappad fishing village, Kerala. The boats are in and the catch is coming ashore: fish poured out on the sand, nets hauled, baskets carried, women bargaining. Into the middle of it the three children arrive at a run, weaving through the working crowd, and nobody stops for them. Emotion: excited. The shot in one sentence: a locked, eye-level wide along the busy waterline as the work goes on and the children run in from the right. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the coast of Antony's childhood; the same words in every shot of SB001–SB005)
Place: the pale grey-gold beach at the landing place: three black-tarred plank vallams drawn up on the sand at a slant, their bows toward the palms, about 6 m apart, the nearest at the left of the frame; wet brown cotton nets heaped and being pulled out of the middle boat; silver fish poured in shining heaps on the sand; plaited palm-leaf baskets and dented aluminium pans; behind, the line of coconut palms and the backs of small tiled cottages; the sea and the surf at the right edge of the frame.
Light: morning sun low from the sea at the right, about two hours up, long shadows pointing left across the sand.
People in frame: about fourteen working fishermen in faded lungis tied up at the knee, some bare-chested, some in old vests; about eight women in cotton sarees with the end tucked at the waist, some with baskets on their heads; the father (about 40: tall, broad-shouldered, sun-darkened, short black hair greying at the temples, thick black moustache, bare-chested, faded blue checked lungi folded up to the knee, a faded white thorthu tied round his head, a thin silver chain with a small cross) lifting a full basket at the nearest boat, left of centre; and from 5.0s the three children: Antony (about 10, faded sky-blue shirt, khaki half trousers), Jaison (about 10, white vest, maroon-checked half trousers), Clara (about 7, two plaits with red ribbons, faded yellow dotted frock), all barefoot.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@palmgreenwind (reference video, 0.0s–12.0s, movement only): exactly how the coconut palm leaves sway and flutter in the sea breeze; give every palm along the back of the beach that movement. Never copy its green background, its single tree or its look.
No other reference: the beach, the boats, the father and the children are written out in full in this prompt; build them only from these words.

THE CATCH
The morning's catch of a 1980s Kerala shore boat: oil sardines about 15 cm and Indian mackerel about 25 cm, silver-blue and still flipping, poured from the nets onto the wet sand in three heaps about 1 m across; a few bigger fish, one seer fish about 70 cm laid apart. Round plaited palm-leaf baskets about 55 cm across, dark and dripping at the bottom; dented aluminium pans carried on women's heads on a twisted cloth ring. Brown cotton nets with small cork floats, wet and heavy, sand stuck to them. Crows hopping at the edges. No plastic crates, no ice boxes, no tarpaulins, no weighing machines.

THE WORKING BEACH (most important)
The beach keeps working; every person has one job and does it at their own moment. 0.0s–2.5s: at the middle boat four men haul the wet net out hand over hand in one rhythm; at the right, a man tips a basket of sardines onto a heap and they spill and flip; a woman crouches to pick fish into her pan. 2.5s–5.0s: the father, left of centre at the nearest boat, bends, grips a full basket by its rim with both hands, and swings it up onto his right shoulder; two women lift a pan onto a third woman's head; a crow hops in and is shooed off by a flick of a cloth. 5.0s: the three children run into the frame from the right edge, along the wet sand, moving left: Antony first, Jaison, then Clara. 5.0s–9.0s: they weave between the fish heaps and the working adults, Antony dodging round the net-haulers, Jaison hopping over a stretched net, Clara ducking under a woman's arm; no adult stops working; a man lifts his basket higher to let Antony pass. 9.0s–10.0s: Antony slows as he nears the father at the nearest boat and the father's basket, at the left of centre. 10.0s–12.0s: hold: the children stopped by the nearest boat, catching their breath, looking at the father; the net still coming out, the fish still flipping, the women still picking.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–12.0s: the camera stands on the firm wet sand about 14 m up the beach from the nearest boat, at adult eye level (about 1.6 m), looking along the waterline with the boats at the left, the palms behind and the surf at the right edge; lens about 35mm; deep focus, sharp from the nearest fish heap (about 5 m away) to the farthest boat. Locked and still for the whole shot; no pan, no follow. In frame: the three boats, the working people, the heaps, the palms and cottages behind, a strip of surf at the right. Not in frame: the headland and its house (behind the camera). Action as in the block above; the children enter at the right edge at 5.0s and run toward the left, keeping that direction.

EYELINES
The net-haulers look down at the net in their hands and at the boat. The women look at the fish and at each other's pans. The father looks down at the basket while he lifts it (2.5s–4.0s), then ahead along the beach toward the left; from 9.5s he turns his head and looks down at Antony arriving at his right. The children look ahead at the nearest boat and the father, level, then at the obstacles at their feet as they dodge; from 9.5s all three look up at the father. Nobody looks into the lens.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A very slight drift in the frame at 2.0s and 9.0s; wet fish scales and wet sand flash small points of sun all through; a small exposure dip at 5.0s as the children cross close in the bright foreground; the haze over the palms softens the back; breath visible in the children's chests and shoulders from 10.0s.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
A wet net about 40 kg pulled by four men, coming out about 0.5 m per heave; a full basket about 18 kg — the father's knees bend and his back straightens to lift it; a woman's pan about 10 kg settles on her head ring and she steadies it with one hand. Fish flip on the sand for a few seconds then lie still. Sea breeze about 15 km/h from the sea at the right: palm fronds bend toward the left and flutter, saree ends and the father's lungi edge lift, smoke-free air smelling of salt. Footprints dent the wet sand and fill with water. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
An ordinary working morning, practised and unhurried, people who do this every day; the children's energy cutting through it. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin, real sweat, sand on feet and shins, five fingers on every hand that grips a net or basket. No posing, nobody performs for the camera, no crowd freezing.

LIGHTING
Morning sun about 5000K low from the sea at the right, side-lighting the people and boats from the right, long shadows pointing left across the sand; open blue-white sky fill about 6500K on the shadow sides; strong sparkle on wet fish and wet sand; the palms behind in soft haze. Never noon-flat, never orange all over.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design.
No spoken words and no voices; no clear speech; only an indistinct murmur of a working crowd under the effects, nothing that can be read as words.
0.0s–12.0s: surf at the right, gulls and crows, the wet slap of the net on the boat's side, fish flapping on sand, a basket tipping out with a rush of fish, the creak of the boats' wood; 5.0s–10.0s bare feet slapping on wet sand, quick children's breathing; 10.0s–12.0s the children panting.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the sparkling fish and wet sand, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: silver fish, black-tarred wood, brown nets, faded cotton colours, pale sand.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of shot: palms frozen (L157); the crowd moving in sync or freezing; children running in a different direction from the shot before (keep screen direction, L161: right to left); modern boats, motors, plastic crates, ice boxes, tarpaulins, rubber boots, wristwatches, printed T-shirts; a second father look-alike; faces close and posed. Exactly three boats, exactly three children, one father. No lettering anywhere, no painted names on the boats. No cut, no zoom, no camera move.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One continuous 12-second locked wide along the waterline. Exactly three drawn-up vallams, the nearest at the left. Exactly one father, lifting one full basket onto his right shoulder at 2.5s. Exactly three children, entering at the right edge at 5.0s and stopping by the nearest boat by 10.0s. Every palm moving. Nobody speaks. Runs the full 12 seconds.
```
