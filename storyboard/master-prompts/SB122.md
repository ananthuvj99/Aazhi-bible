## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Morning, 2026, a small fisherman's house on the Alappad coast, Kerala. Aashaan, very old and bedridden, lies propped up in a cot that has been turned so he can see the sea through the window. Holding the hand of the visitor beside him, he asks after a boy he once knew. One continuous medium close-up of Aashaan alone, 12 seconds. Aashaan speaks one line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Aashaan's room in 2026, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a small room with lime-washed walls gone grey-white, a few damp patches; a plain wooden cot with a thin cotton mattress and a white sheet; behind Aashaan, three pillows stacked against the cot's wooden headboard, and above it on the wall one old framed black-and-white photograph of a wooden sailing uru, too soft to read in detail; a steel water tumbler and a strip of tablets on a small stool at the left; a faded blue cotton curtain tied back at the right. The window with its wooden shutters wide open is in front of him, behind the camera; through it, the open sea.
Light: morning; soft 5600K daylight and sea glare from the open window in front of him (behind the camera), even and bright on his face; the room behind him dimmer, 4500K.
People in frame: only Aashaan.
Aashaan: about 88, very thin, the skin of his face and neck loose and deeply lined, sun-darkened with many age spots; thin white hair combed straight back from the forehead; a thick white moustache, yellowed a little at the edges; white stubble; sunken cheeks; clouded, watery eyes that still look straight at a person; a white sleeveless cotton vest, loose on his thin chest; a white cotton towel folded over his LEFT shoulder (on frame right, since he faces the camera); a pair of reading spectacles hooked into the vest's neckline on his left side; a white sheet over him to the waist.
Off screen (never seen): Antony as Rayan sits on a wooden chair pulled close to the left side of the cot, 0.8 m from Aashaan's face, just to the right of the camera; Aashaan's LEFT hand, below the frame, holds Antony's hand on the sheet.
NOW: Aashaan lies propped half upright on the three pillows, his head raised and upright, turned toward Antony; his left hand holds Antony's hand below the frame; his eyes on Antony's face.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Aashaan is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one very old man in frame, propped upright on pillows in the cot, the towel on his left shoulder, who speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

THE PILLOWS AND THE HANDHOLD
Three soft cotton pillows in faded white covers, stacked against the wooden headboard so his head and shoulders are raised about 45 cm above the mattress, his head upright, not lying sideways. His LEFT hand, thin and spotted, holds Antony's hand on the sheet at his waist, below the bottom edge of the frame; the hold is never seen, only felt: his left shoulder (frame right) and forearm lean very slightly down toward it, and at 7.5s his shoulder moves as he squeezes. No hand rises into frame.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera is at the bedside beside Antony's chair, at Aashaan's eye height about 1.0 m, 1.1 m from him, on a 75mm lens, so Aashaan's gaze falls just right of the lens. Medium close-up: the top of his head to his upper chest, the towel on his left shoulder at the right of the frame, his face about 42% of the frame height, a little left of centre; behind him, soft, the stacked pillows, the headboard and the framed uru photograph. Sharp: his eyes and face; the wall soft. The camera is locked.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Aashaan looks at Antony, just right of the lens; his breath is shallow and visible; his lips move once without sound and close.
3.0s to 4.2s: he rests a moment between breaths, eyes on Antony; he gathers breath; his head is still.
4.2s to 6.8s: he speaks his one Malayalam line, slow, thin, with a small breath in the middle, head steady on the pillows.
6.8s to 12.0s (hold): he keeps looking at Antony, waiting for an answer; at 7.5s his shoulder moves as his hand squeezes; his eyes fill; at 9.5s his gaze drifts for a moment past the lens toward the window and the sea, then comes back to Antony. The shot ends on his face.

HIS EYES (most important)
Only small, physical things: the watery eyes blink slowly, about every 4 seconds; the eyebrows lift a little in the middle as he asks; his lower lip trembles once after the line; a single tear gathers on the lower lid of his right eye and stays there, it does not run. His breathing is shallow, the chest rising about 1 cm. No sobbing, no hand to the face, no head shaking, no smile, no glance at the camera.

EYELINES
0.0s to 9.5s: Aashaan looks at Antony, seated just right of the lens at the same height, head turned about 15 degrees to frame right, gaze level. 9.5s to 10.5s: his gaze drifts a little further right and up, past the lens, toward the open window and the sea behind the camera. 10.5s to 12.0s: back to Antony, just right of the lens. He never looks into the lens.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A slight drift of a few millimetres all through; his shallow breaths every 2 seconds, visible at the vest; 3.5s a slight exposure lift as the sea glare brightens; 5.4s the small breath inside the line; 6.8s a half-second of stillness; 7.5s the squeeze shows in his shoulder; 9.5s a small focus breath as his eyes move to the window.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
His thin body, about 45 kg, sinks into the pillows; they hold their shape. A sea breeze of about 10 km/h through the open window behind the camera lifts a few strands of his white hair and the edge of the towel on his shoulder, and sways the tied-back blue curtain at the right. The vest moves with each shallow breath. The tear stays on the lid by surface tension. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Very old, very weak, entirely clear in the eyes; a man who has waited a long time for news. Speech slow, between breaths. Real unretouched skin, deep lines, age spots, loose skin at the neck, five fingers on each hand, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no theatrical ageing.

LIGHTING
Soft 5600K morning daylight and sea glare from the open window in front of him, behind the camera: an even frontal light on his face with bright catchlights from the window in both eyes; the room behind him dimmer at 4500K; the white towel and vest bright but holding detail. Never flat, never glossy, never a hospital look.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh, no transition sound. The room and the sea through the window only.
0.0s to 12.0s: waves breaking on the beach about 60 m away through the open window, the sea wind, gulls and a crow far off; his shallow breaths, a faint rasp; 7.5s the cot creaks; 6.8s to 12.0s the sea and his breath.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Aashaan speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with an old Kollam coastal Malayalam accent; voice Rinoy, older and breathier; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
4.2s to 6.8s — Aashaan: [sad] "അവൻ എന്നെ മറന്നുകാണുമോ?"
Delivery: about 2.6 seconds, nine syllables, slow, thin and low in volume, a small breath after "എന്നെ", the stress on "മറന്നുകാണുമോ", the pitch rising a little at the end as a real question.

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation in the window catchlights and on the white towel, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Bright sea light on grey-white lime and white cotton.

CONSTRAINTS
Aashaan alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no hand, arm or shoulder of Antony or anyone in frame; the handhold stays below the frame. He is propped upright, never lying flat or on his side. The towel on his LEFT shoulder only. Exactly one framed photograph on the wall, of a sailing uru, unreadable. No hospital bed, no drip, no oxygen tube, no monitor. No tear running down. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No lettering or subtitles anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Aashaan, once. Three pillows, one wooden headboard, one framed uru photograph, one tied-back curtain, each fixed. The towel on his left shoulder all through. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 4.2s to 6.8s, head steady, eyes just right of the lens. Locked 75mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let the question be an old man's real fear, asked of the one face that cannot answer it, so the silence after it belongs to the listener.
```
