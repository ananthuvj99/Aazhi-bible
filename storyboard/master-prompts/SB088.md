SCENE CONTEXT
Evening, January 2005, the front veranda of a small tiled house on a red laterite headland above the sea, on the Kerala coast. The emotional situation: Antony has come back to his mother's house as a stranger, found it locked, and has just heard from the neighbour at her window that the woman who lived here died three years ago; he must not show who he is. The shot: one locked medium close-up of Antony on the veranda, 12 seconds; after a pause he asks one short question in Malayalam. Only Antony speaks.

MEMORY (the same house and evening in every shot of this scene; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: the house exactly as in the reference, seven years older and shut up: pale sky-blue walls now faded and streaked with black monsoon stains, the deep blue base band chipped; a front veranda with four turned white pillars, their paint flaking, a red-oxide floor gone dull and dusty, dry palm leaves and brown leaves blown into the corners; the blue wooden double door in the centre, closed, its paint peeling at the bottom, with a NEW shiny brass padlock about 7 cm tall hanging from an iron hasp at chest height; one small dark wooden cross on the wall above the door; one small framed picture of Mother Mary, faded behind dusty glass, on the wall to the right of the door; the window right of the door with blue iron bars, its white shutters closed from inside. No chair, no bench: the veranda is empty. A spider web in the top corner of the door frame.
Light: the sun low in the west, 10 minutes before sunset, behind the camera; warm golden light across the veranda; the sky above the roof turning from pale gold to soft blue.
People in frame: Antony only: about 31, lean, brown skin; black hair cut short and neat; a full black beard about 3 cm long that hides most of a pale scar on his LEFT jawline (on the right of the frame when he faces us); a plain dull-olive half-sleeve cotton shirt, untucked, a little creased; faded dark-grey cotton trousers; worn black rubber sandals. No bag, no watch, no chain.
NOW: Antony stands on the veranda 60 cm from the locked door, at its left side, his body three-quarters to the camera; his right hand (on the left of the frame) has just let go of the padlock and hangs at his side; his left hand rests flat on the white pillar beside him. He faces the neighbour's window, which is just left of the camera, beyond the low compound wall at the west.

One closed blue double door, one new brass padlock, one cross, one Mother Mary picture. One question in Malayalam from 6.0s to 8.0s. Runs the full 12 seconds.
