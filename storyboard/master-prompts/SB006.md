## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Morning, about 1985, the landing beach of the Alappad fishing village, Kerala. The father has just set his basket of fish down on the sand beside his boat; Antony crouches beside it, and his friend Jaison has just made a remark about the size of the fish. The father, down on one knee by the basket, answers his son with one teasing Malayalam line, half a dare. Emotion: playful. The shot in one sentence: a low, locked medium close-up of the father on one knee behind the basket, turning from Jaison to his son and speaking. The father speaks one Malayalam line; nobody else speaks.
Spoken language: Malayalam. Every spoken word in this video is Malayalam.

MEMORY (the landing beach; the same words in every shot of SB005–SB007)
Place: the pale grey-gold beach at the landing place; behind the father, soft: the black-tarred side of his drawn-up plank vallam with a faded red and white band, a wet heap of brown net in it, and beyond it, out of focus, working people, palms and the morning sky.
Light: morning sun low from the sea, at the right of the frame and a little behind the camera.
People in frame: the father only (about 40): tall, broad-shouldered, sun-darkened skin, short black hair greying at the temples, thick black moustache, crow's feet at the eyes, bare-chested with salt drying on his shoulders, a faded white cotton thorthu tied round his head, a thin silver chain with a small cross, a faded blue checked lungi folded up to the knee. The two boys crouch just outside the frame: Antony beside the lens at the left, Jaison beside the lens at the right.
NOW: the full basket sits on the sand in front of him; he has just come down on his right knee behind it, both hands on its rim; his son Antony crouches by the basket to the left of the lens.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
None attached. The father, the basket and the beach are described in full in this prompt; build them only from these words. Exactly one man in frame.

THE BASKET
One round plaited palm-leaf fish basket, about 55 cm across and 30 cm deep, the weave dark and dripping at the bottom, a twisted rim of split cane; filled to the brim with silver oil sardines about 15 cm and Indian mackerel about 25 cm, a few still flipping, scales shining. In this shot only its rim and the top layer of fish show, soft, along the bottom of the frame; the father's two hands rest on the rim, low, well below his face. No plastic, no crate, no ice.

THE FATHER'S FACE (most important)
Small, real, observable movements only. 0.0s–2.5s: he settles on his knee, breath still heavy from carrying, chest rising; he wipes his forehead with the back of his right wrist once at 0.8s, then puts the hand back on the basket rim; he looks down at the fish, a small satisfied tuck at the corner of his mouth. 2.5s–3.2s: he glances up at Jaison beside the lens at the right, eyebrows lifting, one corner of the moustache rising: he has heard the remark. 3.2s–4.0s: he turns his head to Antony beside the lens at the left, a slow sly grin, his eyes creasing, a short breath in. 4.0s–6.6s: the line, head steady, mouth fully visible under the moustache, a small lift of the chin on the last word as a dare. 6.6s–10.0s: hold: he keeps his eyes on his son, the grin staying, eyebrows up, waiting; at 8.5s he gives one small nod toward the sea behind the camera and back to Antony. Avoid: a wink at the lens, a booming laugh, a pointing finger, a stern face, looking into the lens.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 10 seconds, real time, no cut. 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 10 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–10.0s: the camera sits low on the sand at the height of the father's face as he kneels, about 1.0 m above the sand, about 1.4 m in front of him on the other side of the basket, looking straight at him; lens about 65mm; a medium close-up: his head, his shoulders and the top of his chest with the chain, his face filling about a third of the frame height, just right of centre; the basket rim soft along the bottom of the frame. Sharp: his eyes and mouth; the boat and the beach soft behind. Locked and still the whole shot.
0.0s–2.5s: hold; he settles and looks at the fish. 2.5s–3.2s: he glances at Jaison. 3.2s–4.0s: he turns to Antony. 4.0s–6.6s: he says his Malayalam line. 6.6s–10.0s: hold; he waits, nods once toward the sea. The boys never enter the frame; no hand but his enters the frame.

EYELINES
0.0s–2.5s: the father looks down at the fish in the basket at the bottom of the frame, about 35 degrees below the lens. 2.5s–3.2s: at Jaison's face just right of the lens, at a crouching child's eye height, about 15 degrees right of the lens. 3.2s–6.6s: at Antony's face just left of the lens, the same height, about 15 degrees left of the lens, steady through the line. 8.5s: a short glance past the right side of the camera toward the sea, then back to Antony at 9.0s. He never looks into the lens.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.
While the line is spoken, the father is the only person in the frame.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
His heavy breathing visible 0.0s–2.0s, slowing; a quick breath in at 3.8s before the line; a very slight drift in the frame at 1.5s and 8.0s; a small focus breath as he turns his head at 3.2s, settling on his eyes by 3.6s; sparkle off the wet fish in the lower frame flickering all through; a short pause after the line before the nod.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The basket about 18 kg, just put down, settles a centimetre into the wet sand; a sardine flips once at 1.5s and once at 7.0s. He has just carried it about 15 m, so his chest rises fast and slows over 3 s; a drop of sweat runs from his temple at 2.0s. A sea breeze about 12 km/h from the right lifts the loose end of his head thorthu and flutters it. Water drips from the basket's bottom onto the sand. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A strong, easy-going fisherman teasing his boy with love: the dare is warm, the eyes do the smiling. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin with pores, salt and sweat, sun-cracked lips, five fingers on each hand on the rim. No posing, no grand gestures.

LIGHTING
Morning sun about 5000K low from the right and a little behind the camera, lighting his face softly and evenly; warm bounce about 4500K off the pale sand filling under his moustache and eyes; skylight fill about 6500K on his left side; the boat behind in soft shade. No hard shadow across his mouth; even frontal light for the line.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design.
Spoken language: Malayalam only — not English, not a translation. The father speaks the exact Malayalam words in quotes below, in everyday Kollam coastal Malayalam pronunciation, and nothing else. No English words. Lip-sync exact; every word on camera; the line is spoken once.
4.0s to 6.6s — the father, a warm, deep, slightly rough fisherman's voice, half-teasing and half a dare, unhurried, normal volume to a boy crouching a metre away, the stress on "നീ", a little rise on the last word. About 2.6 seconds.
[playful] "ആ വലിപ്പം വേണേൽ നീ വന്നു പിടിക്കണം."
Ambient, steady: surf at the right, gulls and crows, fish flapping in the basket, water dripping on sand, the working beach behind as a soft wordless murmur.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the wet fish and the bright sky, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: sun-darkened skin, silver fish, faded blue check, black-tarred wood.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of shot: the listener appearing behind or beside the speaker (L34) — the boys stay out of frame; a face looking into the lens; an English line when the scene is written in English (L159) — every spoken word is Malayalam; hands rising in front of the face during the line; a modern fishing outfit, a wristwatch, a plastic crate. Exactly one man in frame, one basket. No lettering. No cut, no zoom, no camera move.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: the father, on his right knee behind the basket, white thorthu on his head, thin silver chain with a small cross. Exactly one basket of fish, its rim soft at the bottom of the frame. One line in Malayalam, spoken once, 4.0s to 6.6s; no English words. Low locked medium close-up, about 65mm. Runs the full 10 seconds.

INTENT: When the father speaks his Malayalam line, let it be a fond dare thrown to his own boy, a grin under the moustache — the first time the sea is offered to him.
```
