SCENE CONTEXT
Mid-morning, 1998, inside a small one-room village tuition centre at Alappad, Kollam, Kerala. A class of young pupils is doing sums; their teacher's sweetheart has just appeared at the door and the children are giggling at him. Clara, the young teacher, stops it: one continuous locked-off medium close-up, 8 seconds: half turned to the chalkboard, she looks back over her shoulder at the giggling class, squares up to them, swallows a smile and says one Malayalam line in a mock-stern teacher's voice. Only Clara speaks, only in Malayalam; she is alone in the frame.
SPOKEN LANGUAGE: Malayalam. Every spoken word in this video is Malayalam, the exact Malayalam text given in quotes in AUDIO. No English words are spoken.

MEMORY (the same classroom as the shots before; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: the front wall of the tuition room as in the reference: a large black chalkboard, about 2.4 m wide and 1.2 m high, in a plain dark wooden frame with a narrow chalk ledge, fixed on a pale-yellow lime-washed wall with soft stains and a faint tide mark of damp near the floor; the wall's plaster rough and worn. On the board, in white chalk, handwritten and uneven: three rows of simple sums, digits with plus and equals signs only, no words (for example 12 + 7 = , 25 + 14 = , 9 + 9 = ), the lowest row half rubbed out in a grey smudge; a short stub of white chalk on the ledge. These sums are the only marks in the frame.
Light: soft mid-morning daylight through the open door on her left and the barred windows behind the camera; bounced warm off the pale-yellow walls.
People in frame: Clara only, exactly as on her sheet: about 20, slim, wheatish-brown skin, calm direct gaze, thick straight eyebrows; black hair with a centre parting in one long thick plait down her back; a light sky-blue cotton saree with a thin white border, the pallu over her LEFT shoulder (screen right when she faces us), a matching light sky-blue short-sleeve blouse; a thin gold chain with a small gold cross; small gold stud earrings; thin wooden-bead bangles on her RIGHT wrist (screen left), thin gold bangles on her LEFT wrist (screen right).
NOW: Clara stands just in front of the teacher's table, close to the board, her shoulders turned toward the board and her face turned back over her right shoulder toward the class; the children sit at their desks in front of her, out of frame; the door is on her left, out of frame.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 8 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion, 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 8 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera stands in the aisle between the front benches, about 1.4 m from Clara, at her standing eye height (about 1.55 m), level, locked off, a 50mm lens. Framing: a medium close-up from mid-chest up, her face filling about a third of the frame height, a little right of centre; behind her the board and the pale-yellow wall. Sharp: her eyes and mouth; the chalk sums readable and slightly softer. Not in frame: the children, the door, Antony, the teacher's table top.
