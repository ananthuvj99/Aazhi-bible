SCENE CONTEXT
Night, 1998, in the cargo hold of our wooden uru at sea, a little after the rescue. Selvan, still damp and hurt, propped against the cargo sacks, a tin tumbler of water beside him, holds out a small sealed packet to Antony, who kneels just beside the camera, out of frame. Selvan speaks one long, tired line, in Malayalam; when he finishes, Antony's hand comes in and takes the packet without opening it. One continuous shot, 15 seconds, a medium close-up of Selvan. Spoken language: Malayalam only.

MEMORY (the hold; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: the uru's cargo hold under the deck: rough dark timber, a low plank ceiling about 1.8 m above the floor crossed by heavy beams, curved ribs of the hull behind; jute cargo sacks stacked two and three high along the side, tied with coir twine; Selvan sits on the plank floor with his back against the lowest sacks. Behind him, soft, a plain wooden ladder rises at the right to a square hatch, through which a patch of navy night sky shows.
Light: exactly one brass hurricane lantern hanging from a beam above the space between Selvan and the camera, just out of the top of the frame, about 2200K, warm and low, lighting Selvan's face softly from the front; deep brown shadow in the corners; a faint cold blue night spill about 7000K through the open hatch at the back right.
People in frame: Selvan: about 45, lean, dark-brown skin, short black hair greying at the temples, still damp and pushed back, thick black moustache, light stubble, tired eyes; long-sleeve shirt and cargo trousers in light-blue and slate-blue horizontal tiger-stripe camouflage, damp and creased, shirt open at the chest; a small clear glass vial on a black cord at his neck; khaki webbing belt with two canvas pouches. His left trouser leg is torn open below the knee and his left shin is bound with a strip of white cotton cloth, a brownish stain on it, the leg stretched out flat. The only face in frame, the only person who speaks.
NOW: Selvan propped against the sacks, his left leg out straight; a dented tin tumbler of water on the floor beside his right hip, half full; the sealed packet in both his hands at chest height; Antony kneeling just left of the camera, out of frame, at his eye height.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@selvan (full): Selvan, the rescued man and the only speaker, sitting against the cargo sacks, holding the flat sealed packet out toward Antony just left of the lens; uniform colours exactly as the sheet, light-blue and slate-blue tiger stripes, never green; the packet exactly as the one on his sheet. 100% matches the reference.
@antony20 (full, partial in frame from 12.5s: only his right hand, wrist and the hem of his pale blue-grey striped short sleeve, coming in from the lower left; never his face): Antony, silent. 100% matches the reference.

THE SEALED PACKET
A flat packet about 14 cm by 9 cm and 1 cm thick, wrapped in tan paper inside a layer of clear, crinkled plastic against the sea, tied crosswise with thin white string knotted in the middle; a few handwritten lines in blue ink on its face, blurred and too small to read; one corner damp and darker. Selvan holds it upright in both hands at chest height, about 25 cm below his chin, its face toward Antony, the thumbs on the front and the fingers behind; it never rises in front of his mouth. At 12.5s Antony's right hand takes it by the long edge; Selvan's fingers stay on it for half a second before letting go.

SELVAN'S FACE (most important)
Observable movements only. 0.0s–2.5s: he looks at the packet in his hands, then lifts his eyes to Antony just left of the lens; a slow breath; the corners of his mouth press in. 2.5s to 12.0s: he speaks slowly, in short phrases with small breaths between, head steady, face about 20 degrees left of the lens, mouth fully visible; on the first word his brows lift a little; in the middle his eyes drop to the packet for a second and come back; near the end the lower lids tighten and his voice thins, but no tears fall. 12.0s–15.0s: mouth closed; as Antony's hand comes in, his fingers loosen; a small nod; his head sinks back against the sack behind him, eyes still on Antony, a long breath out through the nose. Avoid the clichés: no clutching the packet to the heart, no kissing it, no trembling hands, no crying.

FORMAT MODE
21:9 cinematic widescreen. One continuous shot, 15 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. Runs the full 15 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s to 15.0s: the camera is on the hold floor about 1.3 m in front of Selvan, a little to the left, lens height 0.9 m (his seated eye height), a 50mm lens, locked; in frame: Selvan from mid-chest up, slightly right of centre, the packet in his hands at the bottom of the frame, the sacks behind him, the ladder and the hatch soft at the right; not in frame: Antony's face, the lantern, the tumbler (below the frame). Sharp: Selvan's eyes; the background soft. 0.0s to 2.5s silence; 2.5s to 12.0s he speaks; 12.0s–12.5s pause; 12.5s Antony's right hand enters from the lower left, about 1 m from the lens, and takes the packet by its long edge; 13.0s the hand withdraws out of the lower left with it; 13.0s–15.0s Selvan rests his head back.

EYELINES
Selvan: 0.0s–1.0s down at the packet in his hands; 1.0s–12.0s at Antony's face, just left of the lens at his own eye height, about 1 m away; at about 7.0s a one-second drop of the eyes to the packet and back; 12.5s–13.0s down at the packet leaving his hands; 13.0s–15.0s back to Antony, just left of the lens. Never into the lens.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.
Only Selvan speaks; Antony's face is never in frame. The line is spoken in Malayalam, never in English.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A very slow sway in the frame and the hanging lantern's light as the hull rolls. 2.0s: a breath lifts his chest. 5.0s and 9.0s: small breaths between phrases. 7.0s: a tiny focus breath as his eyes drop. 12.5s: the hand entering shades the lower frame and the exposure dips a hair. 14.0s: the lantern light swings 5 cm across his face and back.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The uru, about 30 tonnes, rolls 2 to 3 degrees on a long swell every five seconds; the lantern hanging on a 30 cm wire swings with each roll and its light moves across the sacks; the water in the tumbler tilts. Damp cloth clings to Selvan's shoulders; a drop of water runs from his hairline down his temple at 4.0s. The packet weighs about 60 g: it lies light in his fingers. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Selvan: a hurt, worn-out man trusting a stranger with something that matters to him; quiet and plain. Antony: only his hand, gentle and unhurried. No two people move at the same moment. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin, pores, stubble, five fingers. No posing.

LIGHTING
Exactly one hurricane lantern, about 2200K, hanging from the beam between Selvan and the camera, just above the frame: soft, even, warm frontal light on Selvan's face so his mouth reads clearly; falloff into deep brown shadow on the sacks and the ribs of the hull; a faint cold 7000K blue in the hatch at the back right. No other light source.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, and no cinematic sound design: no riser, no whoosh, no swell, no rumble, no transition sound. Ambient at a steady level that does not build.
0.0s–15.0s: the hull creaking, water sloshing along the outside of the planks, the lantern's wire ticking as it swings, faint footsteps on the deck above; Selvan's tired breathing; 12.5s the crinkle of plastic as the packet changes hands.
Spoken language: Malayalam only, not English and not a translation. Selvan says the exact Malayalam words in quotes below, in plain everyday coastal Malayalam pronunciation, and nothing else. Lip-sync exact; only Selvan speaks. The line is spoken once, exactly as written. Emotion tag for the scene, written once: [softly]
2.5s to 12.0s: Selvan, a calm, measured, tired man's voice, low and slow, about 4.5 syllables a second; his name first, then a short pause; a small apologetic rise on "തോന്നല്ലേ"; stress on "ഭാര്യയ്ക്കുള്ളതാ"; a breath before "നാട്ടിലെത്തുമ്പോൾ"; the last word falls and fades. About 9.5 seconds.
"സെൽവൻ. ബുദ്ധിമുട്ടിക്കുകയാണെന്ന് തോന്നല്ലേ. ഒരു സഹായം ചെയ്യണം. എന്റെ ഭാര്യയ്ക്കുള്ളതാ. നാട്ടിലെത്തുമ്പോൾ ഇതൊന്നു പോസ്റ്റ് ചെയ്യണം."

STYLE
Photoreal 1998 Kerala period feature film, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation around the lantern glow, visible skin texture and pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Warm lantern-lit hold in deep brown shadow. Every frame looks like the film itself, with nothing from the making of it.

CONSTRAINTS
Exactly one face in frame, Selvan's; Antony is only a hand and a sleeve hem from 12.5s; nobody else in the hold. The packet is never opened, never unwrapped, never held in front of Selvan's mouth; its writing is never readable. Exactly one lantern (L155), out of frame. The uniform stays light-blue and slate-blue tiger stripe, never green; the glass vial at his neck stays. No hand near the camera (L150): Antony's hand comes in at Selvan's distance. No English words; Malayalam only. One line, once. No cut, no zoom. No lettering anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One continuous 15-second locked medium close-up of Selvan in the hold, 50mm. Exactly one man's face, once; exactly one sealed packet, tied with string, handed over at 12.5s; exactly one tin tumbler; exactly one lantern. Selvan says "സെൽവൻ. ബുദ്ധിമുട്ടിക്കുകയാണെന്ന് തോന്നല്ലേ. ഒരു സഹായം ചെയ്യണം. എന്റെ ഭാര്യയ്ക്കുള്ളതാ. നാട്ടിലെത്തുമ്പോൾ ഇതൊന്നു പോസ്റ്റ് ചെയ്യണം." once, in Malayalam, 2.5s to 12.0s, head steady, eyes just left of the lens. Only Selvan speaks, only in Malayalam. Runs the full 15 seconds.
INTENT: a tired, hurt man setting his pride aside and putting the one thing that matters into a stranger's hands, quiet and plain; spoken in Malayalam with the calm of a man used to the sea.
