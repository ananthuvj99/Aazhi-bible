## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Evening, 2026, the deep veranda of a large house facing the sea on the Alappad coast in Kerala. Two men alone go through copies of old letters a woman agreed to share; one of them has just mentioned an old criminal case. Rayan closes the folder and sets the limit of what they are doing. One continuous medium close-up of Rayan alone at the veranda table, 14 seconds. Rayan speaks one line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Rayan's veranda, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a deep veranda with a floor of old red oxide tiles; round white-painted pillars; behind Rayan the whitewashed front wall of the house with ONE tall wooden window, its two shutters closed, painted a faded blue-green, and beside it at frame left ONE small plain wooden cross, 30 cm, fixed to the wall with ONE small red electric devotional lamp on a little wooden bracket below it, lit; at frame right a white pillar and beyond it, out past the veranda edge, a slice of dusky sky; a low teak table between the two men with two cane chairs.
Light: evening after sunset: the last cool blue dusk from the open side of the veranda at frame right, about 7000K; one warm bulb in a glass shade on the veranda ceiling above the table, 2800K; the small red lamp a tiny 1800K point.
People in frame: only Rayan.
Rayan (Antony as Rayan): about 52, lean and strong, sun-darkened brown skin with deep lines at the eyes and across the forehead; close-cropped black hair greying at the temples; a full, neatly trimmed salt-and-pepper beard about 2 cm long covering the jaw and chin, thicker on the LEFT jaw where an old scar lies hidden under it; deep-set, dark, steady eyes; a plain off-white linen full-sleeve shirt, the top button open, sleeves rolled to the forearm; charcoal cotton trousers; a plain steel wristwatch on his LEFT wrist; no chain, no ring.
Off screen (never seen): Muthu sits in the other cane chair across the low table, just right of the camera.
NOW: Rayan sits forward in his cane chair, forearms on his knees; on the low table in front of him, at the bottom of the frame, lies an open clear plastic folder of photocopied letters with a small open notebook beside it.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Rayan is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one man in frame, in the cane chair at the veranda table, who closes the folder of letters and speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

THE FOLDER OF COPIES
One clear plastic document folder, 33 x 24 cm, with a white snap button, holding about twelve photocopies of old handwritten letters: grey-white paper, the copied handwriting faint and grey, far too small and soft to read, the shapes of airmail folds and stamps copied as grey ghosts. Beside it one small notebook, 15 x 10 cm, with a brown cover, open on a page with a short column of dates in blue ink, unreadable. They lie on the low teak table, 45 cm high, at the bottom of the frame. Rayan closes the folder's flap with his RIGHT hand (on frame left, since he faces the camera), presses the snap button shut with his thumb, and lays his hand flat on it. The notebook stays open. No original letter, no old police file, no newspaper anywhere.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 14 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 14 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera sits 1.6 m in front of Rayan across the low table, at his seated eye height about 1.0 m, on an 85mm lens, directly beside where Muthu sits, so Rayan's eyeline passes just to the right of the lens. Medium close-up: from just above his head to the middle of his chest, his forearms and the edge of the table with the folder at the bottom of the frame; his face filling about 40% of the frame height, a little left of centre; the closed blue-green shutters and the wooden cross with its small red lamp soft behind him at frame left; the white pillar and dusky sky soft at frame right. Sharp: Rayan's eyes and face all through; the wall soft. The camera is locked; it only breathes.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Rayan sits forward, eyes down on the copies; he has just heard Muthu begin about the old case; his eyes stay on the paper a moment too long; he does not move.
3.0s to 5.6s: his right hand closes the folder's flap and presses the snap shut with his thumb; the hand lies flat on the folder; he straightens a little in the chair.
5.6s to 6.2s: he raises his eyes to Muthu just right of the lens; his head comes to rest and stays still.
6.2s to 11.2s: he speaks his one Malayalam line, quiet and steady, in two parts with a short pause, head steady, hand flat on the folder.
11.2s to 14.0s (hold): he keeps looking at Muthu; one slow breath out; his lips close and stay closed; the hand stays on the folder. The shot ends on his face.

THE SNAP SHUT (most important)
What the old case means to him is hidden, and shown only by small, physical things: at the start his eyes stay on the paper half a second longer than they should; his thumb presses the snap once, firmly; when he looks up, his face is composed and courteous, but the lower lids tighten by about 1 mm and the muscle at the hinge of his LEFT jaw moves once under the beard before he speaks. His voice stays even. No flinch, no sharp look, no hand to the beard or the jaw, no looking away to the sea, no tears, no glance at the camera.

EYELINES
0.0s to 5.6s: Rayan looks down at the folder at the bottom of the frame, head tilted down about 30 degrees. 5.6s to 14.0s: his gaze rises to Muthu just right of the lens, level with his own eyes, head turned about 10 degrees to frame right, and holds through the line and the end hold. He never looks into the lens, never at the cross, never out to the sea.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
0.0s to 14.0s: a very slight drift of a few millimetres in the frame as the camera breathes; 1.5s his breath held; 3.8s a small focus breath as he straightens and settles sharp on the eyes by 5.8s; 4.2s the plastic folder catches the bulb and the exposure dips a hair; 6.0s his short breath in; 8.6s the short pause inside the line; 12.0s his slow breath out; the red lamp glows steadily all through.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The folder of copies weighs about 150 g; the flap folds over with a soft plastic crackle and the snap clicks once. The cane chair creaks as he straightens. A light evening sea breeze of about 10 km/h comes through the open side of the veranda: it lifts the corner of the notebook page 5 mm and lets it fall and stirs the loose collar of his shirt about 1 cm. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Calm, careful, private; a man keeping a door closed in himself while he speaks plainly. Real unretouched skin with lines, pores and the grey in the beard, five fingers on each hand, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no brooding hero.

LIGHTING
The warm veranda bulb at 2800K above and slightly in front of him gives soft, even frontal light on his face; cool blue dusk at about 7000K from the open side at frame right edges the right side of his face and shoulder; the small red lamp an 1800K point behind him at the left; the whitewashed wall warm near the bulb and falling to blue-grey at the edges. Never flat, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh. Only the sounds of the veranda at dusk.
0.0s to 14.0s: the sea washing on the beach beyond the veranda, waves every 8 to 10 seconds; the evening breeze in the palms; crickets beginning; a far church bell rings twice at 1.0s and stops; 3.0s to 5.6s the folder crackles shut and the snap clicks; the cane chair creaks; 11.2s to 14.0s the sea and his breath.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Rayan speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal Latin Catholic Malayalam accent kept soft by years abroad; voice Mahendran J; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
6.2s to 11.2s — Rayan: [thoughtful] "അതു തുറക്കുന്നില്ല. ഈ ആളുകൾക്ക് സംഭവിച്ച കാര്യമാണ് അന്വേഷിക്കുന്നത്."
Delivery: about 5.0 seconds, two sentences, low and even volume, a short pause after "തുറക്കുന്നില്ല", pitch level and falling at each full stop, the stress on "തുറക്കുന്നില്ല" and on "ഈ ആളുകൾക്ക്".

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation round the bulb and the red lamp, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Whitewash, red oxide and off-white linen palette, warm against blue dusk.

CONSTRAINTS
Rayan alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no hand, shoulder or head of Muthu at the frame edges; no hand near the camera. No scar visible on his jaw: the beard covers it. No readable writing on the copies or the notebook; no police file, no newspaper, no photograph of anyone. Exactly one wooden cross and one small red electric lamp on the wall; no oil lamp, no Hindu lamp, no idol, no tulsi. No 1998 look. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No lettering, no subtitles, no logo anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Rayan, once. Exactly one clear folder of copies (closed once at 3.0s to 5.6s, his hand on it to the end), one small open notebook, one low teak table. One wooden cross, one red lamp, one pair of closed shutters, one white pillar. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 6.2s to 11.2s, head steady, eyes just right of the lens. Locked 85mm medium close-up. Runs the full 14 seconds.

INTENT: Let it sound like a simple, practical boundary set for the work, calm and certain; underneath, a man who will not let his own story take the place of theirs.
```
