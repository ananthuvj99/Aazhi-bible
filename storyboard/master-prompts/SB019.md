SCENE CONTEXT
Hazy morning, 1998, an old cargo harbour at Cochin, Kerala. Emotion (one, for the whole shot): excited. A few workers move cargo along a stone quay lined with old tiled warehouses; Antony's wooden cargo uru waits moored at the far end. One continuous locked-off wide shot, 10 seconds: Antony walks onto the quay with his bag and keeps walking along it toward his uru, his eyes on it, eager for the voyage. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the harbour morning; the same in every harbour shot)
Place: the quay as in the reference: a stone quay along the RIGHT half of the frame, its dark stone wall dropping into the water, edged with worn granite blocks; old two-storey warehouses behind it with sloping red clay-tile roofs, lime-washed walls stained grey and ochre, wooden shutters and wide plank doors; on the quay stacked wooden crates, burlap sacks, coils of coir rope and two wooden handcarts; small wooden canoes moored along the near quay wall; the water on the LEFT half, calm and glittering; a low palm-lined shore across the water at the far left; at the far end of the quay, the uru moored with its bow toward the view, and two small boats beyond it.
Light: hazy morning sun, about 5000K, low and in front-left of the view; glitter on the water; warehouse doorways in cool shade.
People in frame: Antony, exactly as on his sheet: 24, lean, sun-browned, short black hair combed neat; a short-sleeve shirt with thin vertical lavender-grey and white stripes, sleeves rolled once; a grey-green checked mundu; a thin silver chain; barefoot; the beige canvas bag's strap over his LEFT shoulder, the bag at his left hip. Exactly five dock workers, small: white or faded shirts and vests, checked lungis folded to the knee, barefoot, one with a towel round his head.
NOW: the five workers at their tasks; the near right end of the quay empty for Antony; nobody on the uru's deck readable.

ACTIVE REFERENCES

The uru is about 28 m long and lies about 110 m from the camera, bow on, its mast about 14 m tall; two coir mooring ropes, thick as a wrist, run from its bow to iron bollards on the quay and sag and lift with the water. The canoes along the near wall are about 6 m long, dark wood, tied by short ropes, knocking gently. Nothing on the quay, the warehouses or the boats carries any lettering.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 10 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion, 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 10 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera is low over the water just off the near corner of the quay, about 3 m above the water and 2 m out from the quay wall, looking along the quay toward the moored uru; locked off, level horizon, a 32mm lens. Water on the left half, quay and warehouses on the right half. Sharp: everything, from the near canoes to the uru and the far shore (deep focus). Not in frame: the open sea, the town, the sky above the warehouse roofs beyond a thin strip.
0.0s to 2.5s — Hold: the harbour works. Two workers carry burlap sacks on their heads from the warehouses toward the uru; one pushes a handcart of crates away along the quay; one stacks crates by the warehouse doors; one coils rope near the quay edge. The canoes rock; light glitters on the water.
2.5s to 7.5s — Antony steps onto the quay from the right edge of the frame, beside the near handcart, and walks away from the view along the quay straight toward his uru, his back three-quarters to the view, his head up and turned a little left toward the uru, with a loose natural gait: heel to toe on the stone, weight rolling from foot to foot, shoulders relaxed, his right arm swinging, his left hand on the bag strap. 7.5s to 10.0s — Hold on the frame: he keeps walking at the same easy pace past the stacked crates, smaller in the frame, about a sixth of the frame height by the end, still heading for the uru and looking up at its mast; the workers keep at their tasks around him.

The sacks, about 50 kg each, press the carriers' heads down and slow their steps; the handcart, about 150 kg loaded, rolls at walking pace, its iron-rimmed wheels bumping over the stone joints. The moored canoes and the uru rise and fall a few centimetres on a slow swell every 4–5 s; the mooring ropes sag and tighten with it. A light breeze, about 8 km/h, from the water ruffles the surface and Antony's shirt; his mundu swings with each step; the bag sways and settles. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

Anticipation in the body: a quickened, purposeful walk, chin up, eyes on the uru; at ease among the workers, he belongs here. The workers are busy and ordinary. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin, five fingers. No posing.

Hazy morning sun, about 5000K, low and in front-left of the view: bright glitter on the water at the left; soft shadows falling toward the right across the quay; the warehouse doorways in cool shade, about 7000K; a warm rim along Antony's left shoulder and hair; the haze softens the far shore and lifts the shadows a little.

AUDIO

No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, and no cinematic sound design: no riser, no whoosh, no swell, no rumble, no transition sound. Ambient at a steady level that does not build. No spoken words and no voices; every mouth stays closed.

STYLE
Photoreal 1998 Kerala period feature film, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the glittering water, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Warm hazy morning palette.

The uru never moves, never comes closer and never looks like a modern fishing boat; one uru only (L51). Exactly five workers, none of them turns to the camera. The bag strap stays on his LEFT shoulder, never carried in front (L137). The moored boats rock (L138). No lettering, flags or numbers anywhere. No cut, no zoom, no camera move. Nobody speaks.

Antony enters from the right edge at 2.5s and is still walking toward the uru at the end. Locked 32mm wide from low over the water. Runs the full 10 seconds.
