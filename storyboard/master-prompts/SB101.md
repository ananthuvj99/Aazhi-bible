## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Sunday lunch, early afternoon, 2026, a large house facing the sea on the Alappad coast in Kerala. Three families share a table; ordinary hospitality is making strangers familiar. Clara, Jaison's wife, looks across the table at their host and a passing doubt crosses her face. One continuous medium close-up of Clara alone, seated at the table, 12 seconds. Clara speaks one short line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Rayan's dining room, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a long, bright dining room: whitewashed walls, a floor of old red oxide tiles polished to a soft shine; a long teak dining table for ten with a plain white cotton tablecloth; high-backed teak chairs with cane seats; behind Clara, on the white wall at frame left, ONE plain dark wooden crucifix about 40 cm tall; at frame right behind her a tall wooden window with its two shutters open on a veranda, coconut palms and a strip of bright sea beyond, soft; one ceiling fan turning slowly above, out of frame.
Light: early afternoon, the sun high and behind the house; bright, soft daylight from the open window at frame right, 5600K, bounced off the white walls and tablecloth; no hard sun on the faces.
People in frame: only Clara.
Clara: about 48, slim, wheatish-brown skin with fine lines at the corners of her eyes and two faint lines across the forehead; calm, direct eyes under thick straight eyebrows; long black hair with a few grey strands, centre parting, tied in a low loose knot at the nape; thin gold chain with a small gold cross at her throat; small gold stud earrings; a muted bottle-green cotton-silk saree with a thin gold border, pallu over her LEFT shoulder, matching short-sleeve blouse; a few thin gold bangles on her LEFT wrist, a thin gold ring.
Off screen (never seen): Rayan sits at the head of the table, just left of the camera; Jaison beside Clara at her left, out of frame; the others further down the table, out of frame.
NOW: Clara sits upright at the long side of the table; in front of her a white plate with red rice, fish curry and a banana-leaf parcel half opened; her right fingers rest on the rim of the plate; a steel water tumbler at the top right of her plate.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Clara is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one woman in frame, seated at the lunch table, who stops eating, looks at the host for a moment and asks one question. Nobody else is in frame.

THE LUNCH TABLE
A white cotton tablecloth on teak, 75 cm high. In front of Clara: one plain white ceramic plate, 27 cm across, with a mound of Kerala red matta rice, a ladle of red fish curry with two pieces of seer fish, a little cabbage thoran, and one banana-leaf parcel of pearl-spot fish half unfolded, the leaf scorched brown at the edges; one plain steel tumbler of water, 10 cm tall, beaded with condensation, at the top right of the plate; one folded white cloth napkin at her left. The table edge and her plate fill the bottom of the frame. She eats with her RIGHT hand, Kerala style; her RIGHT hand is on frame left, since she faces the camera. Nothing else is on the table in frame: no wine, no bottles, no phones.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera sits 1.8 m from Clara across the corner of the table, at her seated eye height about 1.15 m, on an 85mm lens, directly beside where Rayan sits at the head, so Clara's eyeline passes just to the left of the lens. Medium close-up: from just above her head to the middle of her chest with the plate at the bottom of the frame; her face filling about 40% of the frame height, a little right of centre; the crucifix soft on the wall at the left, the open window, palms and bright sea soft at the right. Sharp: Clara's eyes and face all through; the wall and window soft. The camera is locked; it only breathes.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Clara gathers a little rice and curry with her right fingertips, lifts it, eats, and lowers her hand to the rim of the plate; her eyes on her plate; a half-listening face, as if others are talking down the table.
3.0s to 6.0s: she lifts her eyes to Rayan, just left of the lens, by chance; she stops chewing; her eyes stay on him for a full three seconds; her brows draw together about 2 mm, her head tilts about 5 degrees to her right; her right fingers stay still on the plate rim.
6.0s to 6.3s: her head comes upright and stays still.
6.3s to 9.1s: she speaks her one Malayalam line, light and genuinely unsure, head steady, eyes on Rayan.
9.1s to 12.0s (hold): she waits for his answer; one small breath; then the corner of her mouth lifts in a polite half-smile, as if she has decided it is nothing, and her eyes drop back to her plate at 11.0s; her lips stay closed. The shot ends on her lowered eyes.

THE LOOK (most important)
The doubt is shown only by small, physical things: her chewing slows and stops; the eyes narrow about 1 mm at the lower lids while she looks; a tiny tilt of the head; the brows draw together and release; one slow blink. There is no recognition: no widening eyes, no gasp, no hand to the mouth, no pale face, no tears. It is the look of someone trying to place a face from long ago and giving up. Never a glance into the lens.

EYELINES
0.0s to 3.0s: Clara looks down at her plate at the bottom of the frame, head tilted down about 25 degrees. 3.0s to 9.1s: she looks at Rayan, just left of the lens, level with her own eyes, head turned about 10 degrees to frame left, holding through the line. 9.1s to 11.0s: still on Rayan, waiting. 11.0s to 12.0s: her gaze drops back to her plate. She never looks into the lens and never toward the window.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
0.0s to 12.0s: a very slight drift of a few millimetres in the frame as the camera breathes; 1.5s a small focus breath as her hand rises to her mouth and returns; 4.0s her breathing pauses for half a second while she looks; 6.1s her small breath in before the line; 10.0s the light flickers softly as a palm frond outside crosses the sun on the veranda and the exposure lifts a hair.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The rice and curry, about 30 g, sit in her fingertips; a drop of curry stays on the plate rim where her fingers rest. The steel tumbler sweats; one drop of condensation runs 2 cm down its side. The ceiling fan above moves the air at walking pace: a loose strand of her grey-black hair at the temple and the edge of the pallu stir about 1 cm; the palms outside sway slowly in the sea breeze. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Natural, relaxed, a guest at a pleasant lunch; the moment passes almost as soon as it arrives. Real unretouched skin with fine lines and pores, five fingers on each hand, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no dramatic recognition, no stare held too long.

LIGHTING
Bright, soft daylight at 5600K from the open window at frame right, wrapping her face with soft, even frontal light bounced off the white tablecloth and walls; a gentle warm fill at about 4500K from the room; the sea and sky beyond the window held bright but not burnt white; mild shadow behind her on the wall at the left round the crucifix. Never flat, never cold blue, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh, no sting on the look. Only the sounds of this room.
0.0s to 12.0s: quiet room tone of a large house; the ceiling fan turning; the sea faint through the window; soft clinks of steel and china from further down the table, no words understood; a crow far off outside; 3.0s to 6.0s the table sounds fall away under her look but continue faintly; 9.1s to 12.0s room tone.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Clara speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal Latin Catholic Malayalam accent; voice Aadhira; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
6.3s to 9.1s — Clara: [curious] "നിങ്ങളെ ഞാൻ എവിടെയെങ്കിലും കണ്ടിട്ടുണ്ടോ?"
Delivery: about 2.8 seconds, fifteen syllables at a light, easy pace, soft-to-medium volume, the pitch rising gently on the last word as a real question, the stress on "എവിടെയെങ്കിലും"; no break inside the line.

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation round the bright window, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. White, teak and bottle-green palette with a strip of bright sea.

CONSTRAINTS
Clara alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no arm, shoulder or head of Jaison or Rayan at the frame edges. No 1998 look: no plait down her back, no sky-blue saree, no young face. She eats with her right hand only; no spoon, no fork. Exactly one crucifix on the wall; no Hindu lamp, no idol, no tulsi, no oil lamp anywhere. No wine, no bottles, no phone on the table. No gasp, no tears, no recognition. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No lettering, no subtitles, no logo anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Clara, once. Exactly one white plate, one banana-leaf parcel, one steel tumbler, one folded napkin, each where it lies. One crucifix on the wall, one open window. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 6.3s to 9.1s, head steady, eyes just left of the lens. Locked 85mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let the question float out lightly, half to herself, the way people ask it at every crowded table, and let it be dropped as easily as it was asked.
```
