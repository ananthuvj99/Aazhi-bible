## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Late morning, 2026, a small fisherman's house on the open sand of the Alappad coast, Kollam, Kerala; the house still stands, lived in. On its veranda the man of the house sits with a folded bundle of papers in his hands and, looking up at his wife in the doorway, says how it will be from now on. One continuous medium close-up of the resident alone, 12 seconds. The resident speaks one line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (the resident's veranda, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: the narrow front veranda of a small one-storey house of plastered concrete, about 2.2 m deep, under a sloping roof of old red clay tiles on dark wooden rafters; the floor of polished red oxide, worn pale where people walk; the front wall behind him painted sky blue, faded and chalky, with a damp line low down; in that wall, at the left of the frame, a plain wooden front door painted dark green, open, the room inside in shade; at the right of the frame, a small barred window with green wooden shutters half open; on the blue wall above his head, a little to the right, one small framed picture of Mother Mary in blue robes, soft and out of focus, and beside it one small plain wooden crucifix on a nail, about 20 cm tall; a low plastered parapet runs along the veranda edge behind the camera; beyond it, out of this frame, the sandy yard, coconut palms and the sea.
Light: late morning, about 11 a.m.; bright 5600K sun on the sand of the yard in front of the veranda, behind the camera; the veranda in open shade under the tiles, lit by soft bounce from the sunlit sand, about 5000K; the inside of the open door dark.
People in frame: only the resident.
The resident: about 62, a fisherman, broad-shouldered and a little heavy at the middle; very dark sun-burnt brown skin with deep lines across the forehead and at the eyes, real pores, a small healed scar through his right eyebrow; short grey hair cut close, receding at the temples; a thick grey moustache, clean-shaven chin with a day of grey stubble; heavy-lidded, steady dark eyes; a white half-sleeve cotton shirt, washed thin, the top two buttons open, a thin brown wooden rosary showing at his neck; a white mundu with a thin green border; barefoot. A plain steel watch on his LEFT wrist.
Off screen (never seen): his wife stands in the open front door, 1.5 m away, at his left (frame left), just to the left of the camera, at standing height.
NOW: he sits on a low wooden stool against the blue wall, his back straight, his knees apart; on his knees a sheaf of folded papers in a clear plastic sleeve, held in both hands; his eyes on the papers.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. The resident, his veranda and the papers are built only from the words in MEMORY and THE PAPERS: the one older fisherman in frame, seated on a stool on his own veranda with the papers on his knees, who speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

THE PAPERS
A sheaf of about twelve sheets of white A4 paper, folded once down the middle, about 21 x 15 cm folded, about 8 mm thick, slipped into a clear, slightly crumpled plastic sleeve with a press-stud at one end. The outer page is blank, turned outward; no letters, no seal, no photograph is ever readable. The sheets are a little soft and dog-eared at the corners from being handled many times. He holds the sleeve flat on his knees with both hands, his thumbs on top, at the bottom edge of the frame; he raises it once, in the pause before the line, to chest height and lowers it again; it never comes near his face.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera stands on the veranda floor near the parapet edge, 1.3 m in front of him and a little to the left, at his seated eye height of about 0.95 m, on a 65mm lens, so his gaze, lifted to his wife in the doorway, falls just left of the lens and a little above it. Medium close-up: from the top of his head to his mid-chest, his face about 40% of the frame height, a little right of centre; the edge of the open green door soft at the left of the frame, the blue wall, the small Mother Mary picture and crucifix soft above his head at the right, the half-open green shutter soft at the right edge. Sharp: his eyes and face; the wall soft. The camera is locked; it does not move, zoom or follow.
0.0s to 2.2s (hold): he looks down at the papers on his knees; his thumbs smooth the plastic sleeve once; a breath.
2.2s to 3.0s: he lifts the sleeve a little, to chest height at the bottom of the frame, and taps it once against his other palm, then lowers it to his knees and his hands go still; he lifts his eyes and head to his wife.
3.0s to 7.2s: he speaks his one Malayalam line, slow and plain, as a decision, head steady, hands still on the papers.
7.2s to 12.0s (hold): he keeps his eyes on his wife for two seconds; at 9.2s he gives one small nod; at 10.0s he looks down at the papers again and flattens the sleeve with his right hand. The shot ends on his face looking down, his jaw set.

HIS FACE AFTER THE LINE (most important)
Only small, physical things: after the last word his lips press together and the corners of his mouth turn down slightly; the muscle at the hinge of his jaw tightens once, visibly; his nostrils widen on one slow breath out; his eyebrows lower a little and stay low; one slow blink at 8.0s. The nod at 9.2s is small, about 2 cm. No smile, no anger, no raised voice, no finger pointing, no fist, no hand to the chest, no glance at the lens.

EYELINES
0.0s to 2.8s: he looks down at the papers on his knees at the bottom edge of the frame, head tilted down about 30 degrees. 2.8s to 10.0s: he looks up at his wife in the doorway, just left of the lens and a little above it, head turned about 15 degrees to frame left and lifted about 10 degrees. 10.0s to 12.0s: back down at the papers. He never looks into the lens.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A slight drift of a few millimetres all through; 1.0s the plastic sleeve crinkles under his thumbs; 2.6s the tap of the papers on his palm; 3.0s a small breath in before the line; 5.2s a small pause inside the line; 7.2s a half-second of stillness; 8.5s a slight exposure lift as a cloud moves off the sun and the sand outside brightens the veranda; 10.0s a small focus breath as he looks down.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The sheaf, about 60 g in its sleeve, bends a little over his knees; the sleeve crinkles where his thumbs press it. A sea breeze of about 10 km/h comes in from the yard behind the camera, lifts the loose edge of the plastic sleeve and the collar of his shirt, and stirs the half-open shutter at the right, which knocks once against its stop at 6.0s. The stool, under his weight of about 75 kg, creaks once when he leans forward at 10.0s. Dust drifts in the bright air beyond the veranda edge. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A man who nearly lost his house to a signature and has decided, quietly, that it will not happen again. Plain, slow, certain, no speech-making. Real unretouched skin, sun-burnt and lined, five fingers on each hand, thick fisherman's fingers with cracked nails, eyes never on the lens. No posing.

LIGHTING
Open shade under the tiled roof: soft, even 5000K frontal light on his face bounced up from the sunlit sand of the yard behind the camera, with clear catchlights in both eyes; the sunlit yard itself (out of frame) at 5600K; the blue wall behind him softly lit, a shade darker than his face; the inside of the open door dark. The white shirt bright but holding its weave. Never flat, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh, no transition sound. The veranda and the coast only.
0.0s to 12.0s: the sea breaking on the beach about 80 m away, the wind in the palms, crows and a hen in the yard, children's voices far off down the lane without words; 1.0s and 10.0s the plastic sleeve crinkling; 2.6s the soft tap of the papers; 6.0s the shutter knocking once; 10.0s the stool creaks.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. The resident speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal Malayalam accent of an older fisherman; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
3.0s to 7.2s — Resident: [thoughtful] "ഇനി ആരെങ്കിലും പേപ്പർ കൊണ്ടുവന്നാൽ ആദ്യം വായിച്ചിട്ടേ ഒപ്പിടൂ."
Delivery: about 4.2 seconds, about twenty-two syllables, slow and even, medium-low volume; a small pause after "കൊണ്ടുവന്നാൽ"; the stress on "വായിച്ചിട്ടേ"; the pitch falls flat and final on "ഒപ്പിടൂ".

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the white shirt and the bright yard edge, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: faded sky-blue wall, dark green wood, red oxide floor, white cotton, dark skin.

CONSTRAINTS
The resident alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no hand, arm or shoulder of his wife or anyone else in frame; nobody in the doorway. The papers stay on his knees except for the one tap at 2.6s; they never cover his mouth; no readable text, seal, photograph or letterhead on any page. His hands are still while he speaks. Exactly one Mother Mary picture and one crucifix on the wall, soft; no other religious item, no Hindu item, no calendar with writing. No demolition, no machines, no officials. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No subtitles or lettering anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: the resident, once. One stool, one sheaf of papers in one plastic sleeve, one open green door, one half-open shutter, one Mother Mary picture, one crucifix, each in its place. His watch on his LEFT wrist, the rosary at his neck. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 3.0s to 7.2s, head steady, eyes just left of the lens. Locked 65mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let it land as a lesson learned the hard way, said without heat, so the stillness after it shows a man who will not be caught twice.
```
