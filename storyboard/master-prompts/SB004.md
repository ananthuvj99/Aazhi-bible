## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Morning, about 1985, a sandy lane of the Alappad fishing village, Kerala, running down between coconut palms and small tiled cottages to the beach. The three children race down it to meet the boats; Clara, the smallest, ducks through a gap in a fence, cuts through a yard and comes out ahead of the two boys. Emotion: happy. The shot in one sentence: a low, locked wide from the beach end of the lane as the children race toward us and Clara wins by her short cut. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the coast of Antony's childhood; the same words in every shot of SB001–SB005)
Place: the lane exactly as in @villagelane: a narrow lane about 3 m wide of pale sand and red earth, running straight away from the camera for about 30 m and bending left at the top; tall coconut palms on both sides; at the left, a faded blue tiled cottage with dark fishing nets hanging on bamboo poles beside it, and in front of it a woven palm-leaf fence about 1.4 m high with one gap about 50 cm wide, 18 m from the camera; nearer, at the left, a low mossy laterite wall about 1 m high with an open gap 4 m from the camera; at the right, a cream cottage and a faded pink cottage behind a woven bamboo fence and a wooden picket gate; a wooden electric pole at the right.
Light: morning sun from behind the camera and to the right (from the sea), low, throwing long palm shadows up the lane.
People in frame: Antony (about 10: lean, short black hair with a cowlick, faded sky-blue half-sleeve shirt, khaki half trousers, barefoot); Jaison (about 10: stocky, short curly hair, white vest, maroon-and-white checked half trousers, barefoot); Clara (about 7: two short plaits with red ribbons, faded yellow dotted frock, barefoot). One old woman sweeping her yard behind the bamboo fence at the right.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@villagelane (full, 0.0s–12.0s): the lane the children race down, its palms, the faded blue cottage with nets on bamboo poles at the left, the cream and faded pink cottages at the right, the low mossy laterite walls, the woven fences and the wooden electric pole, exactly as in the reference, seen from its beach end looking up the lane. 100% matches the reference.
@palmgreenwind (reference video, 0.0s–12.0s, movement only): exactly how the coconut palm leaves sway and flutter in the sea breeze; give every palm along the lane that movement. Never copy its green background, its single tree or its look.

THE SHORT CUT
Two gaps on the left side of the lane, the whole trick of the shot. The far gap: a ragged opening about 50 cm wide and 1.1 m high in the woven palm-leaf fence in front of the blue cottage, 18 m from the camera, its dry leaf edges bent back; only a small child fits through it. Behind it, unseen, the cottage yard. The near gap: an open break about 80 cm wide in the low mossy laterite wall at the left, 4 m from the camera, a fallen block lying in the sand beside it. Clara goes in at the far gap and comes out at the near gap: about 14 m of lane she does not run. The boys stay in the lane the whole time.

THE RACE (most important)
Each child moves at their own moment, never in step. 0.0s–2.5s: the lane empty except the old woman sweeping behind the right-hand fence; palm shadows sway on the sand. 2.5s: Antony bursts round the bend at the top of the lane, 30 m away, running flat out; 2.9s Jaison right behind him; 3.3s Clara last, short legs pumping, plaits flying. 3.3s–4.8s: they run down the lane; the boys pull ahead; Clara drops behind by about 3 m. 4.8s: at the gap in the palm-leaf fence Clara swerves left, ducks her head and squeezes through; the leaves shake behind her; she is gone. 4.8s–7.5s: the boys keep running down the lane toward the camera, Antony looking back once at 6.0s for Clara, sees nobody, keeps running. 7.5s: Clara bursts out of the gap in the laterite wall at the left, 4 m from the camera, ahead of both boys, hops over the fallen block, and runs on past the left edge of the frame. 8.0s–9.5s: the boys, 6 m behind her, see her; Antony's mouth opens in a laughing shout without words; Jaison throws up a hand; they run on and out past the left edge of the frame, Antony first at 9.0s, Jaison at 9.5s. 9.5s–12.0s: the lane empty again; dust hangs in the sun and settles; the palm-leaf fence still trembling; a hen flutters back onto the lane. Clara never flies or glides; her feet push off the sand every step.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–12.0s: the camera stands at the beach end of the lane, about 0.9 m above the sand (a child's eye height), in the middle of the lane, looking straight up it; lens about 32mm; deep focus, the whole lane sharp from the near laterite wall to the bend 30 m away. Locked and still for the whole shot; no pan, no follow. In frame: the whole lane, both sides, the palms to the top of the frame. Not in frame: the beach and the sea (behind the camera). Action as in THE RACE above; the children leave the frame past its left edge, all three on the same side.

EYELINES
2.5s–4.8s: the three children look down the lane toward the beach behind the camera, level, eyes ahead. 4.8s: Clara looks at the fence gap at her left as she swerves into it. 6.0s: Antony glances back over his left shoulder up the lane for Clara, then ahead again. 7.5s–8.0s: Clara looks ahead toward the beach past the left side of the camera. 8.0s–9.5s: both boys look at Clara ahead of them at the left. The old woman looks down at her broom until 7.5s, then up at the children. Nobody looks into the lens.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A very slight drift in the frame at 3.0s and 10.0s; the low sun flickers through moving palm fronds across the sand all through; a small exposure dip at 7.5s as Clara bursts out close to the camera, her dark hair filling the lower left; dust from the boys' feet hangs in the light from 8.0s; a small focus breath as Clara crosses close at 7.6s.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
Children of 20–30 kg running on soft sand at about 4 m/s for the boys and 3.5 m/s for Clara; each step kicks up a small spray of sand and red dust that hangs in the sun about 1 s. The palm-leaf fence shakes for about 3 s after she pushes through, then stills. Sea breeze about 15 km/h from behind the camera up the lane: palm crowns bend away from the camera and flutter, the plaits and shirt tails stream back. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Real children racing for real: open mouths, flapping arms, one stumble from Jaison at 5.0s caught in one step, Clara's quick, fierce little face as she squeezes through. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin, five fingers. No posing, no slow-motion heroics.

LIGHTING
Morning sun about 5000K low from behind the camera and to the right, lighting the children's faces and fronts as they run toward us, long palm shadows lying up the lane; open shade under the palms about 6500K; warm bounce off the pale sand about 4500K; the far bend in soft haze.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design, no whoosh.
No spoken words and no voices; nobody speaks; only breathing and one wordless laughing shout from Antony at 8.0s.
0.0s–12.0s: the breeze in the palm fronds, crows, the scrape of the old woman's coconut-leaf broom; 2.5s–9.5s bare feet thudding on sand, quick breathing; 4.8s–7.0s the crackle of dry palm leaves as Clara pushes through the fence; 7.5s a scuff of her feet on the fallen laterite block; distant surf and gulls from the beach behind the camera throughout.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation where the sun shines through the fronds, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: pale sand, red earth, faded blue and pink walls, green palms.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of shot: palms freezing (L157) — every palm moves the whole time; a child sliding without steps; Clara appearing twice or a fourth child; the children leaving the frame on different sides (keep screen direction, L161: all exit past the left edge); a paved road, bicycles, scooters, wires across the lane, plastic, signboards. Exactly three children and one old woman. No lettering anywhere. No cut, no zoom, no camera move.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One continuous 12-second locked wide from the beach end of the lane. Exactly three children, each once: Antony first round the bend, Jaison second, Clara last; Clara through the palm-leaf fence gap at 4.8s and out of the laterite wall gap at 7.5s, ahead of both boys. Exactly one old woman sweeping at the right. All three leave past the left edge of the frame. Nobody speaks. Runs the full 12 seconds.
```
