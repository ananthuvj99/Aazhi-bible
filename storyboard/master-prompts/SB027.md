SCENE CONTEXT
Late at night, 1998, the open Arabian Sea off Kerala, aboard our wooden cargo uru moving slowly through a dark sea. Emotion (one, for the whole shot): quietly. The crew sleep on the forward deck under white sheets; Antony is the only man awake on deck. One continuous locked shot, 12 seconds, seen from high above the water ahead of the bow: the sleeping deck rises and falls on the swell; Antony walks slowly out from behind the front corner of the deckhouse on his round, stops by the lantern and the rope coil at his watch corner, rests his forearms on the rail and looks out to sea. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the same night in every shot from SB027 on; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: our uru exactly as in the start image: a grey-blue weathered hull with long rust streaks and a red-oxide band at the waterline; the high, pointed, rust-stained wooden stem nearest the camera; a solid weathered bulwark around the forward deck with red-brown wooden bitts on top, big coils of golden coir rope on the bow; one tall rust-brown mast with a boom and rope wound round its foot, standing in the middle of the forward deck; a grey, two-level weathered timber deckhouse aft with small square windows, two wooden ladders on its front wall, a dark doorway at the left of its front wall, a red-brown balustraded roof deck on top; a short red-brown balustrade at the right-hand front corner of the deckhouse.
Light: a low-key night: a very dark navy sky with a few faint stars, a dark navy sea with slow swells and a little white water along the hull; cold blue-grey night light, about 7000K, on the deck, the sheets and the men. Exactly two lights: a small lamp glowing at the very top of the mast, and ONE small brass hurricane lantern standing on the deck beside a coil of coir rope at the right-hand front corner of the deckhouse, its warm 2000K glow lighting only that corner. Never flat, never bright blue, never orange all over. No moon in this view: the moon is ahead of the uru, behind the camera.
People: Antony: 24, lean, sun-browned Kerala young man, short black hair pushed back, a light stubble; a pale blue-grey short-sleeve cotton shirt with thin white and faint rust vertical stripes, sleeves rolled once, two top buttons open, a thin silver chain; a green-grey checked mundu; barefoot. Seven crewmen asleep under white sheets on the planks around the mast and between the mast and the bow, exactly where they lie in the start image, faces hidden or turned away. One Antony, never a copy.
NOW: the seven men asleep; nobody standing at 0.0s; nobody on the roof deck; Antony just out of sight behind the right-hand front corner of the deckhouse, walking slowly forward along its side.

ACTIVE REFERENCES

Nobody moves except as written: at 5.0s one sleeper by the mast turns over under his sheet; at 9.0s another pulls his sheet up over his shoulder. No faces readable. Between the sleepers, clear planks; no mats, no sacks, no extra bedding.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion, 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
Exactly the start image: a high wide view from about 8 m above the water, ahead of the bow and a little to its right, looking aft and down along the forward deck, a 28mm lens. The camera stays locked, riding the long swell with the uru; it does not move closer and does not follow. Sharp: the whole deck, the sleepers, the deckhouse front and Antony (deep focus). In frame: the bow, the forward deck, the sleepers, the mast, the deckhouse front and roof, the dark sea round the hull. Not in frame: the moon, any other boat, any light on the sea, the inside of the deckhouse.

THE WATCH (most important)
The quiet lives in stillness and small, motivated movements: Antony's walk is slow and loose, the walk of a tired man on a long watch, shoulders a little rounded, one hand trailing along the rail top as he walks; when he stops he shifts his weight onto one hip, lets his forearms take it on the rail, rolls his neck once at 7.0s, and settles; his head turns slowly to the sea and stays there. The sleepers breathe under their sheets, rising and falling slightly. No dramatic pause, no looking up at the stars, no sigh to the sky, no turning toward the camera, no lantern lifted, nobody waking.

The uru, about 28 m long and about 30 tonnes of wood, moves at about 5 knots (9 km/h) and rises and settles slowly on a long low swell about once every five seconds, with a slight roll; a little white water slides along the hull at the bow. A light breeze from ahead, about 12 km/h, lifts the edges of the sheets and the collar of Antony's shirt. The mast-top lamp swings a few centimetres with the roll. Antony's bare feet make soft contact with the planks; he steadies himself with a hand on the rail when the roll comes. The lantern flame leans with the breeze. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A long, quiet night watch: the men exhausted and asleep, the young man awake, tired, alone with the sea. Small, real, unposed. Eyes never on the lens. Real bodies, five fingers on each hand.

LIGHTING
As in MEMORY: cold blue-grey night everywhere, about 7000K; the one lantern's warm 2000K pool only at the right-hand front corner of the deckhouse, catching Antony's legs, shirt and the side of his face as he stands there; the mast-top lamp a small warm point high up, about 2200K; the white sheets pale blue-grey; the deck mostly in deep shadow; the sky a very dark navy with a few faint stars. Low-key, never crushed to black.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, and no cinematic sound design: no riser, no whoosh, no swell, no rumble, no transition sound. Ambient at a steady level that does not build. No spoken words and no voices; every mouth stays closed.
0.0s–12.0s: water washing along the hull, the timbers creaking slowly with the roll, the rigging ticking far above, the breeze, a sleeper's slow snore once at 4.0s; 2.0s–5.0s Antony's bare feet on the planks; the lantern's soft hiss as he passes it; 9.0s a sheet rustling.

Photoreal 1998 Kerala period feature film, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation around the two lamps, visible skin texture, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Low-key deep navy night with two small warm lights, as in Aswanth's night.

CONSTRAINTS

Exactly eight men in the whole shot: seven sleepers and Antony; nobody on the roof deck; nobody else appears; no second Antony. No moon, no moon path, no fire, no light on the sea. Nobody speaks. No cut, no zoom, no camera move. No lettering anywhere.

Exactly two lights. Nobody speaks. Runs the full 12 seconds.
