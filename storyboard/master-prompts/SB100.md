SCENE CONTEXT
Afternoon, 2026, a quiet private lounge in a good hotel on the Kollam coast in Kerala. Over tea, an influential officer has just called the coastal families' homes small obstacles; the wealthy investor across the table will not accept the word. One continuous medium close-up of Rayan alone in his armchair, 12 seconds. Rayan speaks one line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (the hotel lounge, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a private lounge about 6 x 5 m: walls panelled in pale oak to 1 m, cream fabric wallpaper above; deep armchairs in tan leather round a low square coffee table of dark walnut with a white marble top; behind Rayan a tall window with sheer white curtains drawn, a band of grey-blue sea and a pale afternoon sky soft beyond the glass; at frame left behind him one tall brass floor lamp with a cream shade, lit; on the wall at frame right one framed painting of a wooden fishing vallam on a beach, muted colours, no signature visible.
Light: afternoon daylight through the sheer curtains behind him and to the frame left, 5600K, soft; the floor lamp 2800K; warm bounce from the tan leather.
People in frame: only Rayan.
Rayan (Antony as Rayan): about 52, lean and strong, sun-darkened brown skin with deep lines at the eyes and across the forehead; close-cropped black hair greying at the temples; a full, neatly trimmed salt-and-pepper beard about 2 cm long covering the jaw and chin, thicker on the LEFT jaw where an old scar lies hidden under it; deep-set, dark, steady eyes; a plain off-white linen full-sleeve shirt, the top button open, sleeves rolled to the forearm; charcoal cotton trousers; a plain steel wristwatch on his LEFT wrist; no chain, no ring.
Off screen (never seen): SP Ramachandran sits in the armchair across the table, just left of the camera; Jaison and Balan sit further left, out of frame.
NOW: Rayan sits upright in the tan armchair, not leaning back; a white porcelain teacup on its saucer is in his right hand at chest height, the saucer in his left.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Rayan is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one man in frame, seated in the armchair, who lowers his cup to the table and answers. Nobody else is in frame.

THE TEACUP AND THE TABLE
One plain white porcelain teacup, 8 cm across, with milky Kerala tea about two-thirds full, on a matching white saucer 14 cm across; one thin silver spoon on the saucer. The coffee table is dark walnut with a white marble top, 42 cm high; its near corner shows at the bottom left of the frame with one small glass bowl of cashew nuts on it. Rayan holds the cup by its handle with his RIGHT hand (on frame left, since he faces the camera), the saucer flat in his LEFT palm. He sets the cup and saucer down together on the marble without a sound beyond one soft click, and does not drink from it.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera sits 1.8 m in front of Rayan, across the coffee table, at his seated eye height about 1.15 m, on an 85mm lens, directly beside where SP Ramachandran sits, so Rayan's eyeline passes just to the left of the lens. Medium close-up: from just above his head to the middle of his chest; his face filling about 40% of the frame height, a little right of centre; the window and sheer curtains soft behind him, the brass lamp soft at the left edge, the painting soft at the right edge. Sharp: Rayan's eyes and face all through; everything behind is soft. The camera is locked; it only breathes.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Rayan sits still with the cup at chest height, looking just left of the lens at the SP; he has just heard him; his jaw is still, his breathing slow.
3.0s to 5.0s: his eyes drop to the table at the bottom left of the frame; he leans forward about 15 cm and sets the cup and saucer down on the marble with both hands; he straightens back to upright; his hands rest on his knees, below the frame.
5.0s to 5.6s: he raises his eyes to the SP just left of the lens; his head comes to rest and stays still.
5.6s to 9.8s: he speaks his one Malayalam line, quiet and level, head steady.
9.8s to 12.0s (hold): he keeps looking at the SP, unblinking for two seconds, then one slow blink; his lips close and stay closed. The shot ends on his face.

THE STILLNESS (most important)
His refusal is shown only by small, physical things: the cup goes down slowly and evenly, never put down hard; after it, his body stays completely still, shoulders square, no lean in. The only movement is in the face: the lower lids tighten about 1 mm as he looks up; the muscle at the hinge of the jaw shows once under the beard, at the left side; the breath out before the line is slow and silent. He does not smile politely and does not frown. No finger raised, no pointing, no shaking head, no leaning back with crossed arms, no glance at the camera.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. Spoken language: Malayalam only. Rayan speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal Latin Catholic Malayalam accent kept soft and polished by years abroad; voice Mahendran J; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
5.6s to 9.8s — Rayan: [quietly] "ആളുകളുടെ വീടിന്റെ കാര്യം ചെറിയ തടസ്സമായി കാണാൻ പറ്റില്ല."

Cream, tan and walnut palette with a cool grey-blue band of sea.

CONSTRAINTS
Rayan alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no shoulder, hand, khaki sleeve or head of anyone at the frame edges. No scar visible on his jaw: the beard covers it. No 1998 look: no clean-shaven face, no lavender striped shirt, no silver chain. No police uniform, no cap, no badge in frame. No lettering on the cup, the window or the painting; no hotel logo. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No subtitles anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Rayan, once. Exactly one teacup on one saucer with one spoon, set down once at 3.0s to 5.0s and left on the table; one glass bowl of cashews at the table corner. One brass floor lamp, one painting, one window. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 5.6s to 9.8s, head steady, eyes just left of the lens. Locked 85mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let the courtesy of the room stay in his voice while the refusal underneath it is absolute; a calm correction, not an argument.
