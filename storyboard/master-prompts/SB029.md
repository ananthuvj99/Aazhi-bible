SCENE CONTEXT
Late at night, 1998, the open Arabian Sea off Kerala, aboard our wooden cargo uru. Antony, alone on watch at the rail by the front corner of the deckhouse, has just seen a tiny light straight ahead on the far horizon. Emotion (platform tag, written once): worried. One continuous shot, 10 seconds: the camera pushes slowly forward over his right shoulder and the far light becomes clear: a small fishing vallam on fire, 500 m away; as he watches, the fire runs along the whole boat. His body freezes. What happened to the boat is never shown: no attackers, no other vessel. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the same night in every shot; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: our wooden cargo uru exactly as in the start image: one thick square wooden post of the deckhouse corner, its paint worn to bare grey wood with rust-brown stains, at the left edge; the worn wooden rail on top of the solid bulwark, about 0.9 m above the deck, its broad flat top rubbed smooth and dark brown, running across the lower frame; beyond it the open sea to the horizon. Nothing else of the boat is in view.
Light: a low-key night as in Aswanth's night: very dark navy sky with a few faint stars, a dark navy sea with slow swells; cold blue-grey night light on Antony's shirt and hair; a faint warm 2000K glow from the one hurricane lantern on the deck behind the camera, touching only the back of his shoulders and the top of the rail. Out on the sea the only warm light is the fire. Never flat, never orange all over, never bright blue. The moon is out of this view, no moon in frame.
People: Antony: 24, lean, sun-browned, short neat black hair, thin silver chain; pale blue-grey short-sleeve cotton shirt with thin white vertical stripes, sleeves rolled once; green-grey checked mundu; barefoot. Seen only from behind and from the side. Alone, one of him.
Fire: one small Kerala fishing vallam burning, 500 m away, straight out from Antony's face, toward the bow on the uru's left side.
NOW: Antony standing at the rail by the deckhouse corner, both forearms resting on the rail top, leaning slightly forward, head level, staring out at the far light; the crew asleep on the forward deck and Aashaan at the wheel, all out of this view.

ACTIVE REFERENCES

THE BURNING VALLAM (most important)
The same tiny light as the start image, the same boat, 500 m away the whole time: it never comes closer. It grows only because the long lens brings it in and because the fire spreads. 0.0s–2.0s: a single warm flicker sitting exactly on the horizon line, about a fortieth of the frame wide, as in the start image. 2.0s–7.0s: through the tightening lens it resolves into a long, low, dark hull lying on the horizon; at 4.0s the fire catches the nets and flares: the flames run from one end of the hull to the other and jump up to about 2 m, bright yellow-white at the core, orange at the ragged tips, tearing in the wind; sparks lift and drift right; a thin dark plume of smoke starts to climb above it, lit orange at its base, leaning right with the wind. 7.0s–10.0s: as in the end reference: the whole boat about a twentieth of the frame wide, sitting on the horizon right of centre, a row of bright flames along its full length, the smoke plume rising and leaning right, a narrow broken orange reflection streak on the swells running from the boat toward the uru. Small, far and violent: the only warm light out on the sea, lighting nothing near. Nobody on board, no figures, no second boat, no explosion.

FORMAT MODE
21:9 cinematic widescreen. One continuous shot, 10 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion, no zoom jump: one smooth push. Runs the full 10 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s to 2.0s: exactly the start image: a 35mm lens, the camera about 1.2 m behind Antony's head at his shoulder height (about 1.5 m above the deck), still, riding with the boat. The tiny light flickers on the horizon straight ahead of his face. Sharp: his head and the rail; the far light a soft point. Antony breathes, still.
2.0s to 7.0s: the camera pushes slowly forward and a little to the right, over his right shoulder, about 1 m in 5 seconds, while the lens tightens smoothly from 35mm to 85mm; his head slides to the left edge of the frame and goes soft; the focus passes to the horizon at 2.5s. Through the long lens the light opens into a burning boat: first a cluster of orange tongues, then the low dark hull on the horizon; at 4.0s the fire flares along its whole length and the smoke plume starts to climb. At 3.0s his breath catches; at 6.0s his right hand tightens on the rail.
7.0s to 10.0s: hold on the end view at 85mm: his right shoulder and the back of his head soft at the left edge, his right hand on the rail; the burning vallam small and sharp on the horizon right of centre, 500 m away. At 8.0s he leans a few centimetres toward the fire, then stops. Sharp: the burning boat; Antony soft.

ANTONY (his body)
Seen from behind and the side only: at 3.0s his breath catches and stops; his shoulders rise and lock; at 6.0s his right hand tightens on the rail, the knuckles pale, five fingers; at 8.0s he leans forward a few centimetres toward the fire, his head pushing out, then stops and stays. No turning to the camera, no pointing, no stepping back, no shout, no hand to the mouth.

EYELINES
Antony is the only person. 0.0s–10.0s: his head points straight at the burning vallam the whole time, gaze level with the horizon; as the camera moves over his shoulder his head stays aimed at the boat, so the line from his face runs exactly to the fire, which sits just right of his head at the start and right of centre at the end. Never down at the water by the hull, never at the rail, never toward the camera.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
2.5s: a tiny focus breath as the focus travels to the horizon. 4.0s: the exposure dips very slightly as the fire flares, then settles over half a second. The push carries a faint, steady drift from the boat's roll. 3.0s: his breath catches; 8.0s: a slow breath out lifts and drops his shoulders. A single small bump at 5.5s as a swell passes under the hull.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The uru, about 30 tonnes of wood, rises and settles on a long low swell about once every five seconds, with a slight roll of 2 degrees; the horizon tilts very gently with it. The burning vallam rides the same swell 500 m off: it lifts and drops on the horizon, once almost dipping out of sight. The flames lean and tear in a light wind of about 10 km/h from the left, the smoke bends right; embers rise on the heat and drift downwind for 2 to 3 seconds before dying. The reflection breaks and re-forms on each swell. The breeze stirs Antony's hair and collar. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Alone, the tired watchman turned to stone: dread, not panic, shown only by the caught breath, the locked shoulders and the tightening hand. No posing, no acting to the lens. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin, five fingers.

LIGHTING
As in MEMORY: cold blue-grey night about 7000K on Antony, the post and the rail; the far fire about 1800K, bright orange-yellow, the only strong light, lighting the base of its own smoke and its reflection streak but nothing on the uru; a faint warm 2000K edge on Antony's shoulder from the lantern behind the camera; the sky deep navy with a few faint stars.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, and no cinematic sound design: no riser, no whoosh, no swell, no rumble, no transition sound. Ambient at a steady level that does not build. No spoken words and no voices; every mouth stays closed.
0.0s–10.0s: water along the hull, the timbers creaking with the roll, the breeze. From 4.0s, very faint and far, the crackle of the fire carried on the wind. 3.0s his breath catching; 8.0s his slow breath out.

STYLE
Photoreal 1998 Kerala period feature film, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation around the fire, visible skin texture and pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Low-key deep navy night with one far fire. Every frame looks like the film itself, with nothing from the making of it.

CONSTRAINTS
Exactly one man in frame, Antony, seen from behind and the side; nobody else on the uru in view. Exactly one burning boat; nobody on it, no second boat, no ship lights. The fire stays 500 m away on the horizon line: it gets clearer through the long lens, never closer, never bigger than a twentieth of the frame width; no huge smoke cloud, no blaze filling the sea (L156). No lantern in frame (the one lantern is behind the camera, L155). No hand, arm or prop near the camera (L150). No moon in frame. No cut, no dissolve, no zoom jump: one smooth push. No lettering anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One continuous 10-second push over Antony's right shoulder, 35mm to 85mm. Exactly one man, Antony, once; his head points at the fire the whole time. Exactly one small fishing vallam on the horizon, 500 m away, the same as the light in the start image: it catches and flares along its whole length at 4.0s, one thin smoke plume leaning right, one narrow orange reflection streak. One rail, one post. Nobody speaks. Runs the full 10 seconds.
