## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Morning, about 1985, the landing beach of the Alappad fishing village, Kerala. The father picks one fish out of his basket and lays it in his son's hands; the boy holds it, turns it a little, and studies it, proud and fascinated. Emotion: curious. The shot in one sentence: a locked close insert of two pairs of hands: the father's big hands laying one fish across the boy's small cupped palms, and the boy's hands holding it still at the end, ready for a match cut. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the landing beach; the same words in every shot of SB005–SB007)
Place: the pale grey-gold beach at the landing place; in this insert only a soft, out-of-focus background of pale sand, the black side of a boat and morning light.
Light: morning sun low from the sea, at the right of the frame.
People in frame (hands only): the boy Antony, about 10: two small, sun-browned hands with sand on the fingertips, short bitten nails, a scrape on the left knuckle, and behind them, soft, the front of his faded sky-blue half-sleeve cotton shirt with one button missing. The father, about 40: two big, sun-darkened, rope-scarred hands with thick fingers, cracked skin at the knuckles, salt drying on the forearms, entering from the top of the frame and leaving again. No face of either in frame.
NOW: the boy holds his two hands out together, palms up and cupped, at his chest height, in the middle of the frame, the little fingers touching side by side so the two palms make one shallow bowl; the father's hands are above the frame holding the fish. The boy stands on the sand facing the sea side of the beach; the father kneels on one knee in front of him beside the basket (both bodies out of frame except the boy's shirt front and the father's hands and forearms).
Background (soft, never sharp): pale grey-gold sand about 1.5 m behind the boy, the black-tarred side of one drawn-up vallam as a dark band across the top third of the frame, a sliver of bright morning sky at the very top corner on the right. Nothing in the background is readable as a person.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
None attached. The hands, the shirt, the fish and the beach are described in full in this prompt; build them only from these words. Exactly four hands: two small, two big.

THE FISH
One Indian mackerel, about 26 cm long and 6 cm deep, fresh from the net: a blue-green back with thin dark wavy lines along it, golden-yellow stripes along the sides, a silver-white belly, a big round dark eye with a gold ring, a forked yellowish tail, two small dorsal fins, wet and shining, a few grains of sand stuck to its side. It is alive for the first seconds: its tail flicks once. In the boy's hands it lies across both palms, head toward the left of the frame, tail toward the right, belly toward the camera. It is the only fish in frame. Never a toy, never a plastic-looking fish, never a giant fish.

THE HANDS (most important)
Small, real, observable movements only, one pair of hands moving at a time. 0.0s–2.5s: hold: the boy's two small hands cupped and waiting in the middle of the frame, fingers twitching once with impatience at 1.2s; his shirt front rises and falls with quick breaths behind them. 2.5s–4.0s: the father's two big hands come down from the top of the frame holding the mackerel, one under its head and one under its tail, and lay it gently across the boy's palms; the boy's fingers close a little to take the weight; the father's right thumb rests on the fish's back for half a second, then both big hands lift away and leave the top of the frame by 4.0s. 4.0s–4.6s: the fish flicks its tail once against the boy's wrist; the boy's hands flinch and hold on. 4.6s–6.0s: the boy tilts his hands a few degrees toward the camera, then back; the thumb of his left hand (on the right of the frame) strokes once along the fish's side from gill to tail. 6.0s–8.0s: hold: his hands still, the fish lying across both palms, head left, tail right, level, in the middle of the frame: this is the match-cut frame. Avoid: the fish sliding off, extra fingers, a third hand, a hand coming toward the lens, cartoon wiggling.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 8 seconds, real time, no cut. The last 2 seconds are a still hold for the match cut. 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 8 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–8.0s: the camera stands about 0.7 m in front of the boy, a little above his hands (about 1.1 m above the sand, looking down about 20 degrees), on the sea side so the low sun comes from the right of the frame; lens about 85mm; an insert: the boy's two hands fill the middle third of the frame, his shirt front soft behind them, the sand far below soft. Sharp: the hands and the fish; everything behind soft. Locked and still for the whole shot. Not in frame: either face, the basket, the other children. Action as in THE HANDS above.

EYELINES
No eyes are in frame. The boy's head is above the top of the frame, looking down at his hands and the fish the whole time (his shirt collar tips forward a little at 4.6s as he bends to look closer). The father's head is above and behind the top of the frame, looking down at the boy's hands while he lays the fish (2.5s–4.0s). No face appears.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A very slight drift in the frame at 1.0s and 7.0s; the boy's breathing moves his hands up and down by about 5 mm every second; a small focus breath when the father's hands enter at 2.5s, settling back on the fish by 3.0s; sun glint on the wet fish flaring a little at 4.2s when the tail flicks; drops of seawater falling from the fish to the sand below.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The mackerel weighs about 250 g; the boy's hands dip about 1 cm as its weight lands, then lift back. Wet scales leave a shine on his palms. The tail flick moves the fish about 1 cm, and his fingers tighten in answer. Seawater runs off the fish between his fingers and drips. A sea breeze about 10 km/h from the right flutters the edge of his loose shirt. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Two real pairs of hands with real weight: the father's sure, gentle and practised, the boy's careful, a little nervous, then proud. Real skin with pores, lines and scrapes, five fingers on every hand, sand on the fingertips. No posing, no hand modelling, nothing placed for the camera.

LIGHTING
Morning sun about 5000K low from the right, side-lighting the hands and the fish so the scales shine and the wavy lines on the back read; warm bounce about 4500K off the pale sand from below; soft skylight fill about 6500K on the left; the background falls to soft warm sand and shade. No hard reflections that burn out the fish.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design, no impact sound on the cut.
No spoken words and no voices; nobody speaks; only the boy's quick breathing.
0.0s–8.0s: surf at the right, gulls, the wordless murmur of the working beach far behind; 2.5s–4.0s the soft wet pat of the fish landing in his palms; 4.0s–4.6s the tail slapping his wrist; drips onto sand.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the wet scales, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: blue-green and gold fish, sun-brown hands, faded sky-blue shirt.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of insert: macro parts that look artificial with no logic (Aswanth's L146) — real hands at a real beach; a hand near the lens (L150); extra fingers or a third hand; the fish changing species or size; the fish lying the other way at the end (the match cut needs head left, tail right, level, centred); a face drifting into frame. Exactly one fish. No lettering, no wristwatch, no rings on the boy. No cut, no zoom, no camera move.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One continuous 8-second locked insert. Exactly two small hands (the boy's) and two big hands (the father's, only from 2.5s to 4.0s). Exactly one Indian mackerel, head left, tail right, belly toward the camera, across both of the boy's palms at the end. The last 2 seconds still. Nobody speaks. Runs the full 8 seconds.
```
