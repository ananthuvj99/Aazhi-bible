On the bow deck of our wooden uru, Antony sits alone with his canvas bag in his lap; he opens it, takes out the sealed packet he was given, checks that it is dry and whole, and pushes it down to the bottom of the bag under his folded clothes. Beyond the bulwark: the open sea, deep blue, a clean horizon, a few small white clouds; no land, no other boat.
Light: high midday sun, about 5600K, from above and a little behind the camera's left; a bright, clear day; the shade under the bulwark cool blue.

NOW: Antony sitting on the bow deck with his back against the right-hand bulwark, knees drawn up a little, the closed canvas bag lying across his thighs, its strap trailing on the deck at his left side; a coil of coir rope beside his right hip.
