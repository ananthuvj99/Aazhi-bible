SCENE CONTEXT
Evening, 2026, a hotel banquet hall in Kochi during a shipping association reception: guests in small groups, soft voices, glasses. The emotional situation: a rich, careful businessman who has waited years for the right partner thinks he has found him in a foreign-based shipowner, and lowers his voice to bring out his old dream as if it were a small thing. The shot: one locked medium close-up of Balan standing, 12 seconds; he glances round, steps closer and says one line of Malayalam to the man beside the camera. Only Balan speaks.

Guests stand in groups of three or four, soft and out of focus behind Balan: about twenty guests in the visible background, men in dark suits or white shirts with mundus, women in silk sarees, none looking at the camera.
