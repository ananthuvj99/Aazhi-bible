SCENE CONTEXT
Early morning, 1998, Antony's second day in an old jail on the Kerala coast, at the breakfast counter. The senior prisoner ladling out food has just served the man after him and passed over his plate. One continuous medium close-up of Antony alone, plate still held out, 8 seconds. Antony speaks one short line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (the jail breakfast counter, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a long, open-sided mess shed in an old jail yard: a low wall of rough grey plaster under a sloping roof of rusted corrugated iron sheets on wooden rafters; a cement serving counter about 90 cm high, its top worn and wet in patches, with a big dented aluminium cooking vessel on it, about 60 cm across, steaming; the back wall of the shed dirty white with soot above the counter; the yard beyond the open side bright, with a few old trees. Prisoners in white shirts and shorts queue along the counter, each with an aluminium plate.
Light: low early-morning sun slanting into the shed from the open side, hazy with steam and smoke.
People in frame: only Antony.
NOW: Antony stands at the counter, turned toward the server, who stands behind the counter beside the lens; he holds his empty aluminium plate out in both hands at chest height, at the bottom of the frame, still where it was when the ladle passed it by.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@antony20 (face and hair reference only, 0.0s–8.0s): Antony, 24, lean, the same face as the reference; hair cut short and rough, a few days of black stubble. The only person in frame. 100% matches the reference face.
