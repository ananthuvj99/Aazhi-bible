Clara, whose wedding was stopped this morning, has come to the station; her whole attention is on Antony, held inside. Beyond the pillars, the night: the dark compound, a single tall coconut palm, black sky. Insects circle the bulb.
Light: one bare tungsten bulb hanging under the veranda roof above and in front of Clara; the open door spills a cooler fluorescent tube-light from inside; the night beyond the pillars deep blue-black.
People in frame: only Clara.
NOW: Clara stands on the veranda 1 m from the open door, turned toward the constable who stands in the doorway beside the lens; her hands together at her waist, below the frame, the fingers of her right hand twisting the thin gold bangles on her left wrist.
