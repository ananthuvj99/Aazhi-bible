The house is asleep; the boy Antony, about 10, lies awake beside his sleeping mother, too excited about tomorrow to sleep. His father appears in the open doorway, catches the boy's eye, lifts one finger to his lips and tips his head toward the door, inviting him out without a word; the boy eases himself up so as not to wake his mother. Emotion: softly. The shot in one sentence: a low, locked shot from beside the cot, the boy's face large and sharp at the right of the frame, the doorway soft at the left, with one slow focus pull from the boy to his father and back. Nobody speaks.
