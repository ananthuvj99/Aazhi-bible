```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Night, about 1985, the kitchen of a fisherman's house on the Alappad coast, Kerala. The family sits on a palm-leaf mat on the floor eating supper by lantern light: the father at the boy's left, the mother by the stove. The boy Antony, his plate in his lap, gathers his courage and asks his father one Malayalam question about tomorrow. Emotion: happy. The shot in one sentence: a locked medium close-up of the boy sitting on the mat, looking up from his plate at his father and asking his line. Antony speaks one Malayalam line; nobody else speaks.
Spoken language: Malayalam. Every spoken word in this video is Malayalam.

MEMORY (supper at home; the same words in every supper shot, SB008–SB009)
Place: the kitchen exactly as in @kitchenclean, behind the boy and soft: the low wood-fire stove at the back with an orange glow under a pot, the soot-black wall above it, a steel pot and a clay pot on the stove, the red-oxide floor and the edge of the woven palm-leaf mat.
Light: night outside; inside, one kerosene hurricane lantern standing on the floor at the right of the frame, and the stove's glow at the back.
People in frame: the boy Antony only (about 10): lean, sun-browned, short black hair with a cowlick at the crown, a little damp from his evening wash, a faded sky-blue half-sleeve cotton shirt with one button missing, a thin black cord with a small aluminium cross medal, sitting cross-legged. The father sits just outside the frame at the left of the lens; the mother sits just outside the frame at the right, near the stove.
NOW: the boy sits cross-legged on the mat with the steel plate of rice and fried fish in his lap, below the frame; his right hand, fingers coated with rice, rests at the plate's rim below the frame; he looks down at his plate.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@kitchenclean (background only, 0.0s–10.0s): the kitchen behind the boy, soft: the wood-fire stove with its glow, the soot-black wall, the pots, the red-oxide floor, exactly as in the reference, out of focus. Its two Sacred Heart pictures stay out of this frame. 100% matches the reference.
The boy himself has no reference image: build him only from the words in MEMORY. Exactly one person in frame.

THE PALM-LEAF MAT
One woven palm-leaf mat (pAya) about 1.8 m by 0.9 m, pale straw colour gone brown at the edges, a few broken strands, laid on the red-oxide floor; the boy sits on it cross-legged. In this shot only its near edge shows at the very bottom of the frame, soft, with a grain of rice on it. The lantern stands on the bare floor just off the mat at the right. No table, no chairs: in this house supper is on the floor.

THE ASK (most important)
Small, real, observable movements only. 0.0s–2.5s: hold: he looks down at his plate, rolls a small ball of rice with the fingers of his right hand below the frame (his right shoulder moves a little), and does not eat it; he glances once at his father at the left at 1.2s and away again; he bites his lower lip. 2.5s–3.5s: he lifts his face to his father beside the lens at the left, his chin coming up, eyes wide and hopeful, a quick breath in. 3.5s–6.3s: the line, head steady, mouth fully visible, a small pause at the comma before the last word, the last word rising. 6.3s–10.0s: hold: he keeps his eyes on his father, waiting, not breathing for a moment; at 7.8s his eyebrows lift a little more; at 8.6s a hopeful half-smile starts at one corner of his mouth and he swallows. Avoid: a pleading cartoon face, hands clasped in prayer, looking into the lens, a face too old for ten.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 10 seconds, real time, no cut. 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 10 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–10.0s: the camera sits low on the floor at the height of the seated boy's face, about 0.75 m above the floor, about 1.3 m in front of him across the mat, looking straight at him, a little to the right of his father's place so that his eyeline to his father falls just left of the lens; lens about 65mm; a medium close-up: his head and shoulders and the top of his shirt, his face filling about a third of the frame height, just right of centre. Sharp: his eyes and mouth; the stove and wall soft behind. Locked and still for the whole shot.
0.0s–2.5s: hold; he looks at his plate. 2.5s–3.5s: he lifts his face to his father. 3.5s–6.3s: he asks his Malayalam line. 6.3s–10.0s: hold; he waits for the answer. Nobody else enters the frame; no hand enters the frame.

EYELINES
0.0s–2.5s: the boy looks down at his plate in his lap, about 35 degrees below the lens; 1.2s–1.6s a quick glance at his father's face just left of the lens and away. 2.5s–10.0s: his eyes on his father's face just left of the lens, about 15 degrees left of it and a little above his own eye height (his father sits taller), steady through the line and the wait. He never looks into the lens and never toward his mother's side (the right) in this shot.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.
While the line is spoken, Antony is the only person in the frame.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
His breath visible at 1.0s and a quick in-breath at 3.2s; a held breath from 6.3s to 8.0s, released at 8.2s; a very slight drift in the frame at 2.0s and 9.0s; the lantern flame flickers, so the light on his cheek moves gently all through; a small focus breath as he lifts his face at 2.5s, settling on his eyes by 2.9s; one swallow at 8.6s.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The lantern flame about 3 cm tall stirs in a faint draught from the open back door, so its light on his face rises and falls by a little every second or two. Heat from the stove behind him makes the air shimmer faintly above the pot. His damp hair dries in loose strands; it stays still during the line, and one strand slips onto his forehead at 7.0s when he lifts his eyebrows. His shoulders rise with each breath. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A real 10-year-old asking for the biggest thing he can think of, trying to sound grown-up and not quite managing: hope he cannot hide. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin with pores, a little rice on his fingers, a sunburnt nose, milk-tooth gap. No posing.

LIGHTING
One kerosene hurricane lantern about 2000K on the floor to the right of the frame and a little in front of him, lighting his face warmly and softly from the right and below; the stove's ember glow about 1800K behind him as a soft warm rim on his hair and shoulder; the rest of the kitchen in deep warm shadow. Even, soft light across his face for the line; no hard shadow over his mouth. No electric light, no blue light.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design.
Spoken language: Malayalam only — not English, not a translation. Antony speaks the exact Malayalam words in quotes below, in everyday Kollam coastal Malayalam pronunciation, and nothing else. No English words. Lip-sync exact; every word on camera; the line is spoken once.
3.5s to 6.3s — Antony, about 10, a clear boy's voice, hopeful and a little shy, quick at first, a small pause at the comma, the stress on "ഞാനും", the last word rising into a question, soft volume to a father sitting beside him. About 2.8 seconds.
[happy] "നാളെ ഞാനും വരട്ടെ അച്ഛാ, കടലിൽ?"
Ambient, steady: the hiss of the lantern, the crackle and pop of the wood fire, a steel tumbler set down on the floor off frame at 1.5s, crickets and distant surf through the open back door.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation around the lantern glow, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: lantern amber, soot black, faded sky-blue shirt, red-oxide floor.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of shot: the listener appearing behind the speaker (L34) — the father and mother stay out of frame; English speech when the scene is described in English (L159) — every spoken word is Malayalam; a hand in front of the mouth; the boy looking into the lens; a table and chairs; an electric bulb; Hindu household items (this is a Latin Catholic home). Exactly one boy in frame, exactly one lantern. No lettering. No cut, no zoom, no camera move.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Antony, about 10, faded sky-blue shirt, small aluminium cross on a black cord. Exactly one lantern, at the right; the stove glow soft behind. One line in Malayalam, spoken once, 3.5s to 6.3s; no English words. Low locked medium close-up, about 65mm. Runs the full 10 seconds.

INTENT: When Antony speaks his Malayalam line, let it be a small boy's whole heart in one question, hopeful and brave, his eyes asking more than his voice.
```
