```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Morning, 2026, the investor's office in a coastal town in Kerala. The relocation plan he agreed to has been replaced by a cheaper site far from the sea; his two partners want to keep it among friends. Rayan will not fund it, and will not settle anything without the families. One continuous medium close-up of Rayan alone behind his desk, 12 seconds. Rayan speaks one line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Rayan's office, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a plain, bright office about 5 x 4 m: white walls, a floor of grey stone tiles; a broad desk of pale wood; behind Rayan on the wall at frame left ONE framed pencil sketch, 30 x 40 cm, of a small old church with a bell gable, an arched door and two side windows, in a plain dark frame; at frame right behind him a wide window with white vertical blinds half open, slices of bright sky and a grey-blue sea beyond; on a narrow shelf below the sketch ONE plain dark wooden crucifix about 25 cm tall standing on a small base.
Light: morning daylight through the blinds behind him at frame right, 5600K; soft white fill from the walls.
People in frame: only Rayan.
Rayan (Antony as Rayan): about 52, lean and strong, sun-darkened brown skin with deep lines at the eyes and across the forehead; close-cropped black hair greying at the temples; a full, neatly trimmed salt-and-pepper beard about 2 cm long covering the jaw and chin, thicker on the LEFT jaw where an old scar lies hidden under it; deep-set, dark, steady eyes; a plain off-white linen full-sleeve shirt, the top button open, sleeves rolled to the forearm; charcoal cotton trousers; a plain steel wristwatch on his LEFT wrist; no chain, no ring.
Off screen (never seen): Jaison sits across the desk just left of the camera; Balan beside him further left, out of frame.
NOW: Rayan sits upright in a plain dark office chair, forearms on the desk; before him the approved plan lies spread flat, and six colour photographs of the unfinished houses lie in a loose row on top of it.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Rayan is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one man in frame, seated behind his desk over the plan and the photographs, who gathers the photographs into one stack, lays his hand on it and speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

THE PLAN AND THE PHOTOGRAPHS
The approved plan: one large sheet, 84 x 60 cm, a soft coloured drawing of neat rows of small houses with a strip of blue sea along one edge; its lines and colours soft, no readable text or numbers; it lies flat across the desk at the bottom of the frame. On top of it, six colour photographs, 15 x 10 cm, glossy, each showing the same bare grey concrete-block houses with empty window holes on red earth under a white sky. Rayan gathers the six photographs into one neat stack with both hands, squares it with a tap on the desk, and lays it down on the sea edge of the plan; then his RIGHT hand (on frame left, since he faces the camera) rests flat on top of the stack. Nothing is thrown, torn or pushed at anyone.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera sits 1.8 m in front of Rayan across the desk, at his seated eye height about 1.2 m, on an 85mm lens, directly beside where Jaison sits, so Rayan's eyeline passes just to the left of the lens. Medium close-up: from just above his head to the middle of his chest, the near edge of the desk with the plan and the stack of photographs at the bottom of the frame; his face filling about 40% of the frame height, a little right of centre; the church sketch and the crucifix soft at the left behind him, the window and blinds soft at the right. Sharp: Rayan's eyes and face all through; the wall and window soft. The camera is locked; it only breathes.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Rayan sits still, forearms on the desk, looking just left of the lens at Jaison; he has just heard him; his face does not change.
3.0s to 5.6s: his eyes drop to the desk; with both hands he gathers the six photographs into one stack, taps it square once on the desk, lays it on the sea edge of the plan, and rests his right hand flat on top; his left hand returns to the desk edge.
5.6s to 6.2s: he raises his eyes back to Jaison just left of the lens; his head comes to rest and stays still.
6.2s to 9.6s: he speaks his one Malayalam line, quiet and level, head steady, the right hand still on the stack.
9.6s to 12.0s (hold): he keeps looking at Jaison, waiting for an answer that does not come; one slow breath out; his lips close and stay closed. The shot ends on his face.

THE HAND ON THE PHOTOGRAPHS (most important)

Spoken language: Malayalam only. Rayan speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal Latin Catholic Malayalam accent kept soft by years abroad; voice Mahendran J; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.

Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. White, pale wood and off-white linen palette with a grey-blue band of sea.

CONSTRAINTS
Rayan alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no hand, shoulder or head of Jaison or Balan at the frame edges; no hand near the camera. No scar visible on his jaw: the beard covers it. No 1998 look. No readable text, numbers or names on the plan, the photographs or the sketch; no company logo anywhere. Exactly one sketch and one crucifix on the wall side; no Hindu lamp, no idol. No laptop, no phone on the desk. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No subtitles anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Rayan, once. Exactly one plan sheet and six photographs (gathered into one stack once at 3.0s to 5.6s, his right hand resting on it to the end). One framed church sketch, one wooden crucifix, one window with blinds. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 6.2s to 9.6s, head steady, eyes just left of the lens. Locked 85mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let it come out as a plain, almost gentle question that closes the matter; the friendliness of the room stays, and nothing in his voice gives way.
```
