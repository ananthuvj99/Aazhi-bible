SCENE CONTEXT
Late afternoon, 1998, the grassy top of a low laterite hill above the coast, the sea at the horizon. Clara has turned half away from Antony with her arms folded, pretending to be angry that he is a day late; Antony waits just behind her, smiling. She glances back at him, tries to keep her stern face, fails, and turns and throws her arms round him; he holds her. One continuous shot, 12 seconds, a two-shot from the side. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the hilltop; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: the rounded top of a low laterite hill: short, dry, gold-green grass with patches of red-brown laterite earth and a few loose red stones; a footpath of bare red earth coming up the slope from the left of the frame; one cashew tree about 4 m tall with a dark, leaning trunk and glossy leaves standing about 5 m behind Clara at the right of the frame. Behind them, beyond the edge of the hilltop: the tops of coconut palms below the hill, then a flat band of grey-blue sea at the horizon, about a third from the top of the frame, and a wide pale sky with thin high cloud. No houses, no road, no other person in view.
Light: low late-afternoon sun, about 3800K, from the left of the frame behind Antony and a little toward the camera, raking across the hilltop: warm rims on their hair and shoulders, long soft shadows on the grass toward the right; the open sky fills their faces, about 6000K, so both faces read clearly.
People in frame:

Clara: about 20, slim, wheatish-brown skin, thick straight eyebrows, calm direct gaze; black hair with a centre parting in one long thick plait down her back to the waist; a light sky-blue cotton saree with a thin white border, the pallu over her LEFT shoulder, a matching light sky-blue short-sleeve blouse; a thin gold chain with a small gold cross; small gold stud earrings; a few thin wooden-bead bangles on her RIGHT wrist and a few thin gold bangles on her LEFT wrist; a thin ring; brown leather thong sandals.
