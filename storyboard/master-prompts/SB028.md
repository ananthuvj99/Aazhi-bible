SCENE CONTEXT
Late at night, 1998, the open Arabian Sea off Kerala, aboard our wooden cargo uru. The crew are asleep on the forward deck; Antony, 24, keeps the night watch alone, leaning on the rail at the front corner of the deckhouse, tired and quiet. Emotion (one, for the whole shot): curious. One continuous shot, 10 seconds: the camera moves slowly round behind him, and straight ahead of his face, far off on the dark horizon, a tiny warm light appears; he notices it and goes still. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the same night in every shot from SB027 on; nothing changes unless this block says so)
Place: our wooden cargo uru as in the start image and in Aswanth's night: at the front corner of the grey two-level deckhouse, thick square wooden posts about 15 cm across, their paint worn to bare grey wood with long rust-brown and orange stains running down them; a worn wooden rail about 1 m high on top of the bulwark, its flat top rubbed smooth and dark by hands; the plain plank deck behind him, brown and dull, lit warm only near the lantern; behind Antony on the deck, ONE big coil of coir rope, golden-brown and fraying, with ONE small lantern standing beside it; beyond the rail, the open sea to the horizon. The hull below the rail is grey-blue with rust streaks.
Light: a low-key night: a very dark navy sky with a few faint stars, a dark navy sea with slow swells; the deck, the posts and the corners of the deckhouse mostly in deep shadow; cold blue-grey night light, about 7000K, on Antony's shirt, hair and the near side of his face; a warm glow about 2000K only from the one lantern on the deck behind him, falling on the rope coil, the lowest part of the posts and the back of his shoulders. The two lights never mix into orange: cold everywhere, warm only near the lantern. Never flat, never bright blue, never orange all over. No moon in this view.
People: Antony: 24, lean, sun-browned Kerala young man, short black hair pushed back, a few loose strands on his forehead, a light stubble, tired eyes; a pale blue-grey short-sleeve cotton shirt with thin white and faint rust vertical stripes, sleeves rolled once, two top buttons open, a thin silver chain at his neck; a green-grey checked mundu; barefoot. Alone, one of him, never a copy.
Fire: one small fishing vallam beginning to burn far off, 500 m away, straight out from Antony's face, toward the bow on the uru's left side: at first only a tiny warm flicker on the horizon, never close.
NOW: Antony leaning forward on the rail with both forearms on it, his profile turned to the right of the frame, eyes heavy; the rope coil and the lantern on the deck behind him at the left.
