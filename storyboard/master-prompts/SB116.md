## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Late morning, 2026, a small, tidy house on the Kollam coast, Kerala: Swathi's sitting room. A grown son is meeting his birth mother for the first time as an adult; she has just put an old photograph into his hands. He looks at it and asks her who it is. One continuous medium close-up of the son alone, 12 seconds. The son speaks one short line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Swathi's sitting room, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a small room with walls painted pale yellow, the paint faded and patched; a red oxide floor, polished smooth with age; a low wooden bench with a thin cotton cushion where the son sits; behind him a wooden window with green-painted shutters open onto bright daylight and the green of a coconut palm and a plantain leaf just outside; a narrow wooden shelf on the wall at the right with a steel tumbler, a small transistor radio and three folded newspapers. No pictures on the wall behind him.
Light: late morning; soft 5600K daylight from the open window behind and to the left of him; warm 3500K bounce off the yellow walls and the red floor.
People in frame: only the son.
Swathi's son: about 28, slim, medium-brown skin, short black hair neatly cut, a short trimmed black beard, a small mole below his LEFT eye; clean, modern clothes: a light-grey and white small-check cotton shirt, sleeves rolled to the forearm, dark-blue jeans; a black fabric-strap wristwatch on his LEFT wrist; no chain, no ring.
Off screen (never seen): Swathi sits on a wooden chair 1.2 m to his right, just to the left of the camera, a little lower than him.
NOW: he sits on the bench, leaning forward, forearms on his knees, holding the photograph in both hands at chest height, its back toward the lens; his eyes are down on it.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. The son is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one young man in frame, seated on the bench, the photograph in both hands with its back to the lens, who looks up and speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

THE PHOTOGRAPH
One small, old colour print, 9 x 13 cm, upright, with a thin white border gone cream with age; its back faces the lens: off-white photo paper, a brown water stain at one corner, a soft crease across the middle, one corner bent. Its front, with the picture, faces only him and is never seen in this shot. He holds it by its edges with the fingertips of both hands, thumbs on the front border, never touching the picture. At 4.0s he lowers it 10 cm toward his knees, still in both hands, its back still toward the lens.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera sits at his seated eye height, about 1.05 m, 1.5 m in front of him, just right of where Swathi sits, on a 75mm lens, so his gaze, when it rises, falls just left of the lens. Medium close-up: the top of his head to his upper chest, his face about 40% of the frame height, a little right of centre; the back of the photograph at the bottom of the frame; the bright window soft behind him at the left, the shelf soft at the right. Sharp: his face; the photograph and the window soft. The camera is locked.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): he looks down at the photograph in his hands; still; one breath.
3.0s to 4.0s: his eyes stay on the picture a moment longer; his thumbs tighten slightly on the border.
4.0s to 5.0s: he lowers the photograph 10 cm and lifts his eyes and then his head to Swathi, just left of the lens; his head comes to rest.
5.0s to 5.4s: stillness.
5.4s to 6.8s: he speaks his one Malayalam line, head steady, the photograph still in both hands below his chin.
6.8s to 12.0s (hold): he keeps looking at her, waiting; his lips close; his eyes search her face; one slow blink at 9.5s. The shot ends on his face.

HIS LOOK UP (most important)
Only small, physical things: his eyebrows lift very slightly as he looks at the picture; the inner corners of the brows draw together a little as he raises his eyes; his lips part a moment before he speaks; after the line his eyes move, very small, between Swathi's two eyes. He does not smile broadly, does not cry, does not touch the picture to his lips, does not hand it back. No glance at the camera.

EYELINES
0.0s to 4.0s: he looks down at the photograph in his hands at the bottom of the frame, head tilted down about 30 degrees. 4.0s to 5.0s: his gaze rises to Swathi, seated just left of the lens and a little lower than him, head coming up to about 5 degrees down. 5.0s to 12.0s: his gaze stays on Swathi, just left of the lens, slightly down. He never looks into the lens.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A slight drift of a few millimetres all through; 1.0s his breath lifts his shoulders a little; 4.5s a small focus breath as his head rises; 5.0s a slight exposure lift as the window light catches his face; 6.8s a half-second of stillness; 9.5s the blink.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The photograph weighs a few grams and bends slightly between his fingertips; it trembles 1 mm with his pulse. A soft breeze through the open window moves the palm leaf and the plantain leaf outside about 10 cm, and lifts one newspaper corner on the shelf. The bench cushion compresses under his forearms as he leans. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Careful, open, unsure where to stand; a young man meeting a part of his life he did not know. Real unretouched skin, pores, five fingers on each hand, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no tears, no big reaction.

LIGHTING
Soft 5600K late-morning daylight from the open window behind him at the left as a gentle rim on his hair and shoulder; warm 3500K bounce from the yellow walls and the red floor softened to an even frontal light on his face; the window bright but holding the green outside. Never flat, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh, no transition sound. The room and the morning outside the window only.
0.0s to 12.0s: crows and a distant koel outside, the palm leaves rustling, far-off waves breaking softly; 4.0s the bench creaks as he sits up; 6.8s to 12.0s the morning sounds and his breath.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. The son speaks one short line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam Malayalam accent; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
5.4s to 6.8s — Swathi's son: [curious] "ഇത് അച്ഛനാണോ?"
Delivery: about 1.4 seconds, six syllables, low volume, unhurried, the stress on "അച്ഛനാണോ", the pitch rising gently at the end as a question.

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation at the window, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Soft yellow, red-oxide and leaf-green palette.

CONSTRAINTS
The son alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no hand, shoulder or sari edge of Swathi at the frame edge. The photograph's picture side never faces the lens; nobody appears in the window. Exactly one photograph. No phone in his hands, no tears, no hug. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No lettering or subtitles anywhere; the newspapers unreadable.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Swathi's son, once. Exactly one photograph, held in both hands all through, its back to the lens. One bench, one window, one shelf. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 5.4s to 6.8s, head steady, eyes just left of the lens. Locked 75mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let the question be the first step toward someone he never knew, careful not to hurt her, and open to whatever she answers.
```
