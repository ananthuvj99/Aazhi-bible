SCENE CONTEXT
Afternoon, 1998, a small police station on the Kollam coast of Kerala, the back room. A young fisherman taken from his own wedding is being questioned; the questions have suddenly narrowed to one sealed letter. One continuous medium close-up of Sub-Inspector Ramachandran alone, seated behind his desk, 8 seconds. Ramachandran speaks one short line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (the station back room, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a small, plain 1998 Kerala police-station room about 3.5 m by 4 m: walls lime-washed pale green to shoulder height with a thin dark green line, dirty cream above, the plaster flaking in patches; a red oxide cement floor worn pale in the middle; one barred window in the wall to the right of the desk, 1 m wide, five vertical iron bars painted black, wooden shutters folded open, the afternoon sky white beyond it; a tall grey steel almirah against the back wall at the left, its paint chipped at the handle; a stack of old buff files tied with red cloth tape on top of it; a ceiling fan with three brown blades turning slowly overhead, out of frame, its moving shadow faint on the back wall; a heavy dark wooden desk, its top worn smooth, with a sheet of glass on it.
Light: daylight from the barred window at the right; one bare tungsten bulb hanging off frame above the desk, switched on, weak in the daylight. No pictures, no calendars, no notices, no lettering on any wall.
People in frame: only Ramachandran.
NOW: Ramachandran sits upright in a wooden chair behind the desk, forearms on the desk edge, holding his open file tilted up toward himself at the bottom of the frame, his right palm flat on the envelope inside it; his eyes on Antony, who sits across the desk just beside the lens, off screen.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Ramachandran exactly as written here, the same man in every shot: about 45, heavy-set, broad face, dark brown skin with real pores and a faint shine on the forehead; short black hair oiled and combed back with a side parting on his right, grey at the temples; a thick black moustache trimmed level with the top lip, clean-shaven chin with a blue-grey shadow; heavy-lidded, steady eyes. 1998 Kerala police khaki uniform: short-sleeve khaki shirt, pressed, two buttoned chest pockets, shoulder straps each with two small silver stars, a black leather belt; a steel wristwatch on his LEFT wrist. No name badge, no lettering on the uniform. One of him, never a copy.

THE FILE AND THE ENVELOPE
One buff cardboard police file, 35 cm by 24 cm, its cover soft and furred at the edges, a length of faded red cloth tape hanging loose from its spine; inside, a few typed pages held by a steel clip and, on top of them, one sealed envelope, 16 cm by 11 cm, cream paper gone wavy from salt water, one corner darker where it was wet, the flap sealed. The file is held open and tilted up at about 60 degrees toward Ramachandran, its cover between the envelope and the lens, so only the envelope's top edge and one darker corner show above the cover; no writing, address, stamp or name on anything is ever seen. His RIGHT hand (on frame LEFT) lies flat on the envelope, four fingers together, the thumb along its edge; his LEFT hand (on frame RIGHT) holds the file's cover edge. The file stays at the bottom edge of the frame for the whole shot and is never turned toward the lens.

THE PALM ON THE ENVELOPE (most important)
The menace is in one small, controlled hand and a still face, never in a shout. 0.0s–3.0s: his right palm rests flat on the envelope; at 1.2s the four fingers press down once, a slow press of about 1 cm, so the envelope's edge bends under them, and stay pressed; the thumb slides 2 cm along the envelope's edge and stops. His face is still: lips closed under the moustache, a slow breath in through the nose, the eyes steady on Antony, blinking once at 2.0s. 3.0s–4.6s: the line, head still, only the lips and jaw move; the fingers never lift. 4.6s–8.0s: he waits; his eyelids lower a fraction, his chin lifts about 1 cm at 5.5s; at 6.5s the fingers tap the envelope once, softly, and lie flat again. No smirk, no raised eyebrow, no leaning into the lens, no pointing, no slamming the file, no villain grin.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 8 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion, 21:9 cinematic widescreen frame. Runs the full 8 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–8.0s: the camera stands where Antony's eyes are: across the desk, 1.3 m from Ramachandran's face, 1.15 m above the floor (a seated man's eye height), level with his eyes, just to the right of Antony's seat, so Ramachandran looks just past the right edge of the lens. A 50mm lens, locked, no move, no zoom. Medium close-up: from the middle of his chest to a little above his hair; his face fills about 40 percent of the frame height, placed just right of centre; the file's top edge and his two hands across the bottom of the frame. In frame behind him, soft: the pale green and cream wall, the grey almirah at the left, the bright barred window at the right edge. Not in frame: Antony, the door, any other person. Sharp: Ramachandran's eyes and moustache; the hands and envelope a touch soft; the wall soft.
Action: as in THE PALM ON THE ENVELOPE. 0.0s–3.0s: hold, the press of the fingers. 3.0s–4.6s: the line. 4.6s–8.0s: hold, the single tap at 6.5s.

EYELINES

The file weighs about 300 g; its soft cover bends 1 cm under his left thumb. The envelope is one sheet of folded paper inside paper: under a 1 cm press of four fingers its edge curls up 3 mm and flattens again when the pressure stays even. The ceiling fan at about 120 turns a minute pushes a light draught: loose hair at his temple does not move (oiled), but the loose red tape hanging from the file sways 1 cm every second. Afternoon heat about 32 degrees: a thin film of sweat on his forehead and upper lip, the shirt damp under the arms. The chair creaks once at 2.8s because he shifts his weight back. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A man in full control who has decided what the answer must be. Small and slow: a press of the fingers, a lifted chin, one tap. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin with pores and sweat, five fingers on each hand, a heavy, real body. No posing, no theatre, no grin.

Gentle contrast, real shade under the moustache and in the pockets of the eyes; the khaki reads warm olive, the walls pale green and dirty cream.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, and no cinematic sound design: no riser, no whoosh, no swell, no rumble, no transition sound. Room tone at a steady level that does not build: the ceiling fan's soft tick and hum, a crow outside the window twice, a distant bus horn on the coast road once at 1.5s, the faint scrape of paper under his fingers at 1.2s, the chair creak at 2.8s, a soft tap on paper at 6.5s.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Dialogue language and dubbing language: Malayalam. The line is spoken in Malayalam exactly as written in Malayalam script below, word for word, nothing added, nothing translated; no English words, no subtitles.

Ramachandran, 3.0s to 4.6s: "എന്നിട്ട് വായിച്ചോ നീ അത്?"

The mouth closes after the line and stays closed.

Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: khaki olive, pale lime green, dirty cream, buff paper; the warm golden Act 1 grade is gone here, the room is flat and dry.

CONSTRAINTS

The envelope never faces the lens and nothing written on it, on the file or on the pages is ever readable; no name, no address, no stamp. Exactly one file, one envelope, one pair of hands. His right hand never leaves the envelope. No pistol, no lathi, no handcuffs on the desk. No pictures, calendars, flags or notices on the walls; no lettering anywhere. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Every frame looks like the film itself, with nothing from the making of it. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words, never in English.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Ramachandran, once. Exactly one buff file held open at the bottom of the frame, exactly one sealed cream envelope inside it, his right palm flat on it for all 8 seconds. One barred window at the right, one almirah at the left. One Malayalam line, spoken once, 3.0s to 4.6s, head steady, eyes just right of the lens. Locked 50mm medium close-up. Runs the full 8 seconds.

INTENT: When Ramachandran says "എന്നിട്ട് വായിച്ചോ നീ അത്?" let it land as a trap set quietly: a man who already knows the answer he will write, testing whether the boy across the desk knows what he carried.
