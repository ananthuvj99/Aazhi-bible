```text
SCENE CONTEXT
A bright morning, about 1985, the flat cement terrace of a fisherman's house on the Alappad coast, Kerala, high above the beach. His mother's call has just come up from the yard below: the boats are in. The boy Antony scrambles up from the shared notebook, runs to the low parapet and searches the sea for his father's boat until he finds it. Emotion: excited. The shot in one sentence: a locked view from just beyond the parapet, looking back at the terrace, as Antony runs straight toward us, grips the parapet top, searches the sea and points. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the childhood terrace; the same words in every terrace shot, SB002–SB003)
Place: a flat roof terrace about 6 m by 4 m: grey cement floor, sun-warmed, with dark rain stains and fine cracks; a low whitewashed plastered parapet about 85 cm high with a flat top 15 cm wide, its whitewash flaking in patches; a coir clothesline across the back corner with one white and one blue cloth; the crown of a coconut palm leaning over the back right corner; open blue sky above.
Light: morning sun from the sea side, behind the camera and a little to the left, lighting the terrace and the children's faces.
People in frame: Antony (about 10): lean, sun-browned, short black hair with a cowlick at the crown, a faded sky-blue half-sleeve cotton shirt with one button missing, khaki half trousers, a thin black cord with a small aluminium cross medal, barefoot. Behind him, small and soft: Clara (about 7, two short plaits with red ribbons, faded yellow dotted frock) and Jaison (about 10, stocky, short curly hair, white vest, maroon-and-white checked half trousers), sitting by the open notebook on the floor.
NOW: Antony is already up and running from the notebook at the back of the terrace toward the parapet; Clara and Jaison still sit on the floor by the notebook, looking after him.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
None attached. The children, the terrace and the notebook are described in full in this prompt; build them only from these words. Exactly three children.

THE PARAPET
The low wall around the terrace edge: brick plastered with lime and cement, about 85 cm high and 23 cm thick, the flat top 15 cm wide, whitewashed long ago, the whitewash flaking to grey plaster and streaked dark below the top by monsoon rain; a small chip missing from the top edge where Antony grips it. For a 10-year-old it comes up to the middle of his chest: he must rise on his toes to lean over. It runs straight across the lower part of the frame, close to the camera, its top edge just below the bottom third. It is the only wall in the shot; there is no railing, no grille, no glass.

ANTONY SEARCHING (most important)
Small, real, observable movements only. 0.0s–2.0s: he runs in from the back of the terrace, arms pumping, bare feet slapping the cement, five steps. 2.0s–2.6s: he arrives and slaps both hands flat on the parapet top, his chest bumping the wall, and rises up on his toes. 2.6s–5.5s: he searches: head turning slowly from his left to his right across the sea behind the camera, eyes narrowed against the light, his mouth slightly open, breath quick; he stops on one place, loses it, searches again; he pushes up higher, elbows locking. 5.5s: he finds the boat; his eyebrows jump, eyes widen, mouth opens into a wide grin. 6.0s–7.5s: he lifts his RIGHT arm and points out to sea past the left side of the camera, his whole arm straight, fingertip, eyes and the boat on one line; his left hand still grips the parapet top. 7.5s–10.0s: hold: still pointing, rocking up and down on his toes once, grinning, eyes fixed on the boat. Avoid: waving at the camera, a posed salute, looking into the lens, a cartoon jump.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 10 seconds, real time, no cut. 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 10 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–10.0s: the camera hangs just beyond the parapet, about 1.5 m out from its outer face over the drop to the yard, at the height of Antony's face when he stands at the wall (about 1.3 m above the terrace floor), looking straight back across the terrace; lens about 40mm. Locked and still for the whole shot. In frame: the parapet top across the lower part of the frame; the whole terrace behind it, the notebook on the floor at the back with Clara and Jaison beside it, the clothesline and the palm crown at the back right. Not in frame: the sea, the beach, the boats (all behind the camera), the yard below.
0.0s–2.0s: sharp on the terrace as a whole; Antony runs toward the camera; focus follows him as he comes. 2.0s–10.0s: sharp on Antony's face and hands at the parapet, about 1.6 m from the camera; Clara and Jaison soft behind. Action as in the block above. 6.5s–8.0s: behind him, soft, Jaison gets up and takes two steps toward the parapet; 8.0s–9.5s: Clara gets up and follows. No two of them move at the same moment.

EYELINES
0.0s–2.0s: Antony looks ahead at the parapet and the sea beyond it, past the camera, level. 2.6s–5.5s: his eyes sweep the sea behind the camera from his left to his right, gaze just above the camera's height and about 20 to 40 degrees to each side of the lens, level with the horizon; never into the lens. 5.5s–10.0s: his eyes lock on the boat about 30 degrees to the left of the lens (his right), level with the horizon, along his pointing right arm. Clara and Jaison: 0.0s–6.5s look at Antony's back; from 6.5s they look past him toward the sea beyond the camera.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
Antony's breath visible at 2.6s (a big in-breath on arrival) and at 5.5s (a short gasp on finding the boat); a very slight drift in the frame at 3.0s and 8.5s; a small focus breath as he arrives at the wall at 2.0s, settling by 2.4s; the sun brightens his face a fraction at 6.0s as a thin cloud clears; the two-beat stillness before he finds the boat.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
A boy of about 30 kg running at about 3 m/s takes about 2 s across the 6 m terrace; his chest stops against the parapet and his weight goes into both hands; rising on his toes lifts his eyes about 6 cm. A sea breeze of about 12 km/h from the camera side lifts his hair and the collar of his loose shirt and flutters the cloths on the line behind him. Dust puffs off the cement where his feet land. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A real 10-year-old in the full grip of excitement about his father coming home: quick, whole-body, unselfconscious. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin with pores, sweat at the hairline, five fingers on each hand flat on the parapet. No posing; the other two children behave like real children, curious, not choreographed.

LIGHTING
Morning sun about 5600K from the sea side behind the camera and a little to the left, lighting Antony's face and chest frontally and softly; the cement floor bounces warm light about 4500K up under his chin; the terrace behind him bright, the clothesline cloths glowing; a pale blue clear sky. His face evenly lit, squinting a little into the bright sea.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design, no riser.
No spoken words and no voices; nobody speaks; every mouth only breathes.
0.0s–2.0s: bare feet slapping on cement, quick breathing; 2.0s the soft thump of his hands and chest on the parapet; 2.6s–10.0s: the breeze, surf and gulls from the beach far below, a distant shout from the shore (wordless), the creak of oars faintly; 6.5s–9.5s small bare-foot steps of the other two children behind him.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the whitewash in the sun, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: whitewash, grey cement, faded sky-blue shirt, sea-light gold.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of shot: a pointing person looking somewhere else (L162) — his eyes run along his arm; the wrong arm (L160) — he points with his RIGHT arm, which is on the LEFT of the frame as he faces the camera; the boat drawn in the frame when the sea is behind the camera; a hand coming near the lens (L150); the parapet turning into a railing or balustrade; adult faces on the children; modern clothes. Exactly three children, each once; nobody else appears. The notebook stays on the floor where it lies. No lettering. No cut, no zoom, no camera move.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One continuous 10-second locked shot from just beyond the parapet. Exactly three children: Antony at the parapet, Clara and Jaison behind him. Exactly one parapet, one notebook, one clothesline with two cloths. Antony points with his right arm, past the left side of the camera, and looks along it. Nobody speaks. Runs the full 10 seconds.
```
