## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Late afternoon, 2026, the beach front of a fishing village on the Alappad coast in Kerala, a community meeting under a tarpaulin. The families have been told the sea is coming; papers are being signed without being fully explained. An old fisherman who cannot read signs because the man in front of him is someone he trusts. One continuous medium close-up of the old fisherman alone at the signing table, 12 seconds. He speaks one short line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (the coastal meeting, the same in every shot of this panel and of SB112)
Place: a blue plastic tarpaulin stretched on bamboo poles over packed sand at the top of the beach; rows of red and white plastic chairs, some empty; one folding table with a white cloth near the front; behind the fisherman, soft and far, the bright beach, two wooden fishing vallams drawn up on the sand with their blue and white painted hulls, nets heaped beside them, a line of coconut palms at frame right and the grey-green sea at frame left; a small white chapel gable with a cross far off at the right edge, very soft.
Light: late afternoon sun low over the sea at frame left, 4800K, the tarpaulin overhead turning the light under it a soft cool blue-white at about 6000K.
People in frame: only the old fisherman.
The old fisherman: about 62, lean and wiry, very dark sun-burnt skin with deep creases at the eyes and across the forehead, a white stubble beard of a few days, short white hair cut close, a thick grey moustache; clouded, kind eyes; a brown cloth scapular cord round his neck disappearing under the shirt; a faded maroon cotton half-sleeve shirt, two buttons open, over a white vest; a checked blue-and-white lungi; thick cracked hands with short nails; no watch, no ring.
Off screen (never seen): Jaison stands across the table, just left of the camera, holding the sheet steady.
NOW: the old fisherman stands bent over the table with his weight on his left hand flat beside the register; a cheap blue ballpoint pen in his right hand above the open page.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. The old fisherman is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one man in frame, bent over the signing table, who signs slowly, straightens and speaks. Nobody else is in frame; the beach behind him is empty of people.

THE REGISTER AND THE SIGNATURE
One long ruled register, 40 x 30 cm open, its cloth spine taped, lying on the white table cloth at the bottom of the frame; on the open page, ruled columns filled with handwritten names and signatures in blue and black ink, all too small and soft to read; the row in front of him empty. One cheap transparent blue ballpoint pen, 14 cm. He writes with his RIGHT hand (on frame left, since he faces the camera), gripping the pen low and hard in his whole fist as people do who write rarely, the tip pressing deep, his signature a slow, careful scrawl of about 6 cm. His LEFT hand (on frame right) lies flat on the table beside the register. Nothing is read aloud; the page is never shown readable.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera stands 1.6 m in front of the fisherman across the table, at his standing eye height about 1.55 m, on a 75mm lens, directly beside where Jaison stands, so the fisherman's eyeline passes just to the left of the lens. Medium close-up: from just above his head to the middle of his chest, the table edge and his writing hand at the bottom of the frame while he bends; his face filling about 40% of the frame height, a little right of centre; the beach, vallams and sea soft behind him. Sharp: his eyes and face all through; the beach soft. The camera is locked; it only breathes.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): he stands bent over the register, the pen held above the empty row; his eyes on the page; he hesitates, the pen tip hovering 1 cm above the paper.
3.0s to 7.0s: he signs, slowly, the pen pressing hard, his lips pressed together with the effort; he finishes with a short stroke and lifts the pen.
7.0s to 7.6s: he straightens up slowly, the pen still in his fist; he raises his eyes to Jaison, just left of the lens; his head comes to rest and stays still.
7.6s to 9.8s: he speaks his one Malayalam line, softly, plainly, head steady.
9.8s to 12.0s (hold): he keeps looking at Jaison; he puts the pen down on the register without looking at it; one breath out; his lips close and stay closed. The shot ends on his face.

THE TRUSTING FACE (most important)
His trust is shown only by small, physical things: the pen hovers before it touches the paper, then goes down; the lips press tight while he writes; when he straightens, his face opens: the eyebrows lift a little, the eyes soften and hold Jaison's without doubt; the faintest nod of 2 degrees at the end of the line. No smile of joy, no handshake, no hand on the heart, no tears, no glance at the camera. He does not read the page.

EYELINES
0.0s to 7.0s: the fisherman looks down at the empty row on the register at the bottom of the frame, head tilted down about 35 degrees, eyes following the pen tip. 7.0s to 12.0s: his gaze rises to Jaison just left of the lens, level with his own eyes, head turned about 10 degrees to frame left, and holds through the line and the end hold; he does not look down as he puts the pen down. He never looks into the lens and never toward the sea.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
0.0s to 12.0s: a very slight drift of a few millimetres in the frame as the camera breathes; 1.0s his breath held; 3.2s a small focus breath as his head dips 5 cm toward the page and returns at 7.2s; 5.0s the tarpaulin flaps once overhead and the light under it flickers a hair; 7.4s his breath in as he straightens; 10.2s his breath out.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The pen presses hard enough to dent the paper and drag it 3 mm; the table, a light folding table, wobbles about 2 mm under his weight on his left hand. A steady sea breeze of about 15 km/h lifts the loose collar of his shirt and the corners of the tablecloth and makes the tarpaulin ripple and slap softly; the palms behind sway slowly. Small waves break on the shore behind him every 8 to 10 seconds. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Simple, unguarded, a working man doing something he finds difficult and doing it because of whom he trusts. Real unretouched skin with deep creases, stubble and pores, five fingers on each hand with cracked knuckles, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no performance of poverty.

LIGHTING
Late afternoon sun at 4800K low over the sea at frame left, reaching under the edge of the tarpaulin as a warm side light on the left of his face (frame right); the tarpaulin overhead gives a soft cool 6000K top light, and a soft, even frontal fill keeps his face readable; the beach behind bright but held, not burnt white. Never flat, never glossy.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh. Only the sounds of the meeting place.
0.0s to 12.0s: small waves breaking on the shore; the sea breeze; the tarpaulin rippling and slapping softly; a low murmur of people under the tarpaulin, no words understood; plastic chairs scraping on the sand; 3.0s to 7.0s the pen scratching on paper; 9.8s to 12.0s the pen laid down with a light tap, waves and breeze.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. The old fisherman speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a broad Alappad coastal Latin Catholic fisherman's Malayalam accent; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
7.6s to 9.8s — The old fisherman: [softly] "നിങ്ങളുള്ളതുകൊണ്ടാ ഒപ്പിടുന്നത്."
Delivery: about 2.2 seconds, twelve syllables at a slow, plain pace, soft volume, a slight rasp in the voice, pitch level and falling on the last word, the stress on "നിങ്ങളുള്ളതുകൊണ്ടാ"; no break inside the line.

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the bright beach behind, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Faded maroon, sand and grey-green sea palette under a blue-white tarpaulin light.

CONSTRAINTS
The old fisherman alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no hand, arm, sheet or head of Jaison or anyone else at the frame edges; no hand near the camera. The beach behind is empty of people. No readable writing on the register; no logo, no company name, no banner, no party flag anywhere. Exactly two vallams and one far chapel gable behind him. No reading glasses. No stage, no banner, no posters in frame. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No subtitles anywhere.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: the old fisherman, once. Exactly one register, one blue ballpoint pen (signature written once at 3.0s to 7.0s, pen laid down once at 9.8s). One tarpaulin overhead, two vallams on the sand, one line of palms, the sea at frame left. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 7.6s to 9.8s, head steady, eyes just left of the lens. Locked 75mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let it sound like a simple statement of faith in a person, said almost kindly, with nothing in it of a question; it should hurt later, not now.
```

3. Jaison, MCU: "അതൊക്കെ നോക്കിയിട്ടാ ഞാൻ പറയുന്നത്."
4. The old fisherman, MCU: "പുതിയ സ്ഥലത്ത് വള്ളം എവിടെയാ ഇടുന്നത്?"
5. Jaison, MCU, a hand on his chest in the pause: "അതും നോക്കിയിട്ടുണ്ട്. നിങ്ങളെ വഴിയാധാരമാക്കാൻ ഞാൻ സമ്മതിക്കുമോ?"
6. Resident's wife, MCU, eyes toward Jaison: "ഈ പേപ്പറിന്റെ കോപ്പി വേണം."
7. Jaison, MCU, pushing the register forward in the pause: "എല്ലാവർക്കും കിട്ടും. ആദ്യം പേരും ഒപ്പും കൊടുക്കൂ."
8. Master above (he signs, then speaks): "നിങ്ങളുള്ളതുകൊണ്ടാ ഒപ്പിടുന്നത്."
