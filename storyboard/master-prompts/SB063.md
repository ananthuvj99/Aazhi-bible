```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Late afternoon, 1998, a remand cell in an old jail on the Kerala coast. Antony, taken from his wedding two days ago, has just been locked in; through the one high barred window he finds the sea he has fished all his life. One continuous static medium shot, 12 seconds. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (the first cell, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: a narrow old jail cell about 2.5 m wide and 3.5 m deep: thick walls of rough lime plaster gone grey-yellow, stained dark with damp in long streaks below the window, scratched marks low on the wall; a grey cement floor, cracked; one small window set high in the end wall, its sill 2.0 m above the floor, 60 cm wide and 50 cm high, four vertical iron bars 2 cm thick with flaking black paint, set deep in the 45 cm thick wall; a folded grey cotton blanket and a rolled straw mat against the side wall on the floor; one dented aluminium plate and one aluminium tumbler on the floor beside the mat. Through the window: a strip of pale sky and, in its lower third, a thin band of grey-blue sea and the horizon, beyond the top of a high outer wall and two coconut palm crowns.
Light: late-afternoon daylight through the high window only; the rest of the cell in dim grey shade.
People in frame: only Antony.
NOW: Antony stands below the window, close to the end wall, both hands up on two of the bars, on his toes so his eyes reach the sill, his face turned up to the window.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@antony20 (face and hair reference only, 0.0s–12.0s): Antony, 24, lean, the same face as the reference; hair now cut short and rough, a few days of black stubble. His clothes are NOT the striped shirt of the reference: a white half-sleeve cotton prison shirt, coarse and too wide for him, buttoned to the second button, and white cotton shorts to the knee; barefoot. Along his LEFT jaw, from below the ear toward the chin (on the side of his face toward the camera), a healing wound about 5 cm long: a dark red line with a dry crust, the skin around it faintly yellow-purple; no fresh blood. The thin silver chain is gone. 100% matches the reference face.

THE HIGH WINDOW AND THE SEA
One window only, high in the end wall at the upper LEFT of the frame: 60 cm by 50 cm, four black iron bars, the deep sill white with old bird droppings and dust. Through it, small in the frame: pale white-blue sky, two palm crowns at the right of the opening, and a thin straight band of grey-blue sea with the horizon across the lower third of the opening, a few faint glints of sun on it. The sea is small, far and real, never a big painted view. Antony's two hands grip the two middle bars: his RIGHT hand on the bar farther from the camera, his LEFT hand on the bar nearer the camera (he faces the window with his left side toward the camera).

HIS FACE AT THE BARS (most important)
Not knowing yet what has been done to him; only the sea is familiar. 0.0s–3.0s: he holds still on his toes, his face lifted into the window light, eyes on the band of sea; his lips are slightly parted; his chest rises and falls fast from the effort of standing on his toes. 3.0s–6.0s: his fingers tighten on the bars at 3.5s, the knuckles paling; his eyes narrow against the light; his mouth closes; a slow swallow at 4.8s; his eyes follow something on the horizon a few degrees to the right (a far fishing boat, too small to see) and come back. 6.0s–9.0s: his calves tire; at 6.8s his heels come down, his face drops 10 cm below the sill, so now he sees only the sky; he does not let go of the bars; his forehead comes forward and rests against the cool wall below the sill at 7.8s, his face turned three-quarter toward the camera, eyes open. 9.0s–12.0s: hold: still, forehead to the wall, hands on the bars above him, eyes open, the breath slowing; a blink at 10.5s. No tears, no shouting, no shaking the bars, no sliding down the wall, no dramatic collapse.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion, 21:9 cinematic widescreen frame. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–12.0s: the camera stands inside the cell near the side wall, 2.4 m from Antony, 1.3 m above the floor, looking slightly up toward him and the window, locked, no move, no zoom. A 40mm lens. Static medium shot: Antony from the knees up at frame right of centre, seen three-quarter from the front as he faces the window at the upper left, the left side of his face and the wound toward the camera; the window high at the upper left of the frame with the sea strip visible through it; the damp-streaked end wall between them; the folded blanket and plate on the floor at the lower left edge. Not in frame: the cell door, any other person. Sharp: Antony's face, his hands on the bars and the window bars; the sea strip slightly soft and bright.
Action: as in HIS FACE AT THE BARS.

EYELINES
Antony, 0.0s–4.8s: up through the window at the band of sea, at the upper left of the frame, head tilted back about 25 degrees and turned to frame left, gaze on the horizon. 4.8s–5.4s: his eyes move a few degrees to the right along the horizon and back to the same point. 5.4s–6.8s: on the sea again. 6.8s–7.8s: as his heels come down, his eyes stay up at the sill, now seeing only sky. 7.8s–12.0s: forehead to the wall, eyes open, looking down and toward the camera's side at the wall surface 10 cm from his face, unfocused. Never into the lens.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A very slight drift in the frame, never a move. 0.0s–6.8s his fast breathing on his toes, the shirt moving at the shoulders; 3.5s the knuckles paling; 4.8s the swallow; 6.8s a small focus breath as his head drops 10 cm; 8.0s a cloud thins outside and the window light on his face lifts a quarter stop for two seconds; 10.5s the blink; 11.0s dust drifts slowly through the window beam.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
Standing on the toes of both feet to bring his eyes up 10 cm, a 60 kg man can hold for a few seconds; his calves tremble very slightly from 5.5s and give at 6.8s. The bars are set in stone and do not move; his grip whitens his knuckles and pulls his sleeves up 2 cm on the forearms. The coarse shirt hangs loose; a light sea breeze through the window at about 2 m/s stirs the hair on his forehead by 1 cm and lifts the shirt collar slightly. The palm crowns outside sway slowly in the same wind. Dust floats in the beam of light, moving with the draught. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A man in his first hours behind bars, numb, holding on to the one thing he knows. Small: the effort of the toes, the tightening grip, the swallow, the forehead against the wall. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin, stubble, a real healing wound, five fingers on each hand, real weight. No posing, no acting to the camera, no weeping.

LIGHTING
Late-afternoon daylight about 5000K coming through the high window in a soft beam, lighting his upturned face from the upper left, the wound and the stubble clearly readable; a little cooler skylight about 6500K from the opening. The rest of the cell in dim grey shade with soft bounce from the plaster, the corners dark but holding detail. The window bright but holding the sea band and the palm crowns. No artificial light. Gentle contrast.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, and no cinematic sound design: no riser, no whoosh, no swell, no rumble, no transition sound. No spoken words and no voices; nobody speaks; every mouth stays closed except for breath. 0.0s–12.0s: far, through the window, the steady wash of waves on a shore and the sea wind; crows; somewhere far inside the jail a steel door clangs once at 2.0s and keys jingle; Antony's quick breathing on his toes; his bare heels touching the cement at 6.8s; from 7.8s only the sea and his breath, the sound of the waves steady to the last frame.

STYLE
Photoreal 1998 Kerala period feature film, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation at the window, visible skin texture and pores, stubble, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: grey-yellow plaster, white prison cotton, a thin band of grey-blue sea; the warm golden coast is gone.

CONSTRAINTS
Exactly one window, high at the upper left; the sea is a thin far band through it, never a big view filling the window, never a beach close by. Antony alone in frame; no guard, no other prisoner, no hand at the frame edges; nobody enters. The wound is along his LEFT jaw only (the side toward the camera), the same size for the whole shot; no fresh blood, no bandage. No striped shirt, no chain, no wedding clothes. No graffiti, no writing, no lettering on the walls. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Every frame looks like the film itself, with nothing from the making of it. Nobody speaks.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person: Antony, once. Exactly one high barred window with four bars, one band of sea through it, one folded blanket, one rolled mat, one aluminium plate and one tumbler on the floor. His two hands on the two middle bars for all 12 seconds. Heels down at 6.8s, forehead to the wall from 7.8s to the end. Locked 40mm medium shot. Nobody speaks. Runs the full 12 seconds.
```
