```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Morning, 2026, the open beach at Alappad on the Kollam coast, Kerala. Muthu, an ageing fisherman, sits on the side of his own wooden boat drawn up on the sand and wraps food in a banana leaf for a friend who is leaving. His friend has thanked him for a meal given long ago; Muthu looks up from the parcel and answers with one gentle question. One continuous medium close-up of Muthu alone, 12 seconds. Muthu speaks one line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

MEMORY (Muthu's boat on the beach, the same in every shot of this panel)
Place: wide pale-gold sand, dry and loose on top, damp and darker toward the water; Muthu's wooden fishing vallam, about 7 m long and 1.4 m wide, drawn up on the sand on two round wooden rollers, its bow toward the sea; the hull sheathed in dark-blue painted fibreglass, scuffed to grey at the edges, with one white band below the gunwale and a faded red-oxide waterline; the gunwale a 10 cm wide strip of dark, oil-stained wood; inside the boat a heaped brown nylon net with small white floats and a coil of blue rope; at the stern, out of this frame, a grey outboard engine tilted up out of the water. No name, number or lettering anywhere on the boat. Beyond the boat, soft and far: the low surf line, the flat blue-grey Arabian Sea and a hazy horizon.
Light: morning, about 9 a.m.; the sun about 40 degrees up, behind the camera and to its left, a clear 5600K light; warm bounce from the sand below; the sea glare soft behind him.
People in frame: only Muthu.
Muthu: about 60, a fisherman, short and wiry with strong forearms; deeply sun-darkened brown skin, weathered and lined across the forehead and at the eyes, real pores and small sun spots on the cheeks; short black hair now mostly grey, thinning at the crown; a thick moustache gone grey-black, clean-shaven chin with a day of white stubble; kind, narrow, creased eyes; a faded sky-blue half-sleeve cotton shirt, open, over a white sleeveless vest; a blue-and-white checked lungi folded up to the knee; barefoot, sand on his feet. No chain, no ring, no watch.
Off screen (never seen): Antony as Rayan sits on an upturned wooden fish box on the sand 1.2 m in front of Muthu, just to the right of the camera, at the same height.
NOW: Muthu sits sideways on the gunwale at the middle of the boat, his feet on the sand, his knees together; on his lap a square of washed banana leaf with a mound of rice, a spoonful of red fish curry and one piece of fried fish on it; his two hands are folding the leaf over the food; his eyes on the parcel.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. Muthu, his boat and the parcel are built only from the words in MEMORY and THE PARCEL: the one ageing fisherman in frame, seated on the side of his boat, wrapping food, who speaks. Nobody else is in frame.

THE PARCEL
A real Kerala food parcel for a journey: one wide piece of banana leaf, wilted over a flame so it folds without cracking, glossy green on top, paler underneath, about 45 x 35 cm before folding. On it: about two handfuls of white boiled rice, a spoonful of thick red fish curry soaking into its edge, and one piece of fried fish, about 10 cm. Folded twice, edges tucked under, it becomes a flat block about 22 x 15 x 6 cm, then tied round once each way with one thin strip of dried pale-brown banana fibre, finished with a single bow-less knot. It weighs about 600 g. It stays on his lap at the bottom edge of the frame; it is never lifted to his face. No paper, no newspaper, no foil, no plastic, no box.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut, no slow motion. 21:9 cinematic widescreen. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
The camera stands on the sand beside Antony's fish box, at Muthu's seated eye height, about 1.0 m above the sand, 1.3 m from him, on a 65mm lens, so Muthu's gaze falls just right of the lens. Medium close-up: from the top of his head to his mid-chest, his face about 40% of the frame height, a little left of centre; the top of the folded parcel and his fingertips just touch the bottom edge of the frame at the start and the end; behind him, soft, the inside of the boat with the heaped net and white floats, the far gunwale, and above it a band of blue-grey sea and the hazy horizon at about the height of his ears. Sharp: his eyes and face; the net soft; the sea very soft. The camera is locked; it does not move, zoom or follow.
0.0s to 3.0s (hold): Muthu looks down at the parcel; his hands fold the last flap of banana leaf over the rice and press it flat, the fingertips just visible at the bottom edge; a breath.
3.0s to 3.6s: his hands stop and rest on the parcel; he lifts his head and looks at Antony, just right of the lens; the corner of his mouth lifts slightly.
3.6s to 6.4s: he speaks his one Malayalam line, gently, as a plain question, his head steady, his hands still on the parcel.
6.4s to 12.0s (hold): he keeps his eyes on Antony for two seconds with the small smile, then at 8.5s looks down at the parcel; his hands draw the strip of banana fibre round it and pull the knot tight at 10.5s; he pats the parcel once at 11.3s. The shot ends on his face turned down to the parcel, the smile still there.

HIS SMILE (most important)
Only small, physical things: the smile is on one side only, the left corner of his mouth (frame right) lifting about 3 mm, his moustache moving with it; the creases at the outer corners of his eyes deepen; his eyebrows lift a little on the last word of the question and then settle. He blinks once at 3.4s before he speaks and once at 7.2s. His chin dips very slightly after the question, as if the answer is obvious. No wide grin, no laugh, no wink, no head shake, no hand on the heart, no tears, no glance at the lens.

EYELINES
0.0s to 3.0s: Muthu looks down at the parcel on his lap at the bottom edge of the frame, head tilted down about 30 degrees. 3.0s to 8.5s: he looks at Antony, seated just right of the lens at the same height, head turned about 15 degrees to frame right, gaze level. 8.5s to 12.0s: back down at the parcel and his knot, head tilted down about 30 degrees. He never looks into the lens and never at the sea.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A slight drift of a few millimetres all through, as from a breath of wind on the sand; 1.5s a small exhale through his nose as he presses the leaf flat; 3.0s a small focus breath as his face lifts toward the lens plane; 5.0s a thin cloud edge softens the sun for one second, a slight exposure dip; 6.4s a half-second of stillness after the question; 10.5s the knot tightens with a small tug that moves his shoulders; 11.3s the pat.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The parcel, about 600 g, presses into the cloth of the lungi on his lap; the leaf bends only where his fingers fold it. A sea breeze of about 12 km/h from the sea behind him lifts a few grey hairs at his crown and the open front edges of his shirt, and makes the edges of the net in the boat tremble. The boat, about 600 kg on its two rollers, does not move under his weight of about 60 kg; the gunwale creaks once when he leans to tie the knot. The fibre strip tightens and creases the leaf at the corners. Waves break about 40 m behind him, white and low. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A fisherman who does not think his kindness was anything special, gently amused at being thanked for it. Plain, warm, unhurried; his hands are sure from years of the same work. Real unretouched skin, sun spots, deep lines, five fingers on each hand, cracked thumbnails, eyes never on the lens. No posing, no speech-making.

LIGHTING
Clear 5600K morning sun from behind the camera and to its left, high enough to light his whole face evenly, with a warm 4800K bounce from the pale sand filling the shadows under his moustache and chin: soft, even frontal light, with small catchlights in both eyes. The sea behind him brighter, its glare held, never clipped. The white vest bright but holding its weave. Never flat, never glossy, never a golden-hour orange.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no background music, no song, no humming, no riser, no whoosh, no transition sound. The beach only.
0.0s to 12.0s: low waves breaking about 40 m behind him and running up the sand, the sea wind, a crow and a few gulls far off, a distant outboard engine far out at sea; 0.0s to 3.0s the banana leaf creaking softly as it folds; 8.5s to 10.5s the dry fibre rasping as he ties it; 10.5s the gunwale creaks once; 11.3s a soft pat on the leaf.
Spoken language: Malayalam only. Muthu speaks one line in Malayalam, in Malayalam script exactly as written below, with a Kollam coastal Malayalam accent of an older fisherman; dubbing language Malayalam. No English words, no translation, no subtitles.
3.6s to 6.4s — Muthu: [softly] "വിശക്കുന്ന ആളെ അറിയേണ്ട കാര്യമുണ്ടോ?"
Delivery: about 2.8 seconds, about fifteen syllables, unhurried, low-medium volume, warm; a tiny pause after "ആളെ"; the stress on "വിശക്കുന്ന"; the pitch rises gently on "കാര്യമുണ്ടോ" as a real question that expects no answer.

STYLE
Photoreal 2026 Kerala feature film in Malayalam, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the sea glare and the white vest, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing, no beauty filter. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: dark blue hull, green leaf, pale sand, blue-grey sea, sun-dark skin.

CONSTRAINTS
Muthu alone in frame from the first frame to the last; no hand, arm or shoulder of Antony or anyone else in frame, nobody walking on the beach behind him. The parcel stays on his lap at the bottom edge; it never rises to his face, never covers his mouth. His hands are still while he speaks. No paper, no plastic bag, no steel tiffin box, no newspaper. No name, number, logo or lettering on the boat, the engine or anywhere. No modern fishing rod. No religious items on him. No camera move, no zoom, no cut. Only Malayalam is spoken; no English words. No subtitles.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: Muthu, once. Exactly one boat, one heaped net, one banana-leaf parcel, one strip of banana fibre, one knot. He sits on the gunwale all through; the boat stays still on its rollers. One Malayalam line, spoken once, from 3.6s to 6.4s, head steady, eyes just right of the lens. Locked 65mm medium close-up. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: Let the question come from a man who never counted what he gave, light and certain, so the warmth sits in his eyes and not in the words.
```
