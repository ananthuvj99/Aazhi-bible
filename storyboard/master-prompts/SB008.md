## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Night, about 1985, the kitchen of a fisherman's house on the Alappad coast, Kerala, at supper. The fish the boy carried home that morning is now fried, on his plate; his father's hand sneaks in and pinches a piece off it, and the boy pulls his plate away. Emotion: playful. The shot in one sentence: a locked close insert that opens on the boy's two hands in exactly the same place as the morning's fish — now holding a steel plate with the same fish fried — and ends with the father's stolen bite. Nobody speaks.

MEMORY (supper at home; the same words in every supper shot, SB008–SB009)
Place: the kitchen exactly as in @kitchenclean, seen only as a soft background: the low wood-fire stove at the back with an orange glow under a pot, the soot-black wall above it, a steel pot and a clay pot on the stove; the family sits on a woven palm-leaf mat on the red-oxide floor.
Light: night outside; inside, one kerosene hurricane lantern standing on the floor to the right of the frame, and the stove's glow at the back.
People in frame (hands only): the boy Antony, about 10: two small, sun-browned hands, short bitten nails, a scrape on the left knuckle, and behind them, soft, the front of his faded sky-blue half-sleeve shirt with one button missing. The father, about 40: one big, sun-darkened, rope-scarred right hand with thick fingers and cracked knuckles, entering from the left edge of the frame.
NOW: the boy, sitting cross-legged on the mat, holds his round steel plate up in both hands at chest height, in the middle of the frame, the fried fish lying on it head left, tail right.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@kitchenclean (background only, 0.0s–10.0s): the kitchen behind the hands, soft: the low wood-fire stove with its glow, the soot-black wall, the pots, the red-oxide floor and the edge of the palm-leaf mat, exactly as in the reference, out of focus. Nobody else is seen. 100% matches the reference.

THE FRIED FISH
The same Indian mackerel as the morning, about 24 cm now, fried whole the Kerala way: two diagonal cuts on its side, rubbed with red chilli and turmeric, fried crisp in coconut oil to a deep red-brown with darker crisp edges, a sheen of oil, a few fried curry leaves stuck to it; its forked tail and round eye still clear. It lies on one round stainless-steel plate about 28 cm across with a low rim and small dents, beside a mound of parboiled red rice about the size of a fist and a little fish curry at the rim. Head toward the left of the frame, tail toward the right, exactly where the raw fish lay in his palms in the shot before. It is the only fish and the only plate in frame.

THE STOLEN BITE (most important)
Small, real, observable movements, one hand moving at a time. 0.0s–2.5s: hold: exactly the match-cut frame: the boy's two hands holding the plate level at the rim, left and right, in the middle of the frame; steam rising thinly from the rice; the lantern light flickering on the oil of the fish. 2.5s–3.5s: the father's big right hand comes in slowly from the left edge of the frame, fingers together, low over the plate. 3.5s–4.5s: his thumb and two fingers pinch a piece of crisp flesh, about 4 cm, from the fish's back behind the head, and lift it; a curl of steam comes off the white flesh. 4.5s–5.0s: the hand leaves the frame at the left with the piece. 5.0s–6.5s: the boy's hands jerk the plate toward his chest and a few centimetres to the right, away from the left edge; a grain of rice falls off the rim. 6.5s–7.5s: the boy's left hand (on the right of the frame) curls protectively over the rim on the fish's side, fingers spread. 7.5s–10.0s: hold: the plate held close and tilted a little toward the camera, the gap in the fish's back clear, the father's hand gone. Avoid: the whole fish lifted, a fork or spoon, the plate flipping, extra hands, any face in frame.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 10 seconds, real time, no cut. The first frame matches the last frame of the shot before (match cut). 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 10 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–10.0s: the camera stands about 0.7 m in front of the boy, a little above his hands (about 0.9 m above the floor, he sits cross-legged, looking down about 20 degrees), with the lantern at the right of the frame; lens about 85mm; an insert framed exactly like the fish in his palms in the shot before: the plate and his two hands fill the middle third of the frame, his shirt front soft behind, the stove glow soft at the back. Sharp: the hands, the plate and the fish; everything behind soft. Locked and still for the whole shot. Not in frame: any face, the mother, the rest of the room. Action as in THE STOLEN BITE above.

EYELINES
No eyes are in frame. The boy's head is above the top of the frame, looking down at his fish the whole time; at 5.0s his shirt collar turns slightly to the left as he looks toward his father off frame at the left. The father sits off frame at the left, looking at the boy's plate as his hand comes in. No face appears.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A very slight drift in the frame at 1.5s and 8.5s; the lantern flame flickers, so the light on the oil and the steel moves all through; a small focus breath as the father's hand enters at 2.5s, settling on the fish by 3.0s; thin steam rising from the rice; the boy's breathing moves the plate by a few millimetres; a short stillness at 7.5s before the hold.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
The plate with rice and fish weighs about 600 g; the boy's hands hold it at the rim, so it tilts a few degrees when he jerks it at 5.0s and a grain of rice rolls off. The fried skin cracks when pinched; a flake of crisp red skin falls back onto the plate. Steam rises from the hot rice and the broken flesh at about 2 cm per second and drifts right in the lantern's warm air. The lantern flame stirs with the night air from the open door. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
Two real pairs of hands playing a family game: the father's sly and unhurried, the boy's quick and protective. Real skin with pores, lines and scrapes, five fingers on every hand, a little oil on the father's fingertips. No posing, no hand modelling.

LIGHTING
One kerosene hurricane lantern about 2000K on the floor to the right of the frame, warm and flickering, lighting the plate, the fish and the hands from the right; the stove's ember glow about 1800K at the back, soft; deep warm shadow at the left where the father's hand comes from; the steel plate catching the lantern in one soft highlight. No electric light, no blue light, no daylight.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design, no impact sound on the match cut.
No spoken words and no voices; nobody speaks; only breathing and one small huff from the boy at 5.0s.
0.0s–10.0s: the hiss of the lantern, the crackle of the wood fire in the stove, a pot lid ticking, crickets and distant surf through the open door; 3.5s–4.5s the crisp crack of the fried skin; 5.0s the scrape of the steel plate rim and the tiny tick of a rice grain on the floor.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation around the lantern and on the steel, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: lantern amber, red-brown fried fish, steel, deep warm shadow.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of insert: macro parts with no logic (L146); a hand near the lens (L150); a second plate or a second fish appearing from a prop sheet (L51); the fish lying the other way from the shot before (it must be head left, tail right, as at the end of SB007); faces drifting in; spoons, forks, a table, chairs, electric bulbs, plastic. No Hindu items: this is a Latin Catholic home. Exactly one plate, one fish. No lettering. No cut, no zoom, no camera move.

POSITIVE LOCKS
One continuous 10-second locked insert. Exactly two small hands (the boy's) holding exactly one round steel plate with exactly one fried mackerel, head left, tail right. Exactly one big hand (the father's right), only from 2.5s to 5.0s, taking one piece. Exactly one lantern, at the right. Nobody speaks. Runs the full 10 seconds.
```
