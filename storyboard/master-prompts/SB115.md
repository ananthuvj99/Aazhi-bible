The officer, holding the complaint already received, does not refuse and does not obey: he asks for the order in writing. One continuous medium close-up of the officer alone, 12 seconds. The officer speaks one line in Malayalam; nobody else speaks.

The SP's large wooden desk with a glass top sits between the officer and the camera, below the frame.
Light: late afternoon; warm 4300K daylight through the window at the left; cool 5000K white tube light from the ceiling, steady.
People in frame: only the officer.
The officer: about 42, lean, medium-dark skin, short black hair cut close at the sides, a neat black moustache, clean-shaven chin with a little stubble shadow; Kerala Police khaki uniform shirt, short sleeves, pressed, with plain shoulder straps carrying small metal stars and no readable badge or name; a black leather belt; no cap (his cap is tucked under his left arm); a steel wristwatch on his LEFT wrist.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
No reference images are attached for this shot. The officer is built only from the words in MEMORY: the one man in frame, standing before the desk with the complaint file at his chest, who speaks. Nobody else is in frame.
