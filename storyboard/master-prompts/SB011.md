## Master prompt
```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Late at night, about 1985, the flat cement terrace of a fisherman's house on the Alappad coast, Kerala, under a full moon. A father and his son of about 10 sit side by side on the warm terrace floor with their backs against the low parapet at one end, the moonlit sea beside them; the boy has just admitted he is a little afraid of tomorrow's first trip out to sea. The father looks out at the water, then turns to his son and gives him one quiet Malayalam line, slowly, the most serious thing he has ever told him. Emotion: thoughtful. The shot in one sentence: a locked medium close-up of the father seated against the parapet, turning from the sea to his son beside the lens and speaking. The father speaks one Malayalam line; nobody else speaks.
Spoken language: Malayalam. Every spoken word in this video is Malayalam.

MEMORY (the terrace at night; the same words in every shot of SB011)
Place: a flat roof terrace about 6 m by 4 m: grey cement floor with dark rain stains and fine cracks; a low whitewashed plastered parapet about 85 cm high with a flat top, its whitewash flaking in patches, silver-blue in the moonlight; beyond the parapet, below and far, the crown of a coconut palm in black silhouette and a wide calm sea with a broad silver path of moonlight on it, the horizon a soft line; a coir clothesline across one corner with one white cloth hanging still.
Light: night under a full moon high behind the camera and to its right, out of frame; no lamp on the terrace.
People in frame: the father only, about 40: tall, broad-shouldered, sun-darkened, short black hair greying at the temples, thick black moustache, crow's feet, a faded white cotton vest, a faded blue checked lungi, a thin silver chain with a small cross. He sits on the cement floor with his back against the parapet, knees up, his forearms resting on his knees, his hands loosely together. The boy sits just outside the frame, beside the lens at the right, against the same parapet.
NOW: the father looks out over the parapet's end at the sea at the left of the frame, his face in three-quarter profile, quiet, after the boy's answer.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
None attached. The father, the terrace, the sea and the moonlight are described in full in this prompt; build them only from these words. Exactly one person in frame.

THE PARAPET AND THE SEA
One low parapet wall about 85 cm high and 23 cm thick, brick plastered and whitewashed, the whitewash flaking to grey plaster in hand-sized patches, a flat top about 25 cm wide; the father's back and shoulders lean against its inner face, his head about 15 cm above its top. Behind him, over the parapet's end at the left half of the frame, the sea about 150 m away and below: calm, a long low swell, a silver path of moonlight on the water widening toward the horizon, soft and out of focus. The palm crown at the far left edge, black, its fronds stirring. No boat on the sea, no shore lights, no lighthouse, no moon in frame.

THE FATHER'S LINE (most important)
Small, real, observable movements only. 0.0s–2.5s: hold: he looks out at the sea at the left, still; at 1.0s he breathes in slowly through his nose and his chest lifts; at 1.8s his thumbs rub together once in his loosely joined hands. 2.5s–3.0s: he turns his head from the sea to the boy beside the lens at the right, slowly, and his eyes settle on the boy's face; the corners of his mouth soften but he does not smile. 3.0s–8.6s: the line, head steady, mouth fully visible, unhurried: a short pause in the middle at the full stop, where he lets his breath out and his eyes stay on the boy. 8.6s–12.0s: hold: he keeps looking at his son, a small, private half-smile coming at 9.6s under the moustache, then he looks back out to the sea at 10.8s, slowly. Avoid: a lecturing finger, a hand on the boy's shoulder in frame, a grand speech face, tears, looking into the lens.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, 12 seconds, real time, no cut. 21:9 cinematic. Runs the full 12 seconds.

CAMERA AND ACTION
0.0s–12.0s: the camera sits on the terrace floor in the boy's place, a little in front of him, about 0.9 m above the cement (the height of the seated father's face), about 1.4 m from the father, looking at him along the parapet; lens about 65mm; a medium close-up: his head, shoulders and the top of his vest, his face filling about a third of the frame height, at the right of centre; the parapet's flaking top a soft band behind his shoulder; the moonlit sea soft in the left half of the frame. Sharp: his eyes and mouth; the sea and the palm soft. Locked and still for the whole shot.
0.0s–2.5s: hold; he looks at the sea. 2.5s–3.0s: he turns to the boy. 3.0s–8.6s: he speaks his Malayalam line. 8.6s–12.0s: hold; he looks at the boy, then back at the sea. Nobody else enters the frame; no hand enters the frame.

EYELINES
The father: 0.0s–2.5s out at the sea at the left of the frame, gaze level with the horizon, head turned about 60 degrees left of the lens; 2.5s–10.8s at the boy's face just right of the lens, about 20 degrees right of it and a little below his own eye height (the boy sits lower), steady through the line and the hold; 10.8s–12.0s back out to the sea at the left, level with the horizon. He never looks into the lens.

DIALOGUE RULES
Dialogue rules: whenever a character speaks, frame them in a medium close-up with their face filling at least a third of the frame height. The speaking face is turned toward the camera, no more than 30 degrees from the lens, upright and not lying sideways. Only one person speaks in the shot; everyone else keeps their mouth closed. The speaker's head stays steady during the line; head turns, gestures and camera moves happen only in pauses between lines. The mouth is always fully visible, with nothing in front of the face, and the face is lit with soft, even frontal light. Natural, clearly articulated mouth movements matching the dialogue.
While the line is spoken, the father is the only person in the frame.

CAPTURE IMPERFECTIONS
A slow in-breath at 1.0s and a long out-breath in the pause of the line at about 5.6s; a very slight drift in the frame at 1.5s and 10.0s; a small focus breath as he turns his head at 2.5s, settling on his eyes by 3.0s; the moonlit water behind him glints and shifts softly all through; a swallow at 9.0s; a pause of about 0.6 s at the full stop inside the line.

REAL-WORLD PHYSICS
A night land breeze of about 8 km/h moves across the terrace from the right: the loose hairs above his ear stir every 2–3 s and the white cloth on the clothesline sways slowly at the edge of the soft background. The palm crown at the far left nods slowly in the same breeze. The sea's long swell rolls the moon path into slow, wide ripples about every 6 seconds. His shoulders rise and fall with each breath against the parapet; the cement stays still. Nothing moves by itself; every movement has a cause.

PERFORMANCE
A fisherman who knows the sea and its fear, choosing his words for a child: calm, warm, weighed, a little far away. Every feeling in the eyes and the breath, not in the hands. Eyes never on the lens. Real skin with pores, deep crow's feet, a little grey in the stubble, five fingers on each hand. No posing.

LIGHTING
Full moonlight about 4100K from high behind the camera and to its right, soft and even on his face from the front, so the line is fully readable; a cool silver edge on the right side of his hair and shoulder; the white vest glowing pale blue-grey; the silver moon path on the sea behind him the brightest thing in the frame, held without clipping; the terrace floor and the parapet's inner face in soft blue shadow. A faint warm glow about 2200K from a lamp-lit window far below at the bottom left corner only, tiny and soft. No electric light on the terrace.

AUDIO
Sound effects only — natural on-location sound, the soundtrack carries no music. No music at all: no score, no song, no humming, no cinematic sound design.
Spoken language: Malayalam only — not English, not a translation. The father speaks the exact Malayalam words in quotes below, in everyday Kollam coastal Malayalam pronunciation, and nothing else. No English words. Lip-sync exact; every word on camera; the line is spoken once.
3.0s to 8.6s — the father, about 40, a low, warm, unhurried voice, quiet so it stays between the two of them; the first sentence slow and even with the stress on "ആഴമുണ്ടാവണം", a pause of about 0.6 s at the full stop, then the second sentence lighter, falling gently at the end. About 5.6 seconds.
[thoughtful] "കടലിനെക്കാളും ആഴമുണ്ടാവണം നമ്മുടെ മനസ്സിന്. അപ്പോൾ ഇതൊന്നും ഒന്നുമല്ലാതാവും."
Ambient, steady: the slow wash of surf far below, crickets, the palm fronds rustling in the breeze, a dog barking once far away at 10.5s.

STYLE
Photoreal Kerala period feature film, about 1985, 24fps, fine 35mm grain, gentle halation on the moon path, visible skin texture and real pores, no smoothing. Shot on a Sony Venice 2 full-frame digital cinema camera: very wide dynamic range that holds the brightest light and the deepest shade in one frame; clean, low-noise shadows; soft, gradual highlight roll-off; accurate, natural colour with rich, true skin tones; gentle filmic contrast; fine, organic texture; no over-sharpening, no glossy or video look. Palette: moonlight silver-blue, whitewash grey, faded white vest, warm sun-brown skin.

CONSTRAINTS
Known failures for this kind of shot: the listener appearing behind or beside the speaker (L34) — the boy stays out of frame; English speech when a scene is described in English (L159) — every spoken word is Malayalam; a moon drawn in the frame or in the wrong place (L142) — the moon is behind the camera, never seen; shore lights or boats invented at sea (L78); a hand in front of the mouth; looking into the lens (L54). A Latin Catholic family: his small cross on the silver chain only. Exactly one person in frame. No lettering. No cut, no zoom, no camera move.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Exactly one person in frame: the father, about 40, faded white vest, silver chain with a small cross, seated against the parapet. Exactly one parapet, one palm crown, one clothesline with one white cloth, one silver moon path on the sea, no moon in frame. One line in Malayalam, spoken once, 3.0s to 8.6s; no English words. Locked medium close-up, about 65mm. Runs the full 12 seconds.

INTENT: When the father speaks his Malayalam line, let it be the quiet weight of a lifetime at sea handed to a child gently, steady and loving, his eyes holding the boy's.
```

## Coverage (rest of the panel)
One silent wide first (8 s): the two of them from behind on the terrace, small against the parapet and the moon path, about 32mm, locked. Then one single per line, each 8 s with 2.5 s holds, the speaker alone in an MCU about 65mm, eyes just off the lens toward the other (father toward the right of the lens, boy toward the left), emotion tag [thoughtful] for the whole scene:
1. Father (MCU, as the master): "നാളെ കടലിൽ വരുന്നത് ആലോചിച്ച് ഭയങ്കര സന്തോഷത്തിലാണല്ലേ നീ?"
2. Antony, boy (MCU, chin on his knees, moon from the front): "ആ, സന്തോഷമൊക്കെ ഉണ്ട്, പക്ഷേ..."
3. Father: "പേടിയുണ്ടോ നിനക്ക്?"
4. Antony: "കുറച്ച്. ഉൾക്കടലിൽ എത്തിയാൽ പിന്നെ മൊത്തം തിരയും കാറ്റുമൊക്കെ ആയിരിക്കും, അല്ലേ?"
5. Father: "ആദ്യം പോയപ്പോൾ എനിക്കും ഇങ്ങനെയൊക്കെ പല പേടിയുണ്ടായിരുന്നെടാ."
6. Antony: "അങ്ങനെ ഇപ്പോഴും ഉണ്ടോ അച്ഛാ?"
7. Father: "ഇപ്പോഴും ഉണ്ട്. പക്ഷേ പേടിക്കണ്ട, അതൊക്കെ നമുക്ക് ശരിയാക്കാം."
8. Father — THE MASTER ABOVE.
9. Antony (MCU, he lifts his head from his knees, puzzled): "എന്ത്?"
10. Father (MCU, a small smile; head steady through the line, he looks back at the sea only in the end hold): "ഒന്നുമില്ല. നീ ഇപ്പോ ഉറങ്ങിക്കോ. രാവിലെ നേരത്തേ പോകേണ്ടതാ."
