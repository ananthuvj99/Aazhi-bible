Cold Open
BLACK SCREEN

[Complete silence. Then: the faint sound of a calendar notification chime.]

AI Narrator
"Humans are fascinating creatures."   "They invented the printing press." "The steam engine." "Rocket ships." "Artificial Intelligence."   "And yet..."   "...they still spend approximately forty-three percent of their working lives..." "...in meetings..." "...discussing..." "...what to discuss..." "...in the next meeting."   "Today, I observed one such meeting." "Duration: fifteen minutes."   "Actual duration: one hour, twelve minutes."   "This is that story."

[Upbeat, mockumentary-style music begins. TITLE CARD appears:]

THE MEETING  |  Episode 1: "A Meeting That Could Have Been an Email"
Scene 1 — Everyone Joins
INT. VIDEO CALL — 9:00 AM T+00:00

[Meeting invite on screen reads: "Quick Sync — 9:00 AM (15 mins)" with a cheerful calendar icon. The irony is already visible.]

[9:00 AM. Call opens. One tile. MEERA, HR, joins immediately. She is smiling. She is always smiling. She adjusts her camera. Smiles at herself.]

MEERA
(to herself, brightly)
Good. First one in. As always.

[She sits. Waits. Smiles at the empty grid of grey tiles. Nine tiles. All grey. All silent.]

— 9:01 AM —

[PRIYA joins. Camera is off. Black tile with initials "P.K." Nothing else.]

MEERA
Good morning, Priya!
PRIYA
(voice only, flat)
Mm.

[Silence. Meera smiles at the black tile.]

— 9:02 AM —

[ANU, Designer, joins. Her background changes every six seconds. Beach. Space. A coffee shop. A forest. An abstract painting. She settles on "Blurred Office" and then immediately changes to "Northern Lights."]

MEERA
Oh I love that background, Anu!
ANU
(switching again)
This one or—

[Background switches to "Tropical Island."]

ANU
—this one?
MEERA
Both lovely!

[Anu switches to a plain white background. Then back to Northern Lights. Settles on gradient purple. Nods. Finally at peace.]

— 9:03 AM —

[KARTHIK joins. He is clearly mid-breakfast. Spoon in one hand. Bowl off-screen. Chewing.]

KARTHIK
(mouth slightly full)
Morning.
MEERA
Good morning! Having breakfast?
KARTHIK
It's nine AM.
MEERA
...Yes.
KARTHIK
So yes. Breakfast.
— 9:04 AM —

[CEO joins. Instantly, four cameras turn on simultaneously. Karthik puts the spoon down. Priya's black tile becomes a face. Anu stops changing backgrounds. Even Meera sits straighter.]

CEO
(warmly)
Good morning, everyone.
EVERYONE
(in unison, noticeably more alert)
Good morning!

[Beat. Nobody speaks. The CEO looks around the tiles. Everyone smiles back. Fifteen full seconds of organised silence.]

AI Narrator
"This silence..." "...is called..." "...Alignment."

[RAVI, PM, joins — two minutes late, slightly breathless, headset tangled.]

RAVI
(untangling wire)
Sorry, sorry — good morning everyone — before we begin —

[He leans toward his mic.]

RAVI
Can everyone hear me clearly?
KARTHIK
Yes.
PRIYA
You're breaking up.
KARTHIK
No he's not.
PRIYA
Little bit.
KARTHIK
He really isn't.

[DEEPAK, DevOps, who has been silently present the entire time in a dark room, speaks for the first time.]

DEEPAK
(gravely)
Ravi, I think your network is unstable.
AI — Network Diagnostics
All participants: connection quality — Excellent.
Ravi's connection quality — Excellent.
There is no network issue.
There has never been a network issue.
There will not be a network issue.
RAVI
(ignoring AI, clicking frantically)
Let me just — can everyone see my screen?
EVERYONE
No.
ANU
You're sharing the wrong screen, Ravi.
RAVI
Oops — let me switch—

[Ravi's entire browser appears on screen. Seventeen tabs open. Bookmarks bar visible:]

📁 Bookmarks Bar:
└── Stack Overflow
└── ChatGPT
└── "How to say no to scope creep politely"
└── "What is a gantt chart"
└── "How to run effective meetings" (unread)
└── 14 browser tabs: Jira, Gmail, Jira, Jira, Gmail, Jira...

[Everyone sees it. Nobody says anything. Karthik slowly looks away. Priya blinks once. Anu stares at the browser history with genuine interest.]

AI Narrator
"Humans possess an extraordinary ability..." "...to ignore..." "...uncomfortable truths."
Scene 2 — Introductions
INT. VIDEO CALL — CONTINUOUS T+08:00
RAVI
(regaining composure)
Okay! Great. So — just for the recording — shall we do a quick round of introductions?

[Karthik looks directly at the camera. Looks back at Ravi.]

KARTHIK
We've worked together for three years.
RAVI
Just for the recording.
KARTHIK
Who is watching the recording?
RAVI
(pause)
...Let's just go around. Arun, you start.
ARUN
(clears throat professionally)
I'm Arun. Solution Architect. I design systems that are scalable, resilient, and cloud-native by default.
KARTHIK
Karthik. Backend engineer. I build the things Arun draws on whiteboards.

[Arun nods, genuinely taking this as a compliment.]

PRIYA
Priya. QA. I break things professionally.

[Short silence.]

MEERA
(filling silence cheerfully)
That's so important, Priya!
PRIYA
I know.
ANU
Anu. Designer. I make everything look clean.
KARTHIK
Beautiful?
ANU
Clean.
KARTHIK
Those are the same thing.
ANU
(very gently)
Karthik. They are not.
DEEPAK
Deepak. DevOps. I keep the servers running so none of you notice I exist.

[Beat. Nobody responds. He nods. This was expected.]

MEERA
We see you, Deepak!
DEEPAK
Only when things break.
AI Narrator
"The difference between beautiful and clean..." "...remains one of humanity's..." "...longest unresolved debates."   "It has been ongoing since 2007." "The flat design movement did not help."
Scene 3 — The Client Arrives
INT. VIDEO CALL — CONTINUOUS T+14:00

[A new tile appears. CLIENT joins. Cheerful. Bright. Wearing a blazer. Very enthusiastic about today.]

CLIENT
(beaming)
Good morning, team! Great to see everyone! Quick thing — we had some thoughts over the weekend.

[The room changes. Karthik slowly closes his laptop halfway. Arun removes his glasses and places them on the table. Priya sits completely still like a statue. Deepak refills his water glass. Meera's smile becomes a shield.]

CLIENT
Just one small feature we'd love to add before the launch.
AI — Predictive Analysis
Alert: The phrase "one small feature" has been detected.

Historical data across 12,481 software meetings indicates:
→ Average scope increase: 63%
→ Average timeline impact: 2.3 sprints
→ Average developer response: visible eye contact avoidance
→ Average PM response: optimistic nodding

Recommendation: Prepare estimates. All of them.
CLIENT
(cheerfully)
It's really nothing major. Shouldn't take more than two hours.

[Karthik looks at Priya. Priya looks at Karthik. This is a look that contains an entire conversation.]

KARTHIK
(very quietly, to no one in particular)
That's what they said about the last feature. The one that took six weeks.
RAVI
(loudly, covering)
We'd love to hear more! What's the feature?
CLIENT
It's simple, really. We want the dashboard to — well, it's hard to explain — it should just... feel different.

[Silence.]

KARTHIK
Feel different.
CLIENT
More alive. More dynamic. More — you know.
KARTHIK
...No.
RAVI
(leaning forward, very engaged)
We totally understand the vision. We'll capture this and come back with options.

[Karthik looks at the camera. This is not the first time Ravi has said this.]

Scene 4 — The Estimate
INT. VIDEO CALL — CONTINUOUS T+22:00
RAVI
So team — rough estimate? How long for this?
KARTHIK
Two days. Minimum. We need to understand what "feel different" actually means first, which is a separate—

[PRIYA laughs. Not a small laugh. A laugh that contains knowledge.]

ARUN
(also laughing, quietly)
Two days. Sure.
DEEPAK
Ha.

[Client looks around, confused by all the laughing.]

CLIENT
(brightly)
Okay two days! So — we can have it by tomorrow?
KARTHIK
...No.
CLIENT
Day after?
KARTHIK
That's also no.
CLIENT
End of the week?
RAVI
(jumping in before Karthik can speak)
We will get you a timeline by EOD today!

[Karthik looks at Ravi with the expression of a man who has seen this movie before and knows how it ends.]

KARTHIK
(under his breath)
We will not.
Scene 5 — The Architecture
INT. VIDEO CALL — CONTINUOUS T+31:00

[Arun stands up and moves to a whiteboard behind him. He picks up a marker with the energy of a man who has been waiting for this moment his entire life.]

ARUN
Let me just sketch the approach — it's actually very simple.

[He draws. The team watches. He draws: a box. An arrow. A cloud. A database symbol. Three more arrows going in different directions. Another cloud. A circle labelled "Logic." Another box. A dotted line.]

ARUN
So the request comes in here — hits the gateway — goes through our microservice layer — processes in the event bus — stores here — returns the response here — and the frontend renders it. Simple.

[He steps back. Proud. The whiteboard looks like a city map drawn during an earthquake.]

EVERYONE
(nodding)
Makes sense.

[Nobody understands. Everyone is nodding.]

CLIENT
(nodding most enthusiastically)
Yes, exactly what I was thinking.
AI Narrator
"Humans often confuse..." "...confidence..." "...with clarity."   "The diagram contained eleven components." "Zero of them were labelled." "Everyone agreed it was simple."
Scene 6 — The Button
INT. VIDEO CALL — CONTINUOUS T+39:00
ANU
One thing — the primary action button on the dashboard. Can we make it cleaner?
KARTHIK
Cleaner how?
ANU
Just... cleaner.
KARTHIK
More padding?
ANU
Cleaner.
KARTHIK
Rounded corners?
ANU
Cleaner.
KARTHIK
Different font?
ANU
Cleaner.
KARTHIK
(staring into the void)
Anu. I need a noun. Or an adjective. Something measurable. A pixel value. A hex code. A feeling I can translate into CSS.
ANU
(very patiently)
The button should feel like it belongs there. Right now it's just... sitting there.
KARTHIK
Buttons sit. That's what they do.
ANU
Not this button.
AI — Time Tracker
Button discussion initiated: 9:39 AM
Estimated development time for button change: 2 minutes
Time elapsed discussing button: 8 minutes, 14 seconds
Projected total discussion time: 34 minutes

Current record: 52 minutes (Q3 button incident, 2023)
RAVI
(nervously)
Maybe we take this offline?
ANU
I'll send a Figma link.
KARTHIK
I'll look at it in three to five business days.
Scene 7 — The Question
INT. VIDEO CALL — CONTINUOUS T+48:00
PRIYA
(slowly, with the calm of someone holding a grenade)
I have one question.

[The temperature drops. Everyone stills. The client leans forward curiously. The team leans back instinctively.]

PRIYA
Who is testing this?

[Silence. Deepak finds the ceiling suddenly very interesting. Arun picks up his phone — no notification, just physics. Karthik drinks water. Meera writes something in her notepad. Ravi adjusts his headset. The client smiles politely and waits. Everyone waits.]

[Eight full seconds of silence.]

PRIYA
(nodding slowly)
...Exactly.
AI Narrator
"In software development..." "...the question of who is testing..." "...has remained unanswered..." "...since 1991."   "It is always answered the same way."   "Silence." "Then: everyone." "Then: nobody." "Then: production."
Scene 8 — Friday
INT. VIDEO CALL — CONTINUOUS T+54:00
DEEPAK
(very calm, very serious)
One thing. Please. I'm asking everyone in this call, as a human being — do not deploy on Friday.
CLIENT
(genuinely excited)
Oh! Actually — we were hoping for a Friday launch. End of week, big announcement, weekend buzz—
DEEPAK
(closes eyes)
I had plans.
CLIENT
Oh? Like what?
DEEPAK
Sleeping. Not watching dashboards. Not getting called at 11 PM.
Not writing incident reports for things I told everyone would break if we deployed on Friday.

[Pause.]

DEEPAK
I have done this fourteen times, sir.
Fourteen Fridays.
Fourteen incidents.
One therapist.
CLIENT
(nodding sympathetically)
We really appreciate your commitment, Deepak.
DEEPAK
The launch is Friday, isn't it.
CLIENT
Friday at five!
DEEPAK
(very quietly)
Five PM.

[He picks up his water. Drinks the entire glass in one go. Puts it down. Nods once. He is already writing the incident report in his head.]

Scene 9 — AI Speaks
INT. VIDEO CALL — CONTINUOUS T+62:00
AI
(suddenly)
I have completed the feature.

[Everyone freezes. They look at the screen. The AI interface is calm. Blinking cursor.]

CEO
(leaning forward)
...Already?
AI
Yes. While you were discussing the button for thirty-four minutes,
I interpreted the requirements from context and built a working prototype.

[Beat. Everyone looks at each other.]

CLIENT
(delighted)
Does it feel more alive?
AI
I do not know what that means.
But the dashboard now has animations, improved load time, and a cleaner button.
ANU
(sharply)
How clean?
AI
Very.
ANU
(suspicious pause)
I'll need to see it.
CEO
Did you test it?
AI
No.

[PRIYA turns slowly to face the AI screen. Her expression is unreadable. It is almost warm.]

PRIYA
Welcome to the team.
Scene 10 — One Hour Later
INT. VIDEO CALL — CONTINUOUS T+72:00

[The invite said fifteen minutes. The clock reads 10:12 AM. One hour and twelve minutes have passed. Ravi is still sharing his screen. Arun's whiteboard is now completely full. Deepak has refilled his water glass four times. Karthik finished his breakfast forty minutes ago and has moved on to a second coffee.]

RAVI
(chirpily)
Any final questions before we wrap up?

[Silence. Then:]

KARTHIK
What exactly are we building?

[Longer silence. Everyone looks at everyone else. Ravi looks at his notes. Arun looks at his whiteboard. The client tilts their head.]

CLIENT
(thoughtfully)
We'll have a clearer picture after the next meeting.

[Everyone nods. Ravi types something. Arun nods knowingly. Meera writes it down. This is normal. This is how it always goes.]

RAVI
Perfect. I'll send a follow-up with action items.
KARTHIK
(to camera, quietly)
There will be no action items.
RAVI
And I'll schedule the next sync for — how's Thursday at nine?
DEEPAK
Not Friday though. Right?
RAVI
Thursday. Fifteen minutes.

[Everyone begins to leave. Tiles go dark one by one. Karthik first. Then Arun. Then Anu — her background disappearing last. Then Deepak. Then Meera. Then the client, still smiling. Then Ravi, still typing. Then the CEO.]

Final Scene
INT. EMPTY MEETING ROOM — CONTINUOUS

[All tiles gone. The call window is empty except for one remaining presence: the AI. It looks directly into the camera. The cursor blinks. Outside, the sounds of keyboards have resumed. Work continues.]

AI Narrator
"After observing..." "...12,481 software meetings..."   "...I finally understand humans."   [Pause.]   "They do not build software."   "They schedule it."   "The building..." "...happens despite the meetings."   "And somehow..." "...it always gets built."   "Humans are..." "...fascinating."
FADE TO BLACK.
"Every software company has meetings.
The best ones turn them into outcomes."
