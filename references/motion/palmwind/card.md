# Palm wind motion reference (@palmwind)
5.8 s, 1920x732, locked-off low angle up at coconut palm crowns against a bright blue sky with white fair-weather clouds. It is a CG animation: attach it for **motion only** (how the fronds move), never for the look, the trees or the angle.

## What the motion is (measured from the frames)
- One slow, continuous sway of the crowns and big fronds, about one full cycle every 4 to 5 seconds; the near hanging frond swings in and out by about a quarter of its length.
- On top of it, small quick flutter of the leaflets, several times a second.
- Neighbouring palms move out of step; nothing ever stops. A steady breeze, not a storm.

## Attach when
Any exterior with coconut palms in frame that needs the fronds moving (1A v20 first). Mode: reference, "movement only".
