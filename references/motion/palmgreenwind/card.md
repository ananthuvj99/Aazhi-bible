# Palm in the wind on green screen (@palmgreenwind)
10 s, 640x360. One CG coconut palm swaying in a steady breeze on a plain green screen. Trimmed from Ananthu's download (Free Use clip): the last 2 s, a "subscribe / like / share" end card with lettering, were cut off so no text can reach a generation (L40).

## Motion
The crown sways slowly side to side, fronds rising and falling and flexing, leaflets fluttering; the trunk bends slightly; nothing stops.

## Attach when
Any exterior with coconut palms that must move. MOVEMENT ONLY: never the green background, the single tree, its look or framing. Replaces @palmwind when that one is refused by the platform.
